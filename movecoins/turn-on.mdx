---
title: "Turn Movecoins on or off"
description: "How to turn on Movecoins, what you agree to, what happens to your welcome gift, and how to turn it off."
---

Movecoins is off until you turn it on. Turning it on lets MoveTogether use your daily steps and exercise minutes from Apple Health to award coins.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Movecoins">
    Tap the **Rewards** tab, or tap your coin balance at the top of **Home**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the screen">
    It explains what Movecoins uses and links to the **Movecoins program terms** and the **Privacy policy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Turn on Movecoins">
    Tapping the button is how you agree to the program terms and to MoveTogether using your activity data as the privacy policy describes. Tap **Not now** to skip.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You may see "We'll update how your activity syncs first." That means your Apple Health sync needs a quick update before Movecoins can start. Follow the screen, then turn Movecoins on.

You can also turn it on from **Invite & Earn**: tap **Turn on Movecoins** on the Movecoins card.

## What happens next

- **Coins count from today.** Activity from before the day you turn it on never earns coins.
- **Welcome gift.** The first time you turn Movecoins on, you get 50 Movecoins. You only get it once, even if you turn Movecoins off and on again.

## Turn it off

1. Open Movecoins.
2. Tap **…** at the top right.
3. Tap **Turn off Movecoins**, then **Turn off**.

What happens:

- You keep the coins you already have. They still expire on the normal schedule.
- You stop earning, starting with the day you turn it off.
- MoveTogether stops using your activity for Movecoins.
- You can't cash out while Movecoins is off.

## Turn it back on

Turn it on again the same way. Earning starts from that day. Days while Movecoins was off never earn coins.

<Note>
  Movecoins is US only and on iPhone only for now. See [What are Movecoins?](/movecoins/overview).
</Note>
