## Editing your profile

Go to the **Profile** tab and tap **Edit Profile**. You can update:

- Your display name (shown on leaderboards and in the activity feed)
- Your username (your unique identifier that friends use to find you)
- Your profile photo (tap to upload from your camera roll or take a new photo)
- Your bio

Tap **Save** when you're done.

## Profile photo guidelines

Your profile photo has to follow our [Community Guidelines](/en/account/guidelines-moderation). No inappropriate, offensive, or explicit content. Profile photos are scanned by AI moderation to make sure they comply.

## Your stats and badges

Your Profile tab also shows your achievements, badges (including seasonal event badges), competition history, streak information, and referral link.
