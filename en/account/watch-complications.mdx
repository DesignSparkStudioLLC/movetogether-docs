MoveTogether ships an Apple Watch complication that puts your Move ring progress right on your watch face.

## What it shows

A circular gauge with your current Move ring as a percentage. You'll see how close you are to today's Move calorie goal without opening the app.

The complication reads directly from Apple Health on your Watch, so it updates without needing a network connection.

## Adding the complication

1. Long-press your Apple Watch face.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Swipe to the complications screen.
4. Tap a complication slot, then scroll to find **MoveTogether** in the list.
5. Tap to select. Press the Digital Crown to save.

## Supported complication families

The MoveTogether complication is designed for **accessoryCircular** slots on modern Watch faces (Modular, Infograph, and similar layouts that support the small circular complication style).

## How often it updates

Every 15 minutes in the background. You can also force a refresh by raising your wrist or opening the MoveTogether Watch app.

## Requirements

- Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later
- MoveTogether Watch app installed (it installs automatically with the iPhone app)
- Apple Health permissions granted to MoveTogether

<Note>
  If the complication shows "—" instead of a percentage, open the MoveTogether iPhone app and confirm Apple Health permissions are granted: **iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MoveTogether**.
</Note>
