MoveTogether is built around showing off your activity to friends. The fastest way to do that is the **share card picker**.

## Weekly recap share cards

When your **Weekly Mo Report** is ready, you can share it as one of three card styles:

- **Grade card** — your weekly grade (A through F) with a mood background that matches.
- **Roast card** — Coach Mo's irreverent take on your week, delivered with personality.
- **Rings card** — a clean visual of your ring closures across the week.

Swipe the picker to preview each style, then tap **Share** to send your favorite.

## Backgrounds change with your grade

The card backgrounds are picked based on how you did:

| Grade | Background mood |
|-------|-----------------|
| A | Sunrise coast |
| B | Trail mountain |
| C | Urban dawn |
| D | Morning fog |
| F | Stadium lights |

A great week looks like a great week. A rough week gets its own visual.

## Where to share

Tap **Share** and your phone's standard share sheet opens. From there you can post to Instagram, send via iMessage, drop it in a group chat, or save the image to your camera roll.

## How the cards are generated

Cards are rendered on-device using your real data — your name, your scores, your week. They're saved as PNG images, so they look sharp on any platform you post to.

<Note>
  Cards prefetch in the background as soon as your Weekly Mo Report is ready, so the picker opens instantly with no loading spinner.
</Note>
