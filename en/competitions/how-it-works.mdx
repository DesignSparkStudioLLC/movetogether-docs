## Scoring methods

**Ring Close Count.** Points for closing each activity ring. You earn points every time you close your Move, Exercise, or Steps ring. Rewards consistent daily activity, good for keeping everyone accountable.

**Percentage of Goals.** Points based on goal completion percentage. Instead of raw numbers, this looks at what percentage of your personal goals you've hit. Levels the playing field since everyone's goals are different. A beginner working toward 5,000 steps gets rewarded just as much as an athlete aiming for 15,000.

**Raw Numbers.** Points for every calorie, minute, and step. The straightforward approach. The more you move, the more points you get.

**Step Count.** Points for every step taken. The classic competition. Simple, easy to understand, and every tracker measures it.

**Workout Based.** Points only during specific workout types. Focuses on dedicated exercise sessions rather than all-day activity. Only activity logged during recognized workouts counts.

## Team mode

Competitions can be **Individual** (the default, everyone solo) or **Team**. For a team competition, you pick 2, 3, or 4 teams. Each team's combined score determines the ranking, which encourages collaboration. Participants pick a team when they join.

## Competition duration

You pick how long your competition runs. MoveTogether has preset durations (**Weekend** for Saturday–Sunday, **Weekly** for Monday–Sunday, **Monthly**) or you can set a **Custom** date range. You can also turn on repeating competitions so they recur automatically.

## Competition lifecycle

Every competition goes through three stages:

- **Upcoming.** The competition exists and friends can join, but hasn't started. The creator can still edit settings.
- **Active.** The start date has arrived. Activity is being tracked, leaderboards are live, scores update in real time.
- **Completed.** The end date has passed. Final rankings are locked in, winners are announced, achievements are awarded.

## Repeating competitions

Turn on **Repeat** and the competition automatically creates a new cycle when the current one ends. Good for ongoing weekly challenges with the same group. Each cycle is tracked separately so you can see your performance over time.

## Joining a competition

Three ways: tap an **Invite Link** from a friend, enter a **Competition Code** in the Compete tab, or accept an **In-App Invitation**. You can join a competition after it has started, but only data from when you join onward counts toward your score.

## Leaving or deleting a competition

To leave, open the competition from the Compete tab, tap the more options menu (three dots), and select **Leave Competition**. Leaving removes you from the leaderboard and can't be undone.

Only the creator can delete a competition. Deleting removes it for everyone.

## Fair play across trackers

One of MoveTogether's promises is that everyone competes on a level playing field, regardless of tracker. The app normalizes data across all supported devices so that differences in how trackers calculate metrics don't unfairly favor anyone. All scores are calculated server-side to make sure they're accurate and to prevent manipulation.
