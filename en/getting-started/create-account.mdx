## How to sign up

1. Download MoveTogether from the App Store.
2. Open the app and tap **Get Started**.
3. Choose how you want to create your account:
   - **Sign in with Apple** uses your Apple ID. Fastest option, and Apple can hide your email for privacy.
   - **Sign in with Google** uses your Google account.
4. Review and accept the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines. Tap each one to read the full policy before agreeing.
5. Choose a display name and username. Your display name is what friends see on leaderboards. Your username is how people find and add you.
6. Add a profile photo (optional). Upload from your camera roll or take a new one. You can change this later in your profile settings.

## Choosing a good username

Your username is unique to you and is how friends find you. Keep it short and recognizable so people can search for you easily. Usernames can include letters, numbers, and underscores. You can change your username later, but your friends will need to search for the new one.

## What happens next

After you sign up, the app walks you through a short onboarding flow: connecting your fitness tracker, setting your goals, and customizing your experience. This is the most important setup step. It's how MoveTogether reads your activity data so you can compete.

If you skip any steps during onboarding, you can finish them later in Settings.
