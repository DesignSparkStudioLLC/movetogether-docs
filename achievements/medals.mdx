Achievements in MoveTogether use a four-tier medal system. Each achievement has progressively harder requirements as you climb.

## The tiers

| Tier | Color | What it means |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Bronze** | Brown | Entry tier — most active users will unlock these in the first few weeks. |
| **Silver** | Silver | Steady consistency — typically requires sustained activity over weeks. |
| **Gold** | Gold | Serious commitment — usually months of consistent activity or a meaningful milestone. |
| **Platinum** | Platinum | Rare. Top tier — for the most consistent and competitive users. |

## How to unlock

Achievements unlock automatically when you meet their criteria. You'll get a notification, and they appear on your **Profile** under **Achievements**.

## Categories

Achievements span four categories — competition wins, daily streaks, calorie/step milestones, and social play. See [Achievement categories](/achievements/categories) for the full list.

<Note>
  Achievements are cosmetic — they don't affect your competition scoring or leaderboard rank. They're there to celebrate progress.
</Note>
