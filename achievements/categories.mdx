---
title: "Achievement categories"
description: "Competition, consistency, milestone, and social achievements."
---

MoveTogether achievements fall into four categories.

## Competition

Unlocked by winning or placing well in competitions.

- First win
- Win streaks (3, 5, 10 in a row)
- Total competitions won
- Wins by competition type (weekend, weekly, monthly, custom)

## Consistency

Unlocked by daily activity streaks.

- 7-day streak
- 30-day streak
- 100-day streak
- 365-day streak

A streak day = all three rings closed before midnight.

## Milestone

Unlocked by crossing lifetime activity thresholds.

- Total active calories (10K, 50K, 100K, 500K, 1M)
- Total steps (100K, 1M, 5M, 10M)
- Total exercise minutes (1K, 5K, 10K)

## Social

Unlocked by competing against new people.

- Compete against 5, 25, or 100 unique opponents
- Invite a friend who joins MoveTogether
- Be part of a group competition with 10+ participants

<Note>
  See all your unlocked and in-progress achievements on your **Profile** tab.
</Note>
