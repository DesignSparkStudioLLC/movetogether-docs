---
title: "Weekly Mo Report"
description: "Your personalized weekly recap."
---

Every Monday morning, Coach Mo prepares a recap of your previous week — what you did, how it compared to your average, and what to focus on next.

## When it appears

The Weekly Mo Report card shows up on your **Home** screen Monday through Friday. It's based on the seven days from the previous Sunday backward.

## What's in it

- **Headline stat** — your biggest highlight or callout from the week.
- **Ring totals** — how many days you closed each ring.
- **Competition recap** — finishing position in any competitions that ended.
- **Coach Mo's take** — a short note from Mo with a suggestion for the week ahead.

## Sharing your report

Tap the **Share** button on the report card to open the share card picker — pick a layout you like and post it to your group chat or Instagram story. Mo can also generate a "savage roast" version if you tap the prompt.

<Note>
  Don't see this week's report? Pull down on the Home screen to refresh, or check that your tracker has finished syncing through Sunday.
</Note>
