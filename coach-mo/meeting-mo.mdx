Coach Mo is the AI coach built into MoveTogether. Mo knows your activity history, your current competitions, and your goals — and uses that context to give you advice that actually applies to you.

## What Mo can help with

- **Weekly check-ins.** Mo reviews your week and points out trends.
- **Competition strategy.** Ask "how do I catch [friend] in this weekend's competition?" — Mo will look at your goals vs. theirs and suggest a plan.
- **Goal tuning.** "My Move goal feels too easy this month" — Mo can suggest adjustments based on your actual data.
- **Quick answers** about how MoveTogether works.

## How to talk to Mo

- **Text chat** — tap the **Coach** tab and type.
- **Voice on iPhone** — tap the microphone icon in the Coach tab.
- **Voice on Apple Watch** — see [Voice chat on Apple Watch](/coach-mo/voice-on-watch).

## What Mo can't do

Mo isn't a doctor, dietician, or licensed coach. He's an AI assistant with access to your activity data. For medical advice, talk to a real professional.

## First time opening Coach Mo?

There's a short one-time onboarding the first time you open the Coach tab — Mo asks a few questions to tailor his guidance. This is separate from the app's initial sign-up flow.
