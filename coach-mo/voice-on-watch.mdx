The MoveTogether Apple Watch app supports real-time voice chat with Coach Mo — useful mid-workout, mid-walk, or whenever your phone isn't reachable.

## How to start a voice chat

1. Raise your wrist and open **MoveTogether** on your Apple Watch.
2. Tap **Coach Mo**.
3. Tap the microphone button and start talking.

Mo responds in real time through your Watch speaker (or paired AirPods).

## Requirements

- Apple Watch Series 6 or later
- watchOS 10 or later
- An active cellular or Wi-Fi connection on the Watch (LTE works without your phone nearby)
- Coach Mo voice access (included on **Mover** and **Crusher** plans — see [Plans](/subscriptions/plans))

## Privacy

Voice is processed in real time and is not stored after the conversation ends. Transcripts of what Mo said back to you are saved to your Coach chat history so you can reference them later.

<Note>
  Voice not working on your Watch? Check that the MoveTogether watch app has microphone permission in **Watch app → MoveTogether → Privacy**.
</Note>
