---

Coach Mo uses real AI under the hood, which costs us money per message. To keep the app sustainable, every plan includes a monthly message allowance.

## Your monthly quota

| Plan | Coach Mo messages per month |
|------|------------------------------|
| **Starter** | Limited preview |
| **Mover** | 200 |
| **Crusher** | 200 |

The Weekly Mo Report does **not** count against your quota.

## When does the counter reset?

On the first day of each calendar month, in your local time zone.

## What happens when I hit the limit?

You'll see a friendly notice from Mo letting you know you've used your monthly messages. You can:
- Wait until the next month for the counter to reset
- Upgrade to a higher plan (if available)

## In-app support questions

When you tap **Support** in the Coach tab to report a bug or ask a help-center question, that conversation **does not count** against your monthly Coach Mo quota. Support is unlimited.

<Note>
  Want to make the most of your quota? Ask Mo specific, contextual questions ("how should I pace my Move ring today?") rather than open-ended ones ("tell me about fitness") — you'll get more useful answers in fewer messages.
</Note>
