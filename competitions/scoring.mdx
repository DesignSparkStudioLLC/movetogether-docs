Every competition uses the same simple scoring system.

## How points are earned

You earn **1 point** each day for closing each of your three rings:

| Ring | Point earned when… |
|------|---------------------|
| Move | Daily active calories goal met |
| Exercise | Daily exercise minutes goal met |
| Steps | Daily step goal met |

That's up to **3 points per day**, every day of the competition.

## Your total

Your competition total is just the sum of your daily points across the date window.

A weekend competition (3 days) has a max of **9 points**. A weekly is **21**. A monthly is **~90**, depending on the month.

## Ties

If two participants finish with the same point total, the leaderboard breaks the tie by total active calories across the window.

## Why this system?

Points reward **consistency**, not raw output. A person who closes all three rings every day will beat a person who has one massive workout day and then nothing — which keeps competitions fair across fitness levels and trackers.
