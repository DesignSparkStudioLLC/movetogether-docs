---
title: "Viewing your competition history"
description: "How to open your completed competition archive, review your wins and podium finishes, search by competition name, and revisit past results."
---

Completed competitions are saved to your history so you can revisit past results and open their detail screens.

## Where to find it

Open the **Compete** tab and tap the archive icon in the navigation bar.

## What you'll see for each competition

- A summary of how many competitions you completed
- Your win and podium-finish totals
- Completed competition cards, grouped by year when the archive is long

Tap a past competition to open its detail screen and final leaderboard.

## Searching your history

When you have more than five completed competitions, a search field appears. Search by competition name to narrow the archive.

<Note>
  Your competition-history archive is your own view. Other participants can still revisit competitions they also joined.
</Note>
