---
title: "Viewing your competition history"
description: "How to view your MoveTogether competition history, revisit past leaderboards, see final rankings and winners, and check your overall win rate."
---

Every competition you've joined is saved to your competition history, so you can look back at past results, see your win rate, and revisit the leaderboard from any past event.

## Where to find it

Open the **Profile** tab and tap **Competition History**. You'll see every competition you've participated in, most recent first.

## What you'll see for each competition

- The competition name and date range
- Your final ranking
- The winner
- Total participants
- The scoring method used

Tap any past competition to open its full leaderboard and see how everyone finished.

## Filtering your history

You can filter your history by:

- **Time range** — past 7 days, past month, past year, all-time
- **Status** — completed, active, upcoming
- **Type** — solo or team competitions

## Why your history matters

Past results power a few things in the app:

- **Win streaks** — consecutive competition wins unlock achievements.
- **Head-to-head records** — if you compete against the same person repeatedly, your history tracks the rivalry.
- **Personal best** comparisons — the "Past You" ghost on live leaderboards is based on your best historical performance.

<Note>
  Competition history is your view only. It doesn't show on your public profile unless you've turned that on in **Settings → Privacy**.
</Note>
