Move Leagues are free weekly cohorts. Open **Compete**, then the leaderboard launcher and **League**, to see your tier, cohort, current zone, and countdown.

## The ladder

You begin at Walker. A full cohort contains up to 30 people at the same tier. Top finishers promote, people in the middle hold their tier, and the demotion zone moves down one tier. The displayed zones are live estimates; actual thresholds scale for a smaller cohort.

| # | Tier | Promotion at a full cohort | Demotion at a full cohort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Walker | Top 15 | None |
| 2 | Jogger | Top 12 | Bottom 5 |
| 3 | Runner | Top 10 | Bottom 5 |
| 4 | Strider | Top 9 | Bottom 5 |
| 5 | Athlete | Top 8 | Bottom 5 |
| 6 | Sprinter | Top 7 | Bottom 5 |
| 7 | Champion | Top 6 | Bottom 5 |
| 8 | Elite | Top 5 | Bottom 5 |
| 9 | Master | Top 3 | Bottom 5 |
| 10 | Legend | None | Bottom 5 |

Promotion is capped at half the cohort. For example, a 10-person Jogger cohort promotes its top five rather than twelve. Demotion also scales down in smaller cohorts, leaving at least one safe middle position. Walker is the floor; Legend is the top tier.

## Weekly timing

Your league week runs from Monday at midnight through Sunday in your own time zone. People in one cohort can live in different time zones, so each person's week closes at their own local Monday.

After the week ends, the League card shows **Results pending** while everyone in the cohort finishes and their trackers sync. Results land by 6 a.m. Eastern Time every Monday, sooner when the whole cohort's week has already ended. Then MoveTogether records your result and assigns your next cohort at your new tier. Your new week's points count from Monday while your next group is being prepared.

If a cohort has fewer than 8 people, it's a quiet week: nobody promotes or demotes. The League card shows **No promotions or demotions this week**.

## Scoring

League points for the week are:

```text
points = move calories + exercise minutes + round(week's steps ÷ 100)
```

Calories and minutes are added up across Monday to Sunday. Steps are added up for the whole week first, then divided by 100 and rounded once. Your daily ring goals don't change your league score, so someone with a low Move goal can't out-rank someone with a tougher goal for the same activity.

**Example:** a week with 2,940 active calories, 245 exercise minutes, and 57,750 steps scores `2,940 + 245 + round(577.5) = 3,763` league points.

The **Friends** leaderboard ranks today's activity and resets at your local midnight. The **Global** leaderboard ranks the current week with its own formula, so your Global score won't match your League points.

## Zones and ties

The leaderboard marks a promotion zone, safety zone, and demotion zone. A league’s final order uses a stable system tie-break based on account identity, so changing a display name cannot affect promotion or demotion. Monthly challenge standings work differently; see [monthly challenges](/engagement/monthly-challenges).

## Demotion shield

You start with one demotion shield. If you finish in the demotion zone while holding it, it's used automatically and you stay at your current tier. Once it's used, it's gone. The League card shows whether you have one.

## Reading the other leaderboard tabs

- **League:** your current weekly cohort and tier.
- **Friends:** a daily ranking of people you have added; it resets at local midnight.
- **Global:** the top 100 movers worldwide for the current week.

For a direct challenge with friends using a different scoring method, create or join a competition. For missing data or a delayed position change, see [competition troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/competition-issues).
