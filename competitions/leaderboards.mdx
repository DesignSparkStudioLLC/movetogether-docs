---
title: "Leaderboards & rankings"
description: "How real-time rankings are calculated and displayed."
---

Every competition has a live leaderboard that updates as participants' trackers sync.

## What you see

- **Your rank** at the top, with the gap to the person above and below.
- **The full ranking**, sorted by total points (then active calories as a tiebreaker).
- **Each person's daily ring breakdown** — tap a name to see their day-by-day chart.

## How often does it update?

Leaderboards refresh whenever a participant's tracker syncs to MoveTogether. For Apple Watch users this is typically within a few minutes. Garmin and Whoop can take up to 15 minutes.

## Privacy

Leaderboards only show the metrics needed to rank participants — points, rings closed, and active calories. They do **not** expose heart rate, workout types, sleep, weight, or any other health data your tracker collects.
