## Joining an existing competition

1. Open the invite link or QR code your friend sent you.
2. If you're not signed in yet, you'll be prompted to create an account first.
3. Tap **Join** on the competition card — you're in.

You can also browse open competitions from the **Compete** tab.

## Creating a new competition

1. Open the **Compete** tab.
2. Tap **+** in the top-right corner.
3. Choose a [competition type](/competitions/types) (weekend, weekly, monthly, or custom).
4. Set the start date and invite participants.
5. Tap **Create** and share the invite link.

## How many competitions can I be in at once?

This depends on your [subscription plan](/subscriptions/plans). Starter members can be in one competition at a time; Mover and Crusher unlock more.

<Note>
  Need help with a specific competition? Open it in the app and tap the **?** in the top-right corner for context-aware help from Coach Mo.
</Note>
