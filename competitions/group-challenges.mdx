A Group Challenge is a bigger competition for a company, gym, club, or event. It holds up to 100 people and can split them into as many as 20 teams (divisions). It can use any scoring method.

<Note>
  Hosting a Group Challenge needs Pro and the iPhone app. Membership isn't enough. Joining one is always free, on every plan.
</Note>

## Group Challenge or shared goal?

They're different things, and you can use them together:

- A **Group Challenge** (Pro to host) is a large competition with a participant limit and up to 20 teams. Teams race each other.
- A **shared goal** (Membership or Pro to host) is an extra on top of any competition: everyone's activity also adds up toward one big number. See [personal and shared goals](/competitions/personal-and-shared-goals).

## Host a Group Challenge

1. Open **Compete**, tap the **+** (**Add competition**) button, then **Host a group challenge**. If you're not on Pro, this opens the upgrade screen.
2. Name it, choose scoring, and pick dates as usual.
3. On **Public or private?**, under **Challenge size**, **Group challenge** is turned on. Set the most people who can join with the **people max** control: 10 to 100, one at a time (hold **\+** or **−** to move faster). The default is 50.
4. To split people into divisions, turn on **Team competition** (**Compete in groups instead of individually**). Then either:
   - set **people per team** (for example, teams of 4). The app works out how many teams you need from the people max: 50 people in teams of 4 makes 13 teams. Nobody can join a team that's already full. Teams can be as small as the people max allows within 20 teams: up to 40 people can be in teams of 2, 41 to 60 in teams of 3, 61 to 80 in teams of 4, and 81 to 100 in teams of 5. Or
   - leave **people per team** on **Any** and choose up to 20 teams yourself. Normal competitions allow up to 4.
5. Choose **TEAM ASSIGNMENT**: **Auto-assign** balances players across teams when they join. **Manual** lets you assign each invited player to a team on the next step.
6. Review the summary and tap **Create competition**.

You can also turn on **Group challenge** from the **Challenge size** section while making any competition. Without Pro, it shows a **PRO** badge.

## Invite people

Open the competition and tap **More options** (the **⋯** button). The invite row shows how full it is, such as **Invite people (12/50 joined)**. Use **Share invite link** to send one link to everyone.

When the limit is reached, new people see "This competition is full."

## Join a Group Challenge

Open the invite link or use **Join with code**, then tap **Join Competition**. Joining is free on every plan.

## Standings

The competition screen starts with a **Your team** card: your team's rank (such as **#4 of 13**) and your teammates with their points. Tap it to see your whole team.

Below it, **Team Standings** ranks every team by **average points per person**, so a team that's one person short isn't penalized. The list shows the top 5 and your team; tap **Show all** for the rest. Tap any team to see who's on it. On the leaderboard, a small dot in each person's team color shows which team they're on.

Each person also has their own score on the leaderboard. See [the leaderboard](/competitions/leaderboard) and the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference).
