title: "Competition types"
description: "Weekend, weekly, monthly, and custom — pick what fits your group."
---

MoveTogether offers four competition formats. Pick whichever matches the rhythm of your group.

## Weekend

A short, high-energy challenge that runs **Friday at midnight through Sunday at 11:59 PM** in each participant's local time zone. Perfect for first-time competitions or low-commitment fun.

## Weekly

Runs **Monday through Sunday**. The classic format — long enough that one bad day doesn't sink you, short enough to stay engaged. Most groups settle into a recurring weekly cadence.

## Monthly

Runs from **the 1st through the last day of the calendar month**. Best for long-term consistency and bigger group challenges. Pacing matters more than peak effort.

## Custom

Pick **any start and end date**. Useful for training blocks, vacation challenges, or syncing a competition with a real-world event (race week, holiday push, etc.).

## Which should I pick?

- **First time?** Weekend.
- **Steady group of friends?** Weekly, on repeat.
- **Big group / office wellness?** Monthly.
- **Training for something specific?** Custom.

<Note>
  All four types use the same [points and scoring system](/competitions/scoring) — only the date window changes.
</Note>
