description: "The basics: rings, points, and scoring."
---

A MoveTogether competition is a head-to-head challenge over a set time window. Every day you complete activity, you earn points. The person with the most points at the end wins.

## The basics

- **Every day, you can earn up to 3 points** — one for closing each of your three rings (Move, Exercise, Steps).
- **Your total for the competition** is the sum of your daily points across the competition window.
- **The leaderboard updates in real time** as everyone's activity syncs from their tracker.

## What "closing a ring" means

Each ring has a personalized goal:

- **Move** — daily active calories goal
- **Exercise** — daily exercise minutes goal
- **Steps** — daily step goal

Hit the goal, the ring closes, and you get a point. Goals are set in **Settings → Goals** and can be adjusted any time.

## What if my tracker doesn't measure one of the rings?

MoveTogether normalizes activity across all eight supported trackers — every one can populate all three rings. If you're seeing a gap, check that your tracker has finished syncing today's data.
