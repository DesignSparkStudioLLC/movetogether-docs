## Scoring methods explained

**Ring Close Count.** Points for closing each activity ring. You earn points every time you close your Move, Exercise, or Steps ring. It rewards consistent daily activity and is great for keeping everyone accountable.

**Percentage of Goals.** Points based on goal completion percentage. Instead of raw numbers, this method looks at what percentage of your personal goals you've hit. This levels the playing field since everyone's goals are different — a beginner working toward 5,000 steps gets rewarded just as much as an athlete aiming for 15,000.

**Raw Numbers.** Points for every calorie, minute, and step of activity. The straightforward approach — the more you move, the more points you get. Every active calorie burned, every exercise minute logged, and every step counts toward your score.

**Step Count.** Points for every step taken. The classic competition — who can walk the most steps? Simple, easy to understand, and every tracker measures it.

**Workout Based.** Points only during specific workout types. This method focuses on dedicated exercise sessions rather than all-day activity. Only activity logged during recognized workouts counts toward your score, rewarding intentional exercise.

## Team mode

Competitions can be run as **Individual** (the default, where everyone competes solo) or as a **Team Competition**. When creating a team competition, you choose 2, 3, or 4 teams. Each team's combined score determines the ranking, encouraging collaboration and group motivation. Participants join a team when they enter the competition.

## Competition duration

You have full flexibility over how long your competition lasts. MoveTogether offers preset durations — **Weekend** (Saturday–Sunday), **Weekly** (Monday–Sunday), and **Monthly** — or you can set a **Custom** date range using the date picker. You can also enable repeating competitions so they automatically recur on a cycle.

## Competition lifecycle

Every competition goes through three stages:

- **Upcoming** — The competition exists and friends can join, but it hasn't started yet. The creator can still edit settings during this phase.
- **Active** — The start date has arrived. Activity is now being tracked, leaderboards are live, and scores update in real time.
- **Completed** — The end date has passed. Final rankings are locked in, winners are announced, and achievements are awarded.

## Repeating competitions

When you enable **Repeat** on a competition, it automatically creates a new cycle when the current one ends. This is perfect for ongoing weekly challenges with your friend group. Each cycle is tracked separately so you can see your performance over time.

## Joining a competition

There are three ways to join: tap an **Invite Link** shared by a friend, enter a **Competition Code** in the Compete tab, or accept an **In-App Invitation** from a friend who invited you. You can join a competition after it has started, but only data from the time you join onward will count toward your score.

## Leaving or deleting a competition

To leave, open the competition from the Compete tab, tap the more options menu (three dots), and select **Leave Competition**. Leaving removes you from the leaderboard and cannot be undone.

Only the competition creator can delete a competition. Deleting removes the competition for all participants.

## Fair play across different trackers

One of MoveTogether's core promises is that everyone competes on a level playing field, regardless of which tracker they use. The app normalizes data across all supported devices so that differences in how trackers calculate metrics don't unfairly advantage or disadvantage anyone. All scores are calculated server-side to ensure accuracy and prevent manipulation.
