title: "How competitions work"
description: "How to join or host a MoveTogether competition, choose a scoring method, understand its lifecycle, and run repeating cycles."
---

Competitions turn synced activity into a shared leaderboard or a shared goal. Open **Compete** to join with an invite, code, or in-app invitation; use **+** to host one.

## Join versus host

You can join competitions on every plan. The Free limit applies to **hosting**: Free accounts can host up to two eligible competitions at a time, while Pro can host unlimited. An active, upcoming, or draft competition you host uses a slot. Seasonal and evergreen competitions do not use that limit, and an eligible bonus slot can let a Free host create another one.

Completing or deleting an eligible hosted competition releases its slot. If joining fails, check the invite, participant eligibility, and whether the competition has ended; upgrading is not required merely to join.

## Choose a format

Use **Individual** for a normal ranking, or enable **Team** and choose two to four teams. Team standings combine the members’ scores. Some formats also offer a shared group goal; that is progress the group works toward, not a replacement for the selected scoring rule.

The host chooses a start and end date. The wizard offers weekend, weekly, monthly, and custom windows. Before the competition starts, the host can edit its settings. During an active competition, changing scoring needs the group’s agreement; see [changing competition scoring](/competitions/changing-scoring).

## Lifecycle and scores

- **Upcoming:** people can join and the competition has not begun.
- **Active:** synced activity is scored and the leaderboard updates as data arrives.
- **Completed:** the cycle’s standings are finalized and retained in history.

Activity is calculated on the server. A sync delay can make a score appear late, and a refresh cannot create activity that has not reached MoveTogether yet.

When someone joins an active competition, MoveTogether schedules a backfill of already-elapsed dates in the competition window. It skips dates before that person’s signup and dates with no activity record. A Free member who joins a seasonal event does not receive pre-join seasonal days; the scoring window starts at their join date. The backfill runs after the join response, so the person can appear in the competition before older eligible days finish processing.

## Repeating competitions

When a host selects **Repeat**, MoveTogether creates sequential cycles with the same competition setup. Each cycle has its own dates and final standings. There can be a gap between cycles for formats whose next window does not begin immediately; the next cycle then appears as upcoming until its start date.

Use a repeating competition for a standing weekly or monthly group, not when you need one uninterrupted all-time total. Review each cycle’s result in the competition details or your competition history.
