---
title: "Badges and achievements"
description: "How MoveTogether badges are organized, which badge groups are available on Free and Pro, and where to view the badges you earn."
---

MoveTogether uses **badges** as the umbrella for the rewards shown on your profile. Badges are named accomplishments, not Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum medal tiers.

## Badge groups

**Streak milestones.** Recognize sustained activity-ring streaks.

**Achievements.** Recognize cumulative activity and notable fitness milestones.

**Competition.** Recognize wins, comebacks, close finishes, rivalries, and other competitive accomplishments.

**Seasonal events.** Limited-time badges tied to seasonal event requirements.

**Referrals.** The Ringleader badge is unlocked through the referral program.

## Free and Pro access

- **Free:** seasonal-event badges and referral rewards are available without Pro.
- **Pro:** streak, achievement, and competition badge groups are included with Pro.

Each badge's detail view explains its requirement. Some badges are earned as soon as their requirement is met, while referral Pro-time rewards are claimed separately from **Invite & Earn**.

## Viewing your badges

Open the **Profile** tab and go to your badges to see what you have earned and review other available badges. You can choose an earned badge to feature on your profile from **Settings → Personal Details**.
