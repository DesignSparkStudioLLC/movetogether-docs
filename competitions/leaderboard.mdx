## Reading the leaderboard

Every competition has a leaderboard that shows all participants ranked by their current score. You'll see each participant's rank, profile photo and display name, current score based on the scoring method, and how far ahead or behind you are compared to others.

## Past You ghost

The leaderboard features a **"Past You" ghost row** that compares your current performance against your personal best week. This ghost appears with distinct styling and sits in the leaderboard so you can see how you're tracking against your own history — not just against others. It's a great way to stay motivated even when you're ahead of the pack.

## Real-time updates

Leaderboard scores update automatically as participants' fitness data syncs throughout the day. You don't need to manually refresh, though you can pull down on the screen to trigger a manual refresh. Apple Watch users see updates within minutes; other tracker users typically see updates within 15–30 minutes.

## Competition events

The leaderboard tracks real-time competition events, including ring closures, position overtakes, taunts, and daily challenge completions. These events add energy to the competition and keep things interesting throughout the day.

## Privacy on the leaderboard

Your privacy settings control how much information competitors can see. You can choose whether to show your real name or display name, and whether competitors see your exact score or just your rank. Adjust these in **Settings → Privacy**.
