The **Leaderboard** screen sits at the top of the Compete tab and has three tabs: **League** (your weekly cohort of up to 30 movers at your tier), **Friends** (a daily ranking of the friends you've added), and **Global** (the top 100 movers worldwide for the week). This page covers the leaderboard inside an individual competition. For the weekly league ladder, see [Move Leagues](/competitions/leagues).

## Reading the leaderboard

Every competition has a leaderboard that ranks all participants by their current score. You'll see each person's rank, profile photo and display name, current score, and how far ahead or behind you are.

## Past You ghost

The **Past You** ghost is a Pro feature. It compares your current performance with your best historical week for the relevant scoring type and cadence. Free members may see a locked preview.

## Real-time updates

Scores update automatically as participants' fitness data syncs throughout the day. Sync timing varies by data source and connectivity; pulling down on the screen triggers a manual refresh.

## Competition events

The leaderboard tracks real-time competition events: ring closures, position overtakes, taunts, daily challenge completions. These add energy and keep things interesting throughout the day.

## Privacy on the leaderboard

Your privacy settings control whether leaderboards show your real name, whether you appear on public leaderboards, and whether competitors can see detailed stats. Adjust these in **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
