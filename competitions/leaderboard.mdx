The **Leaderboard** screen at the top of **Compete** has three views: **League** (your weekly cohort of up to 30 people at your tier), **Friends** (a daily ranking of your friends), and **Global** (the top 100 worldwide for the week). This page covers the leaderboard inside a competition. For the weekly ladder, see [Move Leagues](/competitions/leagues).

## Reading the leaderboard

Each competition ranks its participants by current score, showing rank, photo, name, score, and how far ahead or behind you are. In a [Distance competition](/competitions/distance), the host can choose to show miles or kilometers instead of points.

## You vs. the people around you (iPhone)

Above the leaderboard, a rival card shows the person just ahead of you, you, and the person just behind, with the gap between each pair. It answers who you need to catch and who is catching you. For example: "You're #3 of 8", "120 pts to catch Sam", and "Alex is 45 pts behind you." When two people show the same score, they share a rank, so the card says "Tied for #1" or "Level with Sam".

The card is hidden when you're not on the leaderboard or you're the only one on it.

## Customize view (iPhone)

You can hide the rival card or the **Top Performers podium** on your own screen. It only changes what you see; everyone else's screen stays the same, and the choice applies to every competition.

1. On a competition with at least two people, tap the sliders button above the cards (**Customize view**).
2. Turn **You vs. the people around you** or **Top Performers podium** on or off.

You can also change these in **Settings → Customization → Competition screen**.

## Past You ghost

The **Past You** ghost is a Pro feature. It compares your current performance with your best past week for the same scoring type and cadence. Without Pro you may see a locked preview.

## Real-time updates

Scores update as each person's activity syncs from Apple Health or Health Connect. Pull down to refresh.

## Activity feed

The competition's **Activity** section shows live events such as lead changes, overtakes, close races, all rings closed, workouts, records, people joining, and taunts.

## Privacy on the leaderboard

Your privacy settings control whether leaderboards show your real name, whether you appear on public leaderboards, and whether competitors can see detailed stats. Adjust these in **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
