Weekly challenges build up across the week. They are separate from the challenges you pick under **Choose a challenge**, your workout streak, and the weekly Move League race.

## Open the weekly list

1. On **Home**, find the **Challenges** card.
2. Tap the **Challenges** heading to open the full screen.
3. Scroll to **All week**, below the challenges you pick.
4. Read each challenge's target and progress.

## Read progress

Each row shows the challenge and how far you have progressed toward its target. The target tells you whether you are accumulating an activity total or satisfying another requirement. A completion indicator means that challenge's requirement has been met; it does not mean all challenges for the week are finished.

**Example:** if a challenge asks for an activity total across the week, activity from several days can contribute. You do not need to reach that entire total in one day unless the challenge explicitly says so.

## Weekly reset

The weekly window resets at **Monday 00:00 UTC**. This is a single worldwide instant, so it can be Sunday evening in the Americas. The swap allowance for the challenges you pick resets at your local midnight instead.

Check the current challenge and its period when a value seems to have reset. A new week's challenge is a new target, not lost progress in the previous one.

## Points and the monthly badge

Weekly challenges don't earn points and don't count toward the [monthly challenge badge](/engagement/monthly-challenges). Only the challenges you pick under **Choose a challenge** and finish do. See [Choose a challenge](/engagement/daily-challenges).

## A challenge is not updating

Wait for your tracker to finish syncing, then pull to refresh the Challenges screen. Check that the challenge measures the activity you recorded and that you are looking at its current period. Use [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources) for missing readings or message us in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) if synced activity still does not count.
