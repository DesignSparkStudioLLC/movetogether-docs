---
title: "Complete weekly challenges"
description: "Find this week's challenges, follow progress, and understand their weekly reset."
---

Open the challenges sheet from Home to see daily and weekly challenges. The weekly section shows this week's progress and any completion bonus available in the app.

Weekly challenges are generated on Monday and reset at Monday 00:00 UTC. Daily challenges reset at your local midnight. Progress depends on synced activity, so allow time for your source to update and check **Settings → Data Sources** if it does not.
