## What seasonal events are

Throughout the year, MoveTogether runs themed competitions and events. Limited-time challenges with unique themes, special badge rewards, and a sense of community-wide participation. New Year's kickoff challenges, summer fitness pushes, holiday-themed competitions, that kind of thing.

## How to join

Seasonal events appear in the **Discover** section of your **Compete** tab when they're active. You'll get a notification when a new event launches (if you have seasonal event notifications turned on). Tap the event and join like any other competition.

## Event badges

Joining and completing seasonal events earns you exclusive event badges. They appear on your profile and are a nice way to show you were part of something. Some badges are only available during specific events, which makes them collector's items.

## Event notifications

Turn seasonal event notifications on or off in **Settings → Notifications**.
