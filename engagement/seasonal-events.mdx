## What seasonal events are

Throughout the year, MoveTogether runs themed competitions and events. Limited-time challenges with unique themes, special badge rewards, and a sense of community-wide participation. New Year's kickoff challenges, summer fitness pushes, holiday-themed competitions, that kind of thing.

## How to join

Seasonal events appear in the **Compete** tab when they are active. Tap an event to see its rules, timing, and reward requirements before joining.

## Formats

- **Challenge:** complete a personal progressive goal; there is no ranking.
- **Competition:** compete on a leaderboard. Some events require a number of active days to qualify for a badge, with placement variants for top qualifiers.
- **Community:** contribute with everyone toward a shared target; there is no ranking.

## Event badges

Event rewards vary. Read the event details for the qualification rule rather than assuming that joining alone earns a badge.

## Event notifications

Turn seasonal event notifications on or off in **Settings → Notifications**.
