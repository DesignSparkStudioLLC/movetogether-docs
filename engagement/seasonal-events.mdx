## What are seasonal events?

Throughout the year, MoveTogether runs special themed competitions and events. These are limited-time challenges with unique themes, special badge rewards, and a sense of community-wide participation. Think New Year's kickoff challenges, summer fitness pushes, and holiday-themed competitions.

## How to join

Seasonal events appear in the **Discover** section of your **Compete** tab when they're active. You'll also receive a notification when a new event launches (if you have seasonal event notifications enabled). Just tap the event and join like any other competition.

## Event badges

Participating in and completing seasonal events can earn you exclusive event badges. These badges appear on your profile and are a great way to show you were part of something special. Some badges are only available during specific events, making them collector's items.

## Event notifications

You can control whether you receive notifications about seasonal events in **Settings → Notifications**. Toggle **Seasonal Events** on or off based on your preference.
