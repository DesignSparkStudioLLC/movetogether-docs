## What are daily challenges?

Every day, MoveTogether generates fresh challenges to keep your fitness routine interesting. These are short, specific goals designed to push you just beyond your comfort zone — like hitting a step target, closing a specific ring, or completing a workout.

## How challenges are generated

Daily challenges are personalized to your fitness level. For Pro users, the app analyzes your recent activity averages and sets targets 10–20% above what you've been doing. This means challenges grow with you as you get fitter. Free users receive challenges from a general template pool.

## Completing challenges

Challenges are tracked automatically through your connected fitness tracker. When you hit the target, the challenge is marked as complete. You'll see your daily challenges on the **Home** tab, and completing them can trigger competition events visible to your competitors.

## Challenge variety

Challenges cover the full range of fitness metrics: step targets, calorie goals, exercise minute goals, workout challenges, and ring closure goals. You'll get a unique set each day so your routine never feels repetitive.
