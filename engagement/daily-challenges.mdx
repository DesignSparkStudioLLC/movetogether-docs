description: "Pick challenges from five offers, hold up to three, swap or remove up to three a day, earn points, and see your rank in your group and your completed challenges."

Challenges are personal targets you pick yourself, such as a step count, active calories, exercise minutes, or workouts. Each one is worth points. MoveTogether tracks them from synced activity, so you never mark one complete yourself. Challenges are free on every plan.

## Open your challenges

1. Open **Home** and find **Challenges**.
2. Tap the **Challenges** heading to open the full screen.

The Home card shows your points this month, the challenge you're closest to finishing, and your rank. With nothing picked yet, it says **Pick a challenge**.

## Pick a challenge

Under **Choose a challenge** you get five offers. Each shows its points (for example **+21**) and when it counts:

| The offer says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **One day. Starts now.** | Reach the target today. Only activity after you pick it counts. |
| **One day. Counts from tomorrow.** | Reach the target in one full day, starting tomorrow. |
| **On 5 different days. Counts from tomorrow.** | Reach the target on 5 days, in any order, starting tomorrow. |
| **7 days in a row. Counts from tomorrow.** | Reach the target 7 days running, starting tomorrow. |

Tap **Pick** to take one. It moves to **Active**, and a fresh offer fills its spot.

- You can hold up to **3** challenges at once. The **Active** heading shows how many you hold, such as "2 of 3 held". With 3 held, finish or remove one before you pick another.
- You can pick up to 4 new challenges a day. After that you'll see "That's enough new challenges for today."
- Offers use the kinds of activity you've been logging recently. If you don't track a metric, you won't be offered challenges for it.

## Swap or remove

Don't like an offer? Tap **Swap** to replace it. To drop a challenge you're holding, tap **Remove** on it. It won't earn any points.

Swaps and removals share one allowance: **3 a day**. The offers show how many you have left, such as "2 swaps left today". When they run out you'll see "No swaps left today. More tomorrow." The allowance resets at your local midnight.

## Points

Harder challenges are worth more. The points are fixed when you pick a challenge, so they never change after that.

When synced activity reaches the target, the card says **Done. +21 points banked** and the points are added to your total. A finished challenge leaves **Active**, which frees a spot.

Held challenges have no deadline within the month. Any challenge still unfinished when your month ends earns nothing, and a new month starts with fresh offers.

## Read your progress

Each held challenge has a progress bar and one of these lines:

- **Best day: 6,200 of 8,000** for a one-day target.
- **2 of 5 days** for a multi-day target.
- **0 of 7 days. Counts from Oct 5.** before a multi-day target starts.

## Your points and rank

At the top of the screen, **Your points** shows your **points this month**, your rank (such as **#4 of 50**), and how many days are left in the month. Tap **View the live leaderboard** to see everyone in your group.

You're ranked by points against a group of about 50 people, not against everyone. Groups are set at the start of each month and stay the same all month. If two people have the same points, whoever got there first ranks higher.

If you've turned off appearing on public leaderboards in **Settings → Privacy Settings**, your name is hidden from everyone on the leaderboard, including you. Your points and progress still count.

## Completed challenges

**Completed this month** lists your three most recent finished challenges, each with its points. Tap **View all completed** to open **Completed Challenges**, a list of every challenge you've finished, newest first, grouped by month.

Finished challenges also count toward the [monthly challenge badge](/engagement/monthly-challenges).

## If progress looks wrong

Check the offer's timing line first: a challenge that **Counts from tomorrow** ignores today, and one that **Starts now** ignores what you did before you picked it. Then compare the target with the same metric and date in your activity. Steps and workouts typed in by hand never count.

Refresh after your tracker syncs, and check [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources) if a reading is missing. If a target stays incomplete despite matching synced activity, message us in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) with the challenge name, date, and metric.
