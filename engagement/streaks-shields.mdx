## How streaks work

MoveTogether tracks your consecutive days of ring activity. Every day you close at least one activity ring, your streak continues. Streaks are a powerful motivator — once you've built up a streak, you won't want to break it.

## Streak Shields

Life happens — rest days, travel, illness. That's why MoveTogether includes a **shield** system that forgives missed days. If you miss a day, a shield is automatically used to protect your streak. You earn shields by hitting streak milestones, so the longer your streak, the more protection you have. Think of shields as "get out of jail free" cards for your streak.

## Streak milestone rewards

You'll earn rewards when you hit these streak milestones:

| Milestone | Days |
|-----------|------|
| First milestone | 7 days |
| Building momentum | 14 days |
| Committed | 30 days |
| Dedicated | 60 days |
| Centurion | 100 days |
| Unstoppable | 200 days |
| Full year | 365 days |

## Competition streak bonuses

Active streaks can earn you a **3-point bonus** in competitions, giving you a small edge for your consistency. This rewards users who show up every day, not just on competition days.

## Viewing your streak

Your current streak is displayed on the **Home** tab as a streak widget. You can see your current streak count, your longest streak ever, how many shields you have, and your next milestone.
