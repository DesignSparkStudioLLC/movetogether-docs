Share a snapshot of your week from the [Weekly Mo Report](/engagement/weekly-report). The report and its share cards are Pro features.

## Choose a card

1. Open the weekly report from **Home**.
2. Open its share control to reach **Pick your vibe**.
3. Swipe between the available styles and preview the selected image.
4. Tap **Share** to open your phone's share sheet.
5. Choose an app or destination and finish its sharing flow.

| Style | What it emphasizes |
| --- | --- |
| Grade | Your weekly grade and headline |
| Mo's Take | A quote or interpretation of the week, including roast-style content when present |
| Rings | Your ring progress in a visual summary |

**Mo's Take** needs a quote or suitable story text. If that content is unavailable, the picker can omit the style rather than show a blank card.

## Preview before sending

The image contains personal results and can include your handle or other information shown in the preview. Once you send the image outside MoveTogether, the receiving app and people you share it with control their copies. Changing a profile setting later does not recall an image you already shared.

Cards are generated as images, not live dashboards. Sharing a later version after more data arrives does not update an earlier copy.

## Backgrounds and loading

The Grade style uses a background associated with the grade: sunrise coast for A, trail mountain for B, urban dawn for C, morning fog for D, and stadium lights for F. A fallback treatment can appear while artwork loads or when it cannot be fetched.

If sharing is not ready immediately, wait for the selected card to render. You do not need to repeatedly reopen the report to force an image download.

## If sharing fails

Try opening the picker again and selecting a destination that accepts images. If the destination app fails after the system share sheet opens, check that app's state and connection too.

For a spreadsheet rather than a picture, use [Export an activity report](/engagement/export-activity-report). For help with missing report content, see [Weekly Mo Report](/engagement/weekly-report#why-a-report-may-be-missing).
