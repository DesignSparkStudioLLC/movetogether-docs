---
title: "Review activity analytics, sleep, weight, and BMI"
description: "Understand the activity and body metrics MoveTogether can display when your data source provides them."
---

Open **Today's Activity** from the **Profile** tab to review your activity. Free includes daily Move, Exercise, and Steps rings. Pro adds activity trends and available sleep, weight, and BMI analytics. These features are for fitness context, not medical diagnosis.

## Keep data current

Connect or review a provider in **Settings → Data Sources**. Apple Health permissions are managed in iPhone Settings. Sleep, weight, and other metrics appear only when the selected source provides them and you have granted the applicable access. You can log weight from the weight analytics experience.

BMI uses height and weight. Set or update height from the BMI card, and treat the result as a general reference rather than medical advice. See [Troubleshoot data sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources) if a metric is missing.
