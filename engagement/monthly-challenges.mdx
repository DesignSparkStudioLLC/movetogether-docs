---
title: "Your monthly challenge badge"
description: "Earn each month's challenge badge by finishing challenges you pick, see how points rank you in your group, and manage ranking privacy."
---

Each month has its own challenge badge. You earn it by finishing enough of the challenges you pick under **Choose a challenge**. You can earn it on any plan, and missing a day doesn't remove completions you already have.

## Find your progress

1. Open **Home → Challenges**, then tap the **Challenges** heading.
2. Find the card with this month's badge.
3. Read your count against the target, such as **12/30**.

The card says how far you have to go, for example "18 more to earn this month's badge." Once you have it, it says "You've earned this month's badge." Use the target shown on the card. Targets and badge artwork can differ by month. October 2026's target is 30.

## What counts

Only challenges you pick under **Choose a challenge** and finish count toward the badge. Each finished challenge counts as one, whatever its points. The weekly challenges listed under **All week** don't count toward the badge. See [Choose a challenge](/engagement/daily-challenges).

A challenge counts in the month you finish it, in your local time zone. A challenge still unfinished when your month ends doesn't carry over and doesn't count.

## Earning the badge

Reach the month's target to earn its badge. It isn't reserved for first place, and you don't need Pro. Check your [badges](/competitions/achievements) after the completed challenge has synced.

## Points and rank

The badge and the ranking measure different things. Finished challenges earn the badge. **Points** decide your rank.

You're ranked by points against a group of about 50 people, set at the start of the month. If two people have the same points, whoever got there first ranks higher. Tap **View the live leaderboard** on the Challenges screen to see your group. Each row also shows how many challenges that person has finished.

This is different from the tie-breaking used for a [Move League](/competitions/leagues).

## Ranking privacy

Your public leaderboard setting affects whether you appear, including in your own view of the leaderboard. Hiding your public ranking doesn't stop your progress or badge.

Review **Settings → Privacy Settings** if your progress is present but your row is missing. Blocking can also affect which rows you see.

## If a completion is missing

Refresh Challenges after activity syncs. Check that the challenge is finished, not merely close to its target, and check which month you finished it in. If the count still looks wrong, send the challenge name and completion date through the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
