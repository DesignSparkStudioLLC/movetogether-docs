The **Weekly Mo Report** is a Pro feature that reviews your previous completed week. It arrives **Monday morning** and covers the preceding **Monday to Sunday** period. It is an in-app report, not an email recap.

## Open your report

1. Open **Home** after the report is ready.
2. Tap the **Weekly recap** tile (it shows the week's dates and your grade), or open the report's notification.
3. Check the report's week before comparing it with activity totals.
4. Scroll the **Weekly recap** screen to read your grade, highlights, competition performance, and coaching observations.

The tile only opens a recent report. About 5 days after the week ends, it stops opening that report and reads **Coming Monday** until the next one is ready.

Your selected Coach Mo coaching style affects the writing. The report uses the available fitness context; it is not a replacement for the underlying activity history when you need an exact daily reading.

## Understand the period

Monday's report looks backward. Activity you record on that Monday belongs to the new week, not the Sunday-ended week being reviewed.

**Example:** when checking a report against your tracker, select the same Monday through Sunday. Comparing it with a rolling “last seven days” view that includes today can produce a different total.

## Share your week

At the bottom of the report, tap **Share with friends** to open **Pick your vibe**. Preview a **Grade**, **Mo's Take**, or **Rings** card, then tap **Share** to choose where to send it, or **Save to Photos**. Preview the image before sharing because it contains your personal results.

See [sharing your wins](/engagement/sharing-your-wins) for the card styles. A roast card is a shareable interpretation of your week, not another scoring method.

## Why a report may be missing

- Check that your account has Pro access.
- Confirm you are looking for a completed week's report rather than an in-progress week. If the tile reads **Coming Monday**, the report isn't ready yet or the last one is more than about 5 days old.
- Refresh Home and check connectivity.
- Check whether activity from that period has synced.
- If the report is present but no alert arrived, check [notification settings](/troubleshooting/notification-problems).

Generation or delivery can be delayed; the normal Monday-morning schedule is not a guarantee of an exact minute. If a report remains unavailable, tell the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) which week you expected.

For today's briefing, use [Morning Brief](/engagement/morning-brief). For daily rows in a spreadsheet, use [Export an activity report](/engagement/export-activity-report).
