title: "Use Morning Brief"
description: "Schedule the daily Pro Coach Mo brief on iPhone, open it from Home before noon, and check missing widgets or notifications."
---

Morning Brief is Coach Mo's daily recap and tip. Depending on the data available, it can include weather, sleep or recovery, yesterday's rings, today's competitions, streak context, and a tip.

<Note>
  Morning Brief is a Pro feature. It isn't sent during a Pro free trial.
</Note>

## Set it up

1. On iPhone, tap the red **\+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**. Open the conversations drawer, tap **Settings**, then tap **Morning Brief**.
2. Turn on **Daily Morning Brief**.
3. Choose a **Greeting time** between **5:00 AM and 10:00 AM**.
4. Tap **Save**.
5. If you want an alert, check MoveTogether's notification access and [notification preferences](/account/notifications).

The schedule is a preference for the brief; system notification settings separately control whether an alert can reach you.

## Open the brief

Open **Home** and tap the Morning Brief tile (it shows today's date and **Tap to read**), or tap a Morning Brief notification. A dot on the tile means you haven't read today's brief yet.

Each brief is for the morning only. At noon in your home time zone, the tile stops opening and reads **Tomorrow at sunrise**. Before the brief is ready, the tile reads **Waiting on today**.

Morning Brief is different from the [Weekly Mo Report](/engagement/weekly-report). The brief helps you start today; the weekly report reviews the previous completed week.

## Why a widget may be missing

Weather needs location access. Sleep and recovery need a device that records those metrics and shares them into Apple Health or Health Connect. Rings and competition context need synced activity and relevant participation.

A missing widget does not mean its value is zero. Check the source and permissions in [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources). You can use the parts of the brief that have data without expecting every device to supply every widget.

## The brief or alert has not arrived

First confirm your account has Pro (not a trial), then reopen the Morning Brief settings and check that it's on and the time is saved. Then check Home: if the brief is there but no alert arrived, troubleshoot [notifications](/troubleshooting/notification-problems). If it's after noon, today's brief has already closed. If the brief itself is missing, refresh after activity syncs and try again when connectivity is restored.

If the issue continues, tell the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) your selected greeting time, the date, and whether the Home tile or only the notification was missing.
