title: "Use Morning Brief"
description: "Turn on Coach Mo's daily Morning Brief and choose when it arrives."
---

Morning Brief is a personalized daily recap and tip from Coach Mo. Available widgets include weather, sleep or recovery, yesterday's rings, today's competitions and streak setup, and a tip when the relevant data is available.

## Set it up

Open **Settings → Coach Mo → Morning Brief**. Turn on **Daily Morning Brief**, select a **Greeting time** between 5:00 AM and 10:00 AM, and tap **Save**. Allow MoveTogether notifications if you want a notification when it is ready.

Open the Morning Brief from its Home card or a Morning Brief notification. Missing data can make a widget unavailable; check [Data Sources](/getting-started/connect-tracker) and the permissions for that source.
