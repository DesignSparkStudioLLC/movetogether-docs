## Coach, friends, and account help

Open [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo) for fitness coaching and product how-to questions, or learn about [Chat and communities](/social-coach/chat-announcements-mentions) to connect with other people.

For account, billing, sync, privacy, or security problems, [contact Marco support](/troubleshooting/contact-support). The signed-in app support flow includes account context. Product feedback belongs on the feedback board; keep personal account and health details in private support.
