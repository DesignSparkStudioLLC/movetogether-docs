## How syncing works

MoveTogether pulls your daily activity from your connected tracker on a regular schedule:

- **Apple Watch / Apple Health:** updates within minutes of new data appearing in Apple Health.
- **Fitbit, Oura, Polar, Suunto, Coros:** typically within 5–10 minutes.
- **Garmin, Whoop:** can take up to 15 minutes per sync.

## Historical backfill

When you first connect a tracker, MoveTogether pulls **the last 90 days of activity** so your profile and streaks reflect your real history.

## Why might my numbers differ from my tracker app?

A few reasons:
- **Time zone changes** — if you flew across time zones, your tracker and MoveTogether may segment the day differently.
- **Manual workout edits** in your tracker app can take a sync cycle to propagate.
- **Different goal values** — if your tracker app uses a different daily goal than MoveTogether, the percentages will look different even if the raw numbers match.

## Force a refresh

Pull down on the **Home** screen to trigger an immediate sync.
