MoveTogether works with eight major fitness tracking ecosystems. You only need to wear one.

## Supported

| Brand | Connection method |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Apple Watch** | Apple Health |
| **Fitbit** | Fitbit account OAuth |
| **Garmin** | Garmin Connect OAuth |
| **Whoop** | Whoop account OAuth |
| **Oura** | Oura account OAuth |
| **Polar** | Polar Flow OAuth |
| **Suunto** | Suunto account OAuth |
| **Coros** | Coros account OAuth |

## What about [X]?

If your tracker isn't in this list, it's not currently supported. Many brands can export their data to Apple Health on iPhone, which gives MoveTogether a path to read it indirectly — but native support for that brand isn't guaranteed.

## Phone-only (no watch or ring)?

The iPhone itself can populate Move and Steps through Apple Health (it tracks active calories and steps when you carry it). Exercise minutes will be sparse without a wearable.
