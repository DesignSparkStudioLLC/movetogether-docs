---
title: "Streaks"
description: "How daily streaks are tracked and what breaks them."
---

A streak is a run of consecutive days where you closed all three rings. Streaks live on your profile and unlock achievements at key milestones (7, 30, 100, 365 days).

## What counts toward your streak

A day counts if you closed **all three rings** (Move, Exercise, Steps) before midnight in your local time zone.

## What breaks your streak

- Missing any one of the three rings on a given day
- Not syncing your tracker for several days (we can't credit days we never received data for)

## Streak protection

Coming soon. Today, a missed day resets your streak to zero — but we're exploring streak freezes for premium subscribers.

<Note>
  Your streak isn't tied to a specific competition — it tracks across your whole MoveTogether life.
</Note>
