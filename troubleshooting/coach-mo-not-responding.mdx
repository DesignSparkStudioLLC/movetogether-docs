---
title: "Coach Mo not responding"
description: "What to do when Mo is silent or errors out."
---

## Check your message quota

Mo has a monthly limit per plan — see [Message quota](/coach-mo/message-quota). If you've hit it, Mo will show a notice but won't reply to new messages until the next monthly reset.

## Check your internet connection

Mo's replies are generated in the cloud, so an offline phone means no reply. The Coach tab will show a connection error if it can't reach our servers.

## Force-quit and reopen

Sometimes the iOS connection to the chat backend gets stuck. Swipe up to the app switcher, swipe MoveTogether away, and reopen. Your conversation history is preserved.

## Voice on Apple Watch isn't working

- Make sure you're on a supported watch (Series 6+, watchOS 10+).
- Make sure the watch app has microphone permission: **iPhone → Watch app → MoveTogether → Privacy**.
- Make sure your watch has either cellular or Wi-Fi — voice doesn't work offline.

## Mo gave a weird or wrong answer

Mo is an AI, not an oracle. He occasionally hallucinates or misremembers context. If you got bad advice, tell him directly ("that's wrong because…") — he can correct himself in the same conversation. For something that needs a human, tap **Support** in the Coach tab.

## Still stuck?

Tap **Support** in the Coach tab. Support replies are unlimited and don't count against your Mo quota.
