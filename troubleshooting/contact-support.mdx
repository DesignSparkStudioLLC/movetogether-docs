If you can't find the answer in this help center, Mo can help.

## Chat with Mo

Open [movetogetherfitness.com](https://movetogetherfitness.com) and look for the chat bubble in the bottom-right of the page. Mo is available 24/7 and can:

- Answer questions about the app
- Help diagnose sync, competition, or subscription issues
- Take actions on your account, like resetting your tracker connection, restoring a subscription, or escalating to a human

## When you reach out, include

- A description of what's wrong
- Which fitness tracker you use
- When the issue started
- Any error messages you've seen
- Screenshots if you have them

## Response times

Mo responds instantly. For issues that need to escalate to a human:

- **Pro subscribers:** priority response
- **Free users:** response within 24 to 48 hours

## Feedback and feature requests

Start your message to Mo with **"Feedback:"** and it'll go to our product backlog. Every piece is read.
