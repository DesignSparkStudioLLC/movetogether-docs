## Getting help

If you can't find the answer in this help center, we've got you.

Open the **Coach** tab in the MoveTogether app and tap **Support**. This sends your message directly to our team with your account context attached, which helps us resolve issues faster.

## When you reach out, include

- A description of the issue
- Which fitness tracker you're using
- When the issue started
- Any error messages you've seen
- Screenshots if applicable

Your username, iPhone model, and iOS version are attached automatically when you reach out through the app.

## Response times

- **Pro subscribers:** Priority support with faster response times.
- **Free users:** We do our best to respond within 24 to 48 hours.

## Feedback and feature requests

We love hearing from our users. Open the **Coach** tab, tap **Support**, and start your message with "Feedback:" to send us ideas for new features, improvements, or general feedback. Every piece of feedback is read.
