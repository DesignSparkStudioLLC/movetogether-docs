Support is the in-app support chat. Use it for account access, sync, billing, deletion, security, moderation appeals, and anything else you need help with. When you are signed in, the chat is linked to your account, so support can see who you are without you typing account details.

## Open the support chat

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    - Tap the red **\+** button and choose **Help**. This opens the chat directly.
    - Or open **Settings → Resources → Contact Support**.

    If the chat can't open, the app shows the **Help** screen instead. Tap **Message** there to try the chat again, or browse the frequent questions.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    - Tap the **\+** button and choose **Help**.
    - Or open **Settings → Resources → Contact Support**.

    If the chat can't open on your phone, the app starts an email to [support@movetogetherfitness.com](mailto:support@movetogetherfitness.com) instead.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If your Home screen shows a **Help** button at the top, it opens the same chat.

## When support replies

When support answers, the app shows a **Support replied** banner. Tap it to read the message. Until you open the chat, the **Help** row in the **\+** menu shows a count of new replies.

Our AI support agent may answer your message first, and a member of our team can step in at any time. The agent's replies are AI-generated and can be wrong, so ask for a person if something doesn't look right.

## What to include

To help support understand a technical problem, include:

- A description of what happened
- Your tracker and phone, if relevant
- When the issue started
- Any error messages you saw
- Your phone model and OS version
- Screenshots, if they do not contain sensitive information you do not want to share

Don't send passwords, payment-card details, or other secrets in the chat. Only include health details if they are needed to solve the problem.

## Screen sharing

For some problems, support may ask to see your screen. Nothing is shared until you allow it. See [Share your screen with support](/troubleshooting/screen-share).

## Privacy and data requests

For privacy requests, such as asking for access to or deletion of your data, email [**privacy@movetogetherfitness.com**](mailto:privacy@movetogetherfitness.com) or use the support chat. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

## If you can't open the app

Use the chat on [MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact). That chat is not signed in to your MoveTogether account, so include only the details needed to find and help with the issue.

## Feedback and feature requests

Use [Share product feedback](https://movetogetherfitness.com/roadmap) for ideas, bugs, rough edges, and feature requests.

<Note>
  The feedback board is public. Do not include account, health, billing, security, or other sensitive information there. Use the in-app support chat instead.
</Note>
