## My score is not updating

1. Confirm the data source is connected in **Settings → Data Sources**.
2. Let the provider complete its own sync.
3. Open the competition leaderboard and pull down to refresh.
4. Confirm that the competition is active and that the metric is eligible for its scoring method.

Scores are calculated on the server from synced activity. A refresh cannot add data the provider has not yet sent. See [the scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference) for method-specific caps and qualification rules.

## I cannot join

Check that the competition has not ended, the invite or code is current, and any invitation restriction includes you. Free members can join competitions without a two-competition participation cap. If it is an active competition, its displayed dates and rules determine whether activity can be counted.

## I cannot host

Free accounts can host up to two eligible active, upcoming, or draft competitions. Complete or delete an eligible hosted competition to release a slot, or use an available eligible bonus slot. Seasonal and evergreen competitions are excluded. Also check the Fair Play notice and your connection in **Settings → Data Sources**.

## A score or position changed unexpectedly

First, wait for all affected sources to sync and refresh. If the host proposed a scoring change, every participant must accept before it applies; then prior days are rescored and positions can move. One decline leaves the existing scoring in place. Read [changing competition scoring](/competitions/changing-scoring) for the full process.

If the result still appears incorrect after the source has synced, use the in-app support flow and include the competition name, date, scoring method, and the activity value you expected.
