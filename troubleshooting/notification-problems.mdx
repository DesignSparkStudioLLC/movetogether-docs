---
title: "Notification problems"
description: "How to fix MoveTogether push notification issues with in-app categories and iOS or Android system notification settings."
---

## Check in-app notification categories

Open **MoveTogether → Settings → Notifications** and make sure the categories you want are turned on.

For a missing Morning Brief on iPhone, also open Coach Mo settings and tap **Morning Brief**. Turn on **Daily Morning Brief**, choose its greeting time, and tap **Save**. A Morning Brief alert can arrive only when a brief is available.

For a missing Coach reminder, check **Plans & rest days** from the Coach Mo conversations drawer or Coach Mo settings. A confirmed temporary plan or recurring rest day can deliberately change reminder behavior while it is active.

## Check system notification settings

### iOS

1. Open **iPhone Settings → MoveTogether → Notifications**.
2. Make sure **Allow Notifications** is on and select your preferred alert styles.
3. If you use Do Not Disturb, Work, or Sleep Focus, review Focus settings to allow MoveTogether through.
4. Check whether Low Power Mode is delaying delivery.

<Note>
  MoveTogether can use provisional notification delivery on iOS. If you only see quiet notifications in Notification Center, use a notification-related feature in the app to choose full notification access for sounds, banners, and badges.
</Note>

### Android

If notifications are off for MoveTogether in Android settings, **Settings → Notifications** in the app says so and shows an **Open notification settings** button. Tap it and allow notifications for MoveTogether. Android can list MoveTogether notifications by category, so also check the categories you want there.

## Getting too many notifications

Go to **Settings → Notifications** in MoveTogether and turn off the categories you do not want.

For Coach reminders during travel or recovery, add or edit a temporary plan or rest day.
