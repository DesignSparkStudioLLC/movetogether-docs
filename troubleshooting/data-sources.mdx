---
title: "Manage and troubleshoot data sources"
description: "Check connection status, permissions, pauses, and missing data for Apple Health and wearable services."
---

Use **Settings → Data Sources** to see Apple Health and connected wearable services, their status, and the sources contributing to your health metrics.

## Fix a connection

1. Pull to refresh **Data Sources** and check whether the service is connected, paused, or needs access review.
2. For Apple Health, review MoveTogether's access in **iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MoveTogether**. Return to Data Sources to review access or manually backfill when offered.
3. For a paused wearable service, use its reconnect option and complete the provider sign-in flow.
4. If a service offers **Disconnect**, use it before replacing that connection. Some provider connections must be managed in the provider's own app.

Sync timing depends on the source. Do not rely on two sources being automatically deduplicated. Suunto is shown as coming soon and is not connectable.
