## Quick fix checklist

1. Pull down to refresh on any screen showing your fitness data.
2. Force-close and reopen MoveTogether (swipe up from the bottom, find MoveTogether, swipe up to close, then reopen).
3. Open your tracker's companion app and make sure it has synced recently.
4. Check your internet connection. Data sync needs an active connection.

## Apple Watch not syncing

Check Apple Health permissions: open **iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MoveTogether**, and make sure all relevant categories are enabled. Verify Apple Health has recent data by opening the **Health** app and checking **Browse → Activity**. Restart both your iPhone and Apple Watch, then reopen MoveTogether.

## Third-party tracker not syncing

For Google Health / Fitbit, Garmin, WHOOP, Oura, Strava, Polar, and Ultrahuman:

1. Open your tracker's companion app and trigger a manual sync.
2. Check your connection in MoveTogether: go to **Settings → Data Sources** and make sure your tracker shows as connected.
3. If it is paused, use its reconnect option and sign in again. If it offers **Disconnect**, reconnect after disconnecting. This refreshes authorization.

<Note>
  Third-party trackers sync through OpenWearables and can take up to 15 to 30 minutes for new data to appear. That's normal and depends on how often your tracker's companion app syncs with its own servers.
</Note>

## My workouts sync, but my Exercise ring stays empty

This one isn't a sync problem. Your workouts are arriving, they're just not
all counting toward Exercise.

Exercise minutes feed competition scoring, so they're reserved for deliberate
training:

- **Any workout you record counts.** A run, a ride, a strength session, a
  class. If you started it, it counts.
- **Walks count too**, as long as they run at least 15 minutes at a genuine
  walking pace.
- **Movement your tracker detects on its own doesn't count.** Rings and
  watches log short bursts of walking through the day without you starting
  anything. Moving around the house or a few steps between errands isn't
  training, and counting it would let someone out-score a person who actually
  worked out.

<Note>
  That movement isn't thrown away. It still counts toward your **Steps** ring
  and any step-based competition. Only the Exercise ring is affected.
</Note>

If a workout you definitely did is missing, start the activity in your
tracker's own app before you set off, rather than letting it detect the
session afterwards. Your tracker then records your pace and distance, and
those minutes land on your Exercise ring.

Forgot to start it? Most tracker apps let you add or edit an activity after
the fact. That correction syncs to MoveTogether the normal way and counts.

## If nothing works

Make sure you're on the latest version of MoveTogether from the App Store. Try deleting and reinstalling the app (your account data is stored on our servers and will come back when you sign in).

If it's still not working, chat with Mo at [movetogetherfitness.com](https://movetogetherfitness.com). Include your username, which tracker you're using, your iPhone model and iOS version, when the issue started, and any error messages you've seen.
