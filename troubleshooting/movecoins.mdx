---

Find the message you see, then follow the fix. For anything not listed here, use the in-app support chat: tap the red **+** button and choose **Help**.

## I don't see Movecoins at all

Movecoins only appears when:

- Your App Store account country is the **United States**.
- You're on **iPhone**. Movecoins is not on Android yet.
- You have the latest version of MoveTogether.

## Turning Movecoins on

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Movecoins are only available in the US. | Movecoins needs a US App Store account. |
| Movecoins needs the latest Apple Health sync. Try again. | The sync update didn't finish. Tap **Turn on Movecoins** again and let the update complete. |
| Our terms were just updated. Take a look, then turn on Movecoins again. | Read the updated terms, then tap **Turn on Movecoins** again. |
| Update the app to turn on Movecoins. | Update MoveTogether from the App Store. |
| We didn't hear back. Check your connection and try again. | Check your internet connection and try again. |
| Too many tries. Wait a bit and try again. | Wait a while before trying again. |
| Movecoins aren't available right now. | Movecoins is paused. Try again later. |

## The "Get my card" button stays gray

You need to check both **I'm 18 or older** and **I live in the US**, and enter a valid email.

## My balance looks too low

- Only days from when you turned Movecoins on count.
- Steps and workouts typed in by hand don't count.
- Activity can arrive late, so today's coins may show up later.
- Coins expire at the end of the 12th month after you earned them.

See [How you earn Movecoins](/movecoins/earning).

## My gift card didn't arrive

1. Check your spam folder.
2. Check the cash-out in your history on the Rewards tab.
3. Still missing? Contact support through the in-app support chat within 30 days.

## My history says "Couldn't send. Our team is on it."

The card couldn't be sent. If it failed before a card was ordered, your coins were returned automatically, even though the history row still shows the coins. If you're not sure, contact support through the in-app support chat.
