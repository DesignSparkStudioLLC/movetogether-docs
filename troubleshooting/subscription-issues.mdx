description: "How to fix MoveTogether subscription issues — Pro features still locked after subscribing, missing subscription after a charge, and Restore Purchases."
---

## I subscribed but features are still locked

1. Force-close and reopen MoveTogether to trigger a subscription status check.
2. Wait 1 to 2 minutes. There can be a brief delay between the App Store confirming your purchase and the app recognizing it.
3. Check your subscription status in **Settings → Subscription**.
4. Tap **Restore Purchases** in **Settings → Subscription**.
5. Verify the subscription is active on Apple's side: **iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions**.

If you've confirmed the subscription is active but features are still locked, chat with Mo at [movetogetherfitness.com](https://movetogetherfitness.com).

## I was charged but don't see my subscription

- Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used to make the purchase.
- Try the **Restore Purchases** button.

If the issue continues, chat with Mo at [movetogetherfitness.com](https://movetogetherfitness.com) with your Apple ID and a screenshot of the charge.
