---
title: "Manage your profile"
description: "Edit your display name, username, profile photo, and bio from Profile."
---

Open the **Profile** tab and use **Edit Profile** to manage your display name, profile photo, and bio. Tap **Save** after making changes. To change your username, open **Settings → Personal Details → Username**.

Your username is unique and is used for search and profile links. It must be 3–30 characters and can use letters, numbers, and underscores. Username availability is checked before you save. You can change it once every 30 days, and your old handle is held for you for 30 days after a change.

Profile photos must follow the [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation) and can be reviewed by automated moderation. If an image is rejected, choose a different photo without explicit, hateful, dangerous, or otherwise prohibited content.

Your Profile also shows activity, achievements, badges, competitions, streak information, and Invite & Earn rewards. Control profile visibility from **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
