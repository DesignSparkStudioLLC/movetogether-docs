## Editing your profile

Go to the **Profile** tab and tap **Edit Profile**. You can update your:

- **Display Name** — shown on leaderboards and in the activity feed
- **Username** — your unique identifier that friends use to find you
- **Profile Photo** — tap to upload from your camera roll or take a new photo
- **Bio**

Tap **Save** when you're done.

## Profile photo guidelines

Your profile photo must follow our [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation). No inappropriate, offensive, or explicit content. Profile photos are automatically scanned by AI moderation to ensure compliance.

## Your stats and badges

Your Profile tab also displays your achievements, badges (including seasonal event badges), competition history, streak information, and referral link.
