---
title: "Invite & Earn"
description: "Share an invite link, track qualified referrals, earn the Ringleader badge and Pro time, and claim or bank referral rewards."
---

Open **Profile**, then open **Rewards** to find your Invite & Earn link and referral progress. A friend receives 7 days of MoveTogether Pro when they join through your link. Their referral counts toward your rewards after they finish onboarding and log 3 days of real synced activity.

## Rewards

| Qualified referrals | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Ringleader badge |
| 3 | 1 week of Pro |
| 5 | 1 month of Pro |

Referral rewards qualify after a friend joins through your link, completes onboarding, and records 3 active synced days. Joining a competition is not required. The app shows progress and reward status; qualification is verified to protect the program from activity inconsistent with its intent.

## Claiming and banking Pro time

When a tier is ready, tap **Redeem** in **Rewards**. If you already have a plan or free Pro period, the reward can be saved for later and starts automatically when the current plan ends. The Ringleader badge is granted when the first referral completes.

An existing account cannot be credited as a new referral. If a link does not appear or a referral is not progressing, contact Marco through the support option in the app.

| Completed referrals | Reward |
|----------------|--------|
| 1 referral | **Ringleader badge** — an exclusive profile badge |
| 3 referrals | **1 week of Pro** |
| 5 referrals | **1 month of Pro** |

Rewards stack. At 5 completed referrals, you have unlocked all three rewards: the Ringleader badge, 1 week of Pro, and 1 month of Pro.

## Tracking your progress

The Referrals screen shows:

- How many completed referrals you have
- Your progress toward the next tier
- Which rewards are ready to claim
- Which rewards you've already claimed

## When rewards arrive

Claim Pro-time rewards from Invite & Earn. You can bank them while another paid entitlement is active, so they do not replace your current plan.

## Tips

- Your link works in any messaging app — copy it once, share it everywhere.
- Friends who already have an account can't be credited as referrals; the link only counts new sign-ups.
- Your referral progress is private. Friends don't see who invited them in your view of the app.

<Note>
  Trouble with a referral not crediting? Use the in-app support flow or [contact MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact). Do not post account details in public feedback.
</Note>
