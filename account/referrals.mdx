When a friend joins MoveTogether using your referral link, you both get something. The more friends who join, the better the rewards.

## Finding your referral link

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Scroll to **Referrals**.
3. Tap **Share** to copy your link, or send it directly through text, email, or any sharing app.

Your link is unique to you — every friend who signs up through it gets credited to your account.

## Reward tiers

You unlock rewards at three milestones:

| Friends joined | Reward |
|----------------|--------|
| 1 friend | **Founder badge** — an exclusive profile badge that marks you as an early supporter |
| 3 friends | **1 week of Pro** added to your account |
| 5 friends | **1 month of Pro** added to your account |

Rewards stack. Hitting 5 referrals gives you the Founder badge, the 1 week of Pro from tier 2, and the 1 month of Pro from tier 3.

## Tracking your progress

The Referrals screen shows:

- How many friends have joined through your link
- Your progress toward the next tier
- Which rewards are ready to claim
- Which rewards you've already claimed

## When rewards arrive

Rewards are granted automatically when a friend signs up and finishes onboarding using your link. Pro time is added to your subscription on top of any existing subscription you have — it doesn't replace your current plan, it extends it.

## Tips

- Your link works in any messaging app — copy it once, share it everywhere.
- Friends who already have an account can't be credited as referrals; the link only counts new sign-ups.
- Your referral progress is private. Friends don't see who invited them in your view of the app.
