---
title: "Invite & Earn"
description: "Share an invite link, track qualified referrals, and claim or bank referral rewards."
---

Open **Profile**, then open **Rewards** to find your Invite & Earn link and referral progress. Your friend receives 7 days of MoveTogether Pro when they join through the link.

## Rewards

| Qualified referrals | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Ringleader badge |
| 3 | 1 week of Pro |
| 5 | 1 month of Pro |

Referral rewards qualify after a friend joins through your link, completes onboarding, and records 3 active synced days. Joining a competition is not required. The app shows progress and reward status; qualification is verified to protect the program from activity inconsistent with its intent.

## Claiming and banking Pro time

When a tier is ready, tap **Redeem** in **Rewards**. If you already have a plan or free Pro period, the reward can be saved for later and starts automatically when the current plan ends. The Ringleader badge is granted when the first referral completes.

An existing account cannot be credited as a new referral. If a link does not appear or a referral is not progressing, contact Mo through the support option in the app.
