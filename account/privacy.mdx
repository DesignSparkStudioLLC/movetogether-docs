A short, plain-language summary of MoveTogether's data practices. The full legal version is in our [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy).

## What MoveTogether reads from your tracker

Only what's needed to score competitions:
- Daily active calories
- Daily exercise minutes
- Daily step count
- Workout type and duration (for streak calculations)

We **do not** read or store:
- Heart rate
- Sleep data
- Weight, body composition, or BMI (unless you log it manually)
- GPS routes
- Workout audio or photos

## What competition participants can see about you

Only your competition-relevant data:
- Your name and avatar
- Your daily ring closures and points
- Your active calorie total (for leaderboard tiebreakers)

Nothing else from your account is exposed to other participants.

## Where your data lives

- **Your activity data** is stored encrypted on our servers (Supabase, hosted in the US).
- **Your Coach Mo conversations** are stored to give Mo context for future chats.
- **Your subscription status** is verified through Apple's App Store servers.
