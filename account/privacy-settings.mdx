Open **Settings → Privacy Settings** to change your privacy choices.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Each change saves as soon as you make it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Make your changes, then tap **Save changes** at the bottom (or **Discard** to undo them).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Profile

**Profile visibility** sets who can see your profile:

- **Public:** anyone on MoveTogether
- **Friends only:** only people on your friends list
- **Private:** only you

The other switches in this section:

- **Show real name on leaderboards:** turn off to show your username instead of your name.
- **Allow others to find me by phone**
- **Allow others to find me by email**
- **Show on public leaderboards:** turn off to keep your rank off public leaderboards.

## Activity

- **Show my activity in friends' feeds:** turn off to stop sharing your activities in your friends' feeds.
- **Show detailed stats to competitors:** turn off so competitors see your rank but not your detailed stats.

These choices change what's shared from now on. They don't remove anything already shared, or records needed to run a competition.

## Health metrics

Choose which metrics other people can see: **Calories**, **Exercise**, **Distance**, **Steps**, and **Workouts**. Each one turns on or off by itself.

Hiding a metric only hides it from your profile and from feeds. Hidden metrics still count toward your competitions.

## Social

- **Who can send friend requests:** **Everyone**, **Friends of friends**, or **No one**.
- **Who can invite me to competitions:** **Everyone**, **Friends only**, or **No one**.

## AI Coach

**Personalize Coach with my fitness data:** when this is off, Coach Mo replies without using your activity, competitions, or goals to tailor its answers.

## Data & analytics

**Help improve MoveTogether:** turn off to stop sharing product analytics from now on. When it's on, analytics can include usage events, masked session replay, and diagnostics tied to your account ID. This data is not sold.

**Ad measurement** (iPhone only): helps us understand which ads lead to installs. If you haven't decided yet, tapping it shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency request. After you've answered, tapping it opens MoveTogether in iPhone Settings, where you can change your answer. The row shows **Not requested**, **Allowed**, **Not allowed**, or **Restricted**. Health and fitness data is never used for advertising. See [Ads and measurement](/account/ads-and-measurement) for what is shared if you allow it.

**Blocked users:** see and manage the people you've blocked.

**Download my data** (iPhone only): we email you a link to download a copy of your account data. The link expires after 7 days. See [Your data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy#downloading-your-data).

**Delete account:** the last row on the screen. Deleting your account is permanent. See [Your data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy#deleting-your-data) first.
