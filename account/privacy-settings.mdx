## Accessing privacy settings

Go to **Settings → Privacy** to manage all of your privacy controls.

## Profile visibility

**Profile visibility** — Choose who can see your profile:
- **Public** — anyone on MoveTogether
- **Friends Only** — only people on your friends list
- **Private** — only you

You can also toggle **Show real name on leaderboards** off to display your username instead, and control whether others can find you by email.

## Activity sharing

Toggle **Show my activity in friends' feeds** off to hide your activity feed posts from friends. You can also control whether your rank appears on public leaderboards and whether competitors can see your detailed stats or just your rank.

## Health data visibility

Control which fitness metrics competition participants can see: steps, calories burned, active minutes, distance, and workouts. Each can be toggled on or off independently.

## Social controls

- **Who can send friend requests** — Choose from Everyone, Friends of Friends, or No One.
- **Who can invite me to competitions** — Choose from Everyone, Friends Only, or No One.

You can also view and manage your blocked users list here.

## Analytics opt-out

Toggle **Help improve MoveTogether with anonymous analytics** off if you'd prefer not to share anonymous usage data. This data is used solely to improve the app and is never sold.
