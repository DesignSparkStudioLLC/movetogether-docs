---
title: "Delete your account"
description: "Permanently remove your MoveTogether account and data."
---

You can delete your MoveTogether account at any time. This is **permanent** — once it's done, your activity history, achievements, and Coach Mo conversations are gone and can't be recovered.

## How to delete

1. Open the MoveTogether app.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**.
3. Confirm the prompt.
4. Your account is queued for deletion and removed within 30 days.

## What happens to your data

- Your profile, activity history, achievements, and Coach Mo conversations are deleted from our servers.
- Any competitions you were a participant in keep the leaderboard intact, but your name is replaced with "Deleted user."
- Backups are purged on a rolling 30-day cycle.

## What's *not* deleted by this

- **Your App Store subscription.** Cancel it separately through **Settings → Subscriptions** on your device, or it will keep renewing.
- **Your tracker account.** Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin, etc. are unaffected.

## Before you delete

If you're just tired of notifications or want to take a break, you can:
- Turn off all push notifications in **Settings → Notifications**
- Leave any active competitions
- Cancel your subscription but keep your account on Starter

<Note>
  Deletion is final — there's no "undo" once the 30-day window passes. If you're unsure, [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) first.
</Note>
