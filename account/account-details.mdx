---

Open **Settings → Personal Details** to edit your account details. (To reach Settings, tap your photo at the top right of Home, then the gear.)

## What you can edit

**Photo:** tap your photo at the top of the screen to pick a new one from your photo library. On Android you can also tap **Remove photo**.

**Name:** your display name. Required.

**Username:** the unique handle friends use to find you. Tapping it opens a separate screen. You can change it once every 30 days, and your old username stays reserved for you for 30 days after a change. See [Manage your profile](/account/profile) for the username rules.

**Bio:** a short public bio, up to 160 characters. If it's longer, the app won't save it and asks you to shorten it.

**Featured badge:** pick one of your earned badges to show near the top of your profile, or choose **Automatic**.

**Sex:** Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say. Your choice saves as soon as you pick it.

**Birthday:** on iPhone, tap the row to set it. On Android it's shown but can't be changed here.

**Phone:** on iPhone, tap **Add** to add and verify a number, or tap a saved number to change it. The number is masked on screen. To remove it, press and hold the row and tap **Remove phone**. On Android the phone row is shown but can't be changed.

## What you can see but not change

**Age:** worked out from your birthday. Older accounts that have an age but no birthday show the saved age.

**Email:** the email on your account.

**Sign-in method:** how you sign in, such as Apple or Google.

## Saving changes

**Save** at the top right saves your name, bio, and featured badge. It turns on once one of them has changed.

## Log out and Delete account

**Log out** is in the **Account** section at the bottom of the main **Settings** screen. You confirm before you're signed out.

**Delete account** is the last row of **Settings → Privacy Settings**. Deleting an account is permanent and can't be undone, so read [Your data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy#deleting-your-data) first.

The first-run setup can't be replayed as a single flow. Revisit data sources, notifications, privacy, and Coach Mo preferences from their own settings instead.

## Where this info shows up

- Your **display name** and **photo** appear on leaderboards and in the activity feed (you can control this in [Privacy Settings](/account/privacy-settings))
- Your **username** lets friends find you
- Your **featured badge** shows on your profile
- **Sex, birthday, and age** help personalize defaults (like your starting Move goal) and aren't shown to other users
- **Phone** is only used to help friends find you through contacts, if you allow it
