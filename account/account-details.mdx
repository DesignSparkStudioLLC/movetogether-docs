---
title: "Editing your account details"
description: "How to edit your name, bio, demographic info, phone number, featured badge, and sign out or replay onboarding."
---

Open **Settings → Account** for everything related to your account info beyond your basic profile.

## What you can edit

**Avatar** — tap to change your profile photo. Pull from your camera roll or take a new one.

**Name** — your display name. Required, can't be empty.

**Bio** — up to 160 characters. A character counter shows how much room you have left. It turns orange near the limit and red if you exceed it. Save is disabled when over the limit.

**Featured badge** — pick one of your earned badges to feature on your profile. Tap to open the badge picker and choose any badge you've unlocked.

**Sex** — Male / Female / Non-binary / Prefer not to say.

**Birthday** — date picker (uses UTC to avoid time-zone surprises). Your age is calculated automatically.

**Age** — if your birthday isn't set, you can enter your age directly.

**Phone number** — add, change, or remove. Phone numbers are masked (asterisks) in the UI. Long-press the phone row to get a quick remove option without opening the editor.

## What you can see but not change

**Email** — the email associated with your account, shown as read-only.

**Sign-in method** — whether you signed up with Apple, Google, or email. Shown as read-only.

## Saving changes

The **Save** button in the top-right is only active when something's actually changed. Required fields (like name) must be valid. Tap Save to confirm.

## Account actions

**Log out** — at the bottom, a destructive button that signs you out of MoveTogether. You'll see a confirmation dialog first. You can sign back in anytime from the welcome screen.

**Replay onboarding** — runs the first-launch onboarding flow again (the 8 setup screens). Useful for re-doing your goals, switching trackers, or just retracing the setup. Your existing data stays intact.

**Delete account** — navigates to the delete-account flow. Permanent and not undoable. See [Your data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy#deleting-your-data) before doing this.

## Where this info shows up

- Your **display name** and **avatar** appear on leaderboards and in the activity feed (controllable via [Privacy settings](/account/privacy-settings))
- Your **username** lets friends find you
- Your **featured badge** shows on your profile under your name
- **Sex, birthday, and age** help personalize defaults (like your starting Move goal) and aren't shown to other users
- **Phone** is only used to help friends find you via contacts, if you opt in
