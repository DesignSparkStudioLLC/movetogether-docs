MoveTogether uses different service providers for Coach Mo text, Coach voice, the support chat, chat, communities, feeds, and moderation. Each path sends the context needed to operate that feature.

Coach Mo uses relevant fitness context, goals, profile information, and chat messages to provide coaching and reports. Voice features process your spoken audio to transcribe what you say and generate a response.

## Providers and data

- **Anthropic** processes Coach Mo text coaching, insights, and reports using the relevant fitness context, messages, and report data needed for the feature. Direct names are replaced with placeholders, and Coach text prompts do not include your MoveTogether user ID. Anthropic's Claude also writes the replies of our AI support agent.
- **ElevenLabs** powers Coach Mo voice, where available. It receives spoken audio, messages, relevant fitness context, your first name, and your MoveTogether user ID for that feature.
- **Chatwoot** runs the in-app support chat and support email. Our AI support agent, run by **Skydive** and powered by Anthropic's Claude, may answer first, and a member of our team can take over.
- **Stream** powers chat, communities, feeds, and moderation. It receives the identity and content needed to provide those features. It also carries the live video when you share your screen with support.

These providers process information on our behalf to provide these features. Anthropic and ElevenLabs do not use your coaching data to train their own models. See the [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy) for the published provider and retention disclosures.

We do not use health and fitness data for advertising. Voice is not used to create a voiceprint or to identify or authenticate you.

## Your controls

- Delete Coach Mo conversations and Files from Coach Mo settings.
- Turn off **Personalize Coach with my fitness data** in **Settings → Privacy Settings** if you want Coach Mo to reply without using your activity, competitions, or goals.
- Manage activity and social sharing in **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
- Delete your account from **Settings → Privacy Settings** to request removal of associated account data.

For the complete provider list, retention details, and legal rights, read the [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy).

## Coach Mo text

Coach Mo text prompts replace direct names with placeholders before being sent to Anthropic. Your MoveTogether user ID is not included in the prompt.

| Type of info | What Anthropic actually sees |
|---|---|
| Direct names | Placeholders such as `[USER_NAME]` or `[COMPETITOR_1]` |
| Your MoveTogether user ID | Not included in the prompt |
| Your activity numbers (calories, steps, rings, minutes) | Real numbers |
| Your competition stats and standings | Real numbers |

When personalization is enabled and the data is available, Coach Mo may also receive relevant context about workouts, sleep and recovery, goals, streaks, badges, competitions, weekly history, and rivalries.

## Why your name still appears when Coach Mo replies

The app can replace supported placeholders with familiar names when it displays the response. Anthropic produces the response from the placeholder-redacted prompt.

## Coach Mo on Apple Intelligence (Pro, iPhone)

With Pro on iOS 27, and an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, you can turn on **Use Apple Intelligence** in Coach Mo's settings. Coach Mo's normal replies then run privately on Apple Intelligence when your device supports it. Roast mode, game plans, and rich cards still use the standard engine described above.

The toggle only appears when your plan and iPhone can use it. It is off until you turn it on.

## Coach voice

Coach voice uses ElevenLabs to process spoken audio, relevant fitness context, your first name, and your MoveTogether user ID. Audio is used only to generate a response. MoveTogether keeps transcripts of your voice conversations until you delete your Coach Mo history or your account.

## Support chat and screen sharing

The in-app support chat runs on Chatwoot. When you chat while signed in, Chatwoot receives your MoveTogether user ID, email address, first name, an account verification code, your subscription tier, time zone, and account sign-up date, so support can recognize your account. Our AI support agent may reply first. Its replies are AI-generated and may be wrong, so ask for a person if something doesn't look right. Support conversations are also shared in our internal Slack workspace so our team can follow and reply.

If support asks to see your screen and you allow it, a live video of MoveTogether goes to that support team member through Stream. Your chats are hidden while you share, and nothing is recorded. See [Share your screen with support](/troubleshooting/screen-share).

Avoid sharing passwords, payment-card details, or other secrets in voice or support conversations, and only include health details in a support chat if they are needed to solve the problem.

## Content moderation

Stream provides MoveTogether's chat, communities, feeds, and moderation infrastructure. It receives the identity and content needed to deliver those features, associate content with the right account, and enforce community standards.

## What this means for you

- Anthropic text prompts use placeholders for direct names and omit the MoveTogether user ID.
- On iPhone with Pro, you can choose to run Coach Mo's normal replies on Apple Intelligence.
- ElevenLabs supports Coach voice.
- Chatwoot runs the support chat, and an AI support agent may reply before a person does.
- Stream receives the identity and content needed for its social and moderation features, and carries screen sharing with support.
- You can control Coach Mo personalization and opted-in analytics from **Settings → Privacy Settings**.

For the full list of third-party services and what each one does, see [Your data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy).
