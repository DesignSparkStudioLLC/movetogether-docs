You can request a copy of all data MoveTogether stores about you.

## How to request an export

1. Open the MoveTogether app.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Export my data**.
3. Confirm — you'll get an email with a download link within 48 hours.

## What's in the export

- Your profile information
- Your activity history (every day MoveTogether has recorded)
- Every competition you've participated in
- Your unlocked achievements
- Your Coach Mo conversation history
- Your goal history (every change to your Move / Exercise / Steps targets)

The export is delivered as a ZIP file containing JSON and CSV files.

## Why JSON and CSV?

CSV files open directly in Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets. JSON files are for developers or for importing into another tool.

<Note>
  The export covers data MoveTogether stores. It does **not** include raw data from your tracker (Apple Health, Fitbit, etc.) — for that, use your tracker's own export tool.
</Note>
