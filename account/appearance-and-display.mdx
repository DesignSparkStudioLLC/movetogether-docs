You can customize how MoveTogether looks and which units it uses for activity data.

## Appearance: Light, Dark, or System

Go to **Settings → Appearance**. Pick one of three:

- **System** — follows your iPhone's light/dark mode automatically
- **Light** — always light mode, regardless of iPhone setting
- **Dark** — always dark mode, regardless of iPhone setting

Each option shows a preview card with a stylized version of the app in that mode. Tap to select. Your choice is saved across app launches.

A subtle haptic confirms your selection.

## Units of measurement

Go to **Settings → Customization → Units**. Pick one of two:

- **Metric** — kilometers, kilograms, centimeters
- **Imperial** — miles, pounds, feet and inches

This affects how distance, weight, and height are displayed throughout the app.

**Steps and minutes are unitless** — they don't change based on this setting.

The default matches your iPhone's region settings. You can override it at any time.

## Where appearance settings don't reach

A few things follow your iPhone settings regardless of MoveTogether's appearance choice:

- **System icons and accessibility text size** (Dynamic Type) — set in iPhone Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size
- **Reduce Motion** — set in iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Motion. MoveTogether respects this and disables animations when it's on.
- **Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast** — iPhone accessibility settings that affect MoveTogether visuals automatically

See [Apple's accessibility settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-accessibility-features-iph3e2e4367/ios) for the full list.
