You can customize how MoveTogether looks and which units it uses for activity data.

## Appearance: Light, Dark, or System

Go to **Settings → Appearance**. Pick one of three:

- **System**: matches your phone's light or dark setting.
- **Light**: always light mode.
- **Dark**: always dark mode.

Your choice is saved across app launches.

## Unit of Measurement

Go to **Settings → Customization → Unit of Measurement**. Pick one of two:

- **Metric**: kilometers, kilograms, centimeters
- **Imperial**: miles, pounds, feet and inches

This affects how distance, weight, and height are displayed throughout the app. Steps and minutes are unitless, so they do not change with this setting.

The default matches your phone's region settings. You can override it at any time.

## Accessibility and display settings on iPhone

Dynamic Type and Apple accessibility settings remain controlled in iPhone Settings:

- **Text size**: iPhone Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size
- **Reduce Motion**: iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Motion
- **Reduce Transparency** and **Increase Contrast**: iPhone accessibility settings that can affect MoveTogether visuals

See [Apple's accessibility settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-accessibility-features-iph3e2e4367/ios) for the full list.
