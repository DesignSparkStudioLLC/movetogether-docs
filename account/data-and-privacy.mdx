## What data does MoveTogether collect?

**Account information.** Your name, email address, username, and profile photo — provided by you when you create an account.

**Fitness data.** Steps, active calories, exercise minutes, and workout information — synced from your connected fitness tracker.

**Competition data.** Your competition scores, rankings, and participation history.

**Usage data.** Anonymous analytics about how you use the app, used to improve MoveTogether. You can opt out in **Settings → Privacy**.

## How your fitness data is handled

MoveTogether stores your fitness data on our secure servers to power the app's core features — leaderboards, competitions, activity feeds, achievements, and more. For Apple Watch users, data is read from Apple Health on your device and sent to our servers. For other trackers, data is synced from the tracker provider's servers via secure OAuth connections through our integration partner, OpenWearables.

Competition scores are calculated server-side to ensure security and fairness. Your health data stays in Apple Health (or your tracker's platform) as the original source of truth. MoveTogether reads it — we never modify it.

## Who can see your data?

Competition participants can see your competition-related stats on the leaderboard, subject to your privacy settings. Friends can see your activity in the social feed, subject to your privacy settings. MoveTogether's systems process your data to calculate competition scores and generate achievements. We never sell your personal information or share your raw health data with third parties.

## Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. All connections between your device and our servers are encrypted. Competition scores are calculated server-side to prevent manipulation. Our security model ensures that your data is only accessible through authenticated, validated server requests.

## Downloading your data

You can request a copy of all the data MoveTogether has about you. Go to **Settings → Privacy → Download My Data**, tap the button to request an export, and you'll receive an email with a download link when your export is ready (typically within 24–48 hours).

## Deleting your data

To delete your account and all associated data, go to **Settings → Privacy → Delete My Account** (or **Settings → Account → Delete Account**), confirm your identity if asked, and confirm your choice. This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All of your data will be permanently deleted.
