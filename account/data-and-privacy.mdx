MoveTogether uses your account, activity, fitness, and social information to provide rings, competitions, leaderboards, coaching, and related features. Health and fitness data is sensitive: we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

## What MoveTogether uses

- **Account information:** your name, email address, username, profile photo, and the account settings you choose.
- **Fitness information:** activity totals, workouts, goals, and other health metrics you allow MoveTogether to read from Apple Health or Health Connect.
- **Competition and social information:** scores, rankings, participation history, friendships, posts, and messages needed for the features you use.
- **Product diagnostics:** app events, masked session replays, crash details, and performance information when you opt in to product analytics.

## Sources, sharing, and providers

| Area | How it is used or shared |
| --- | --- |
| Health data | Apple Health (iPhone) and Health Connect (Android) provide the data you authorize. MoveTogether stores the copies needed for scores, leaderboards, activity history, badges, and coaching; it does not write changes back to the original source. |
| Competitions and social features | Competition participants can see the leaderboard information and competition details you share. Friends can see activity you share in social features. |
| Product analytics | When you turn on **Help improve MoveTogether**, PostHog can receive your persistent account UUID, usage events, masked session replay, and crash or performance diagnostics. |
| Social infrastructure | Stream receives the identity and content needed for chat, communities, feeds, and moderation. It also carries the live video when you [share your screen with support](/troubleshooting/screen-share), which is not recorded. |
| Coaching | Anthropic receives Coach Mo text context with direct names replaced by placeholders and without your MoveTogether user ID. ElevenLabs processes Coach Mo voice. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data). |
| Support | The in-app support chat runs on Chatwoot. When you chat while signed in, Chatwoot receives your MoveTogether user ID, email address, first name, an account verification code, subscription tier, time zone, and account sign-up date so support can recognize your account. Our AI support agent, run by Skydive and powered by Anthropic's Claude, may answer first, and a member of our team can take over. |

MoveTogether and its providers process the information needed to operate these features. We do not sell personal or health data.

## Your controls

Open **Settings → Privacy Settings** to manage sharing, friend-request controls, product analytics, blocked users, and account deletion.

- **Export (iPhone):** tap **Download my data** to request a copy of the data associated with your account. The app emails a download link when the export is ready. This account export is a JSON download; it is separate from an [activity report CSV](/engagement/export-activity-report).
- **Delete:** tap **Delete account** to request permanent deletion of your account and associated data. Review the confirmation carefully because this cannot be undone.
- **Coach Mo:** delete individual conversations, all conversations, or Files from Coach Mo settings without deleting the rest of your account.
- **Ad tracking (iPhone):** you can request or manage advertising tracking permission in Privacy Settings, and change it later in iPhone Settings.

Your data is stored using industry-standard encryption. All connections between your device and our servers are encrypted. Competition scores are calculated server-side to prevent manipulation. Our security model means your data is only accessible through authenticated, validated server requests.

## Deleting your data

To delete your account and associated data, go to **Settings → Privacy Settings → Delete account** and follow the confirmation steps. This is permanent and cannot be undone. MoveTogether deletes or de-identifies the account data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or needed for fraud prevention, abuse prevention, or dispute resolution.

Revoking a health permission or disconnecting a source stops future reads covered by that permission or connection. It does not erase copies already stored in your MoveTogether account. Delete the account to request deletion of stored account data.

## Privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests, such as access to or deletion of your data, email [**privacy@movetogetherfitness.com**](mailto:privacy@movetogetherfitness.com) or contact us through the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support). We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
