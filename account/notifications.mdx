Go to **Settings → Notifications** in MoveTogether to turn each category on or off. Your phone's own notification permission is separate: if it's off, no MoveTogether notification arrives, whatever these switches say.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Each switch saves as soon as you flip it. MoveTogether can use iPhone's provisional (quiet) notification delivery before you choose full notification access.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Make your changes, then tap **Save changes** at the bottom (or **Discard** to undo them). MoveTogether follows Android's notification permission and doesn't use iPhone's provisional delivery.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## All notification categories

The sections appear in this order. Some are shown only to some plans on iPhone, as noted. On Android, every section is shown to everyone.

### Daily Reminders

- **Good morning:** a morning nudge for your day ahead.
- **Good night:** an evening reminder.

Morning Brief is not in this list. On iPhone, turn it on or off and set its time in **Settings → Customization → Coach Mo → Morning Brief**. See [Morning Brief](/engagement/morning-brief).

### Mo Automations

Mo watches for meaningful changes and sends one useful next move, within your quiet hours. While it's off, you'll see **Turn on Mo Automations** (up to four personalized updates a week). Once it's on, you can turn each kind on or off:

- **Plan updates**
- **Competition edge**
- **Streak rescue**
- **Recovery adjustments**

On iPhone, you can also set **Quiet hours** here. On iPhone, this section is shown only on Pro.

### How much Mo swears

Pick **Clean**, **Classic**, or **Unhinged**. This applies to Mo's notifications, chat, and voice, and it's the same setting as the one in Coach Mo settings. If you haven't picked, Mo uses **Classic**, which swears. The swearing levels need you to be 18 or older; under 18, only **Clean** is available and the setting shows **Available at 18+**. This section is shown on every plan.

### Competition Updates

Standings, results, and invites for your active competitions. Has a **Push notifications** switch and an **Email** switch.

### Competition Lifecycle

Starting alerts, countdown reminders, last-call syncs, and final results.

- **Lifecycle alerts**
- **Position changes:** when your place on a leaderboard changes

### Friend Activity

When friends join competitions, hit milestones, or send you requests. Has a **Push notifications** switch and an **Email** switch. On iPhone, shown only on Pro.

### Direct Messages

When a friend sends you a direct message. On iPhone, shown only on Pro.

### Group Chat

When someone sends a message in your competition group chat. On iPhone, shown only on Pro.

### Achievements & Milestones

New badges and personal records. On iPhone, shown only on Pro.

### Coach Mo

Motivational tips and personalized insights from Coach Mo. On iPhone, shown only on Pro.

### Account & Security

Important updates about your account, subscription, and security. Has a **Push notifications** switch and an **Email** switch.

### Promotions & Events

Seasonal events, limited drops, and other marketing announcements. Turning this off doesn't affect functional competition updates.

## Mentions and MoveTogether Updates

There's no separate switch for @mentions or for **MoveTogether Updates** announcements. You'll find your mentions in the app: on iPhone in **Social → Chats → Mentions**, and on Android in **Chat → Notifications**. See [Use Chat, Updates, and mentions](/social-coach/chat-announcements-mentions).

## System notification settings

### iPhone

Tap **Open system notification settings** at the bottom of the Notifications screen, or open **iPhone Settings → MoveTogether → Notifications**. Turning off **Allow Notifications** silences MoveTogether even when its categories are on.

Focus modes such as Do Not Disturb, Work, or Sleep can also silence MoveTogether. Check your Focus settings if you want MoveTogether to come through.

A confirmed rest day or temporary Coach plan can change Coach reminder behavior without changing your phone's permission or these switches.

### Android

Tap **Open notification settings** on the Notifications screen to open MoveTogether's notification settings in Android. If notifications are off there, the Notifications screen tells you, and the switches have no effect until you turn them back on.
