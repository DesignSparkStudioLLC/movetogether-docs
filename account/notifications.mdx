## How notifications work

MoveTogether uses **provisional notification delivery** by default — this means you'll receive quiet notifications in your Notification Center without being prompted for permission on first launch. If you want prominent notifications (sounds, banners, badges), you'll be prompted to enable full notification access when you tap a notification-related feature in the app.

## Managing notifications

Go to **Settings → Notifications** to toggle each notification category on or off.

- **Competition updates** — Competition starting soon, competition ended/results, leaderboard position changes, new participant joined.
- **Friend activity** — Friend closed their rings, completed a workout, earned an achievement, or won a competition.
- **Achievement alerts** — You earned a new achievement, new streak milestone reached.
- **Coach Mo** (Pro plan) — Proactive coaching nudges, weekly Mo Report ready, roast card delivery.
- **Direct messages** — New direct messages from friends.
- **Seasonal events** — New seasonal event launches and event reminders.
- **Account & system** — Friend request received, competition invitation received, subscription reminders.

## Disabling all notifications

To turn off all MoveTogether notifications at the system level, open the **Settings** app on your iPhone (not MoveTogether), scroll down and tap **MoveTogether**, tap **Notifications**, and toggle **Allow Notifications** off. This overrides all in-app notification settings.
