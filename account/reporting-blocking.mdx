## Reporting a person

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open the person's profile.
    2. Tap the **More** menu (the circle with three dots) at the top.
    3. Tap **Report**.
    4. Under **What's the issue?**, pick the reason.
    5. Under **What happened?**, describe what happened. A short description is required so the team can investigate.
    6. Tap **Submit report**.

    The reasons are **Inappropriate content**, **Harassment**, **Spam**, **Fake profile**, **Bullying**, **Hate speech**, **Violence**, **Impersonation**, **Explicit content**, **Misinformation**, and **Other**. For a profile photo, pick **Inappropriate content** or **Explicit content** and say it's the photo.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open the person's profile.
    2. Tap **Report**.
    3. Under **What's happening?**, pick the reason.
    4. Add details in **Anything else? (optional)** if you want.
    5. Tap **Send report**.

    The reasons are **Harassing me**, **Bullying**, **Hate speech**, **Explicit content**, **Inappropriate content**, **Violence or threats**, **Spam**, **Fake profile**, **Pretending to be someone**, **Misinformation**, and **Something else**. For a profile photo, pick **Inappropriate content** or **Explicit content** and say it's the photo.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Reporting a chat message (iPhone)

1. Press and hold the message.
2. Tap **Report**.

You can't report your own messages.

## Reporting a competition (iPhone)

Open the competition, open its menu, and tap **Report competition**. Pick the reason, describe what happened, and tap **Submit report**. The competition's creator doesn't see this option.

You can also tell us about a problem in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).

## What happens after you report

All reports are reviewed by our team. We may not be able to tell you what action was taken, for privacy reasons. If a violation is confirmed, the appropriate enforcement action is taken.

## Blocking a user

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Open the person's profile, tap the **More** menu, and tap **Block**. Confirm with **Block**. They won't be able to see your profile or message you.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Open the person's profile, tap **Block**, then tap **Block** again to confirm. They won't see your activity, reach you in chat, or be able to add you back.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Blocking removes the friendship between you and deletes your direct-message conversation with them for both of you. Blocking is immediate, and the other user isn't notified.

## Managing blocked users

Go to **Settings → Privacy Settings → Blocked users** to view and unblock people. Unblocking doesn't add them back as a friend.

## Blocking vs. reporting

Blocking is a personal tool that prevents interaction. Reporting tells our team to investigate and protect the community. For the best outcome, do both: block to protect yourself right away, and report so our team can address the behavior for everyone.
