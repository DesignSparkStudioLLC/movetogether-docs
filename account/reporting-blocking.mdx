## How to report a user or content

**Reporting a user's profile:**
1. Tap the user's profile photo or name to open their profile.
2. Tap the more options menu (three dots).
3. Select **Report**.
4. Choose the reason for your report.
5. Add any additional details and tap **Submit**.

**Reporting a message in chat:**
1. Tap and hold the message you want to report.
2. Select **Report Message**.
3. Choose the reason and submit.

**Reporting a profile photo:**
1. Tap the user's profile, tap the three dots menu.
2. Select **Report**, choose **Inappropriate profile photo** as the reason.

You can also report concerns directly by opening the **Coach** tab and tapping **Support**.

## What happens after you report

All reports are reviewed by our moderation team. Your report is **confidential** — the other user will not be told who reported them. If a violation is confirmed, the appropriate enforcement action is taken.

## How to block a user

Tap the user's profile, tap the three dots menu, and select **Block User**. Confirm your choice. When you block someone, they cannot send you friend requests, invite you to competitions, see your profile or content, or message you. They will not appear in your feeds, leaderboards, or search results. Blocking is immediate and the other user is not notified.

## Managing blocked users

Go to **Settings → Privacy → Blocked Users** to view and unblock users. Unblocking does not automatically re-add them as a friend.

## Blocking vs. reporting

**Blocking** is a personal tool that prevents interaction. **Reporting** notifies our moderation team to investigate and protect the entire community. For the best outcome, we recommend both: block to protect yourself immediately, and report so our team can address the behavior for everyone.
