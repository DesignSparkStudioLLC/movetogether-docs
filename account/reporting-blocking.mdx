## Reporting a user or content

**Reporting a user's profile:**
1. Tap the user's profile photo or name to open their profile.
2. Tap the more options menu (three dots).
3. Select **Report**.
4. Pick the reason.
5. Add any details and tap **Submit**.

**Reporting a message in chat:**
1. Tap and hold the message you want to report.
2. Select **Report Message**.
3. Pick the reason and submit.

**Reporting a profile photo:**
1. Tap the user's profile, tap the three dots menu.
2. Select **Report**, pick **Inappropriate profile photo** as the reason.

You can also report concerns directly by chatting with Marco at [movetogetherfitness.com](https://movetogetherfitness.com).

## What happens after you report

All reports are reviewed by our moderation team. Your report is **confidential**. The other user won't be told who reported them. If a violation is confirmed, the appropriate enforcement action is taken.

## Blocking a user

Tap the user's profile, tap the three dots menu, and select **Block User**. Confirm. When you block someone, they can't send you friend requests, invite you to competitions, see your profile or content, or message you. They won't appear in your feeds, leaderboards, or search results. Blocking is immediate and the other user isn't notified.

## Managing blocked users

Go to **Settings → Privacy Settings → Blocked users** to view and unblock users. Unblocking doesn't automatically re-add them as a friend.

## Blocking vs. reporting

Blocking is a personal tool that prevents interaction. Reporting tells our moderation team to investigate and protect the community. For the best outcome, do both: block to protect yourself immediately, and report so our team can address the behavior for everyone.
