The MoveTogether Apple Watch app puts your rings, competitions, friends, and events on your wrist. Install it from the Watch app on your paired iPhone if it isn't already there.

## Signing in

The Watch app signs in with your iPhone session, so there's no separate login. The first time you open it, give it a few seconds to sync.

- If it shows **Install MoveTogether**, install MoveTogether on your paired iPhone, then open it and sign in.
- If it shows **Sign in on your iPhone**, open MoveTogether on your iPhone and sign in. The watch follows automatically.

## The four pages

The app opens on **Today**. Turn the Digital Crown or swipe up and down to move between the four pages:

1. **Today:** a banner when a seasonal event is live, your Move, Exercise, and Steps rings with progress toward each goal, your weekly streak, and **Today's challenges** with progress bars.
2. **Competitions:** your active competitions. Tap one for its leaderboard, with the top 3 on a podium, everyone else ranked below, and your row highlighted.
3. **Friends:** today's leaderboard with your friends, with the top 3 on a podium and your row highlighted.
4. **Events:** limited-time events grouped as **Live now**, **Coming up**, and **Wrapped**.

The microphone button at the top left of every page opens Coach Mo.

If the Today page asks you to **Connect Health**, tap **Continue** and allow activity and steps so your rings can show.

## Talking to Coach Mo on Watch

Tap the microphone button at the top left of any page.

1. You'll see a greeting and a **Chat with Coach Mo** button.
2. After a brief **Connecting…** step, the conversation is live. Mo's latest reply shows as text you can scroll, with a red button at the bottom to end the call.
3. When the conversation ends, you can tap **Talk again** or **Close**.

Coach Mo voice on Apple Watch requires Pro. Without Pro, the watch shows **Pro feature** and asks you to upgrade in the iPhone app. If you've used Coach Mo's weekly allowance, the watch shows **Coach Mo is resting** until it resets.

For more on how Mo works, see [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo) and [What makes Coach Mo different](/social-coach/what-makes-mo-different).

## Streak detail

Tap the streak card on the Today page for the full breakdown:

- **Your streak:** the number of qualifying weeks in a row, and the weeks to your next milestone
- **Last 30 days:** a grid of qualifying days, with today outlined

The day grid and the weekly streak are different measures. A qualifying day counts toward the current week, while the streak number is in weeks. Badge progress has its own rules and isn't the same as either.

## Events

Tap an event on the Events page for its details: name, tagline, reward, when it ends or starts, and community progress if the event has a shared goal. If you've joined, you'll see **You're in**. To join an event, use MoveTogether on your iPhone.

## Complications

MoveTogether has a **Move Ring** complication that shows how close you are to closing your Move ring today. It works in four complication slots:

- **Circular:** a ring gauge with your Move calories
- **Corner:** a curved gauge with your Move calories
- **Inline:** one line at the top of the watch face with your calories and goal
- **Rectangular:** a gauge with your calories, your goal, and your percent of goal

The complication reads today's Move ring from Apple Health on your watch. It asks for an update about every 15 minutes, and watchOS decides when updates actually happen.

**Adding it:**

1. Touch and hold your watch face.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Swipe to the complications screen.
4. Tap a complication slot, scroll to **MoveTogether**, and select it.
5. Press the Digital Crown to save.

## Requirements

- Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later
- MoveTogether iPhone app installed and signed in
- Apple Health permissions granted

## Background behavior

The Watch app can refresh when the watch has a connection, but watchOS controls background updates. Open the Watch app when you need the latest data. Coach Mo voice needs a live connection.

<Note>
  If the complication shows no progress or looks out of date, open the MoveTogether Watch app once to refresh it, or check Apple Health permissions in **iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MoveTogether**.
</Note>
