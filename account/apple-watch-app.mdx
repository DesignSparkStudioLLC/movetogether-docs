The MoveTogether Apple Watch app gives you the most useful pieces of the iPhone app, right on your wrist. It installs automatically when you install the iPhone app.

## Signing in

The Watch app signs in automatically using your iPhone session — no separate login. The first time you open it after installing, give it a few seconds to sync. If it shows a "needs pairing" screen, open the MoveTogether iPhone app once and the Watch should pick up the session.

## Home screen

The main view shows today at a glance:

- **Date header** at the top
- **Live event banner** (only when there's an active seasonal event)
- **Hero ring** — your Move ring, large and front-and-center
- **Three small ring tiles** — Move, Exercise, Steps with current progress
- **Streak card** — current streak count + tap target for the detail view
- **Today's challenges** — your daily challenges with progress
- **Friends preview** — quick glance at where you stand vs friends today
- **Competitions summary** — your active competitions and standings

Two toolbar buttons:

- **Microphone icon** — tap to talk to Coach Mo (see below)
- **Sparkles icon** — opens the events list (seasonal events)

## Talking to Coach Mo on Watch

Tap the microphone in the top-left of the Home screen.

You'll see the conversation go through a few states:

1. **Idle** — a greeting and a "Chat with Coach Mo" button
2. **Connecting** — brief spinner while we set up the voice session
3. **Live** — the orb animates while Mo talks, with a red end-call button always pinned at the bottom
4. **Ended** — confirmation with "Talk again" and "Close" buttons

Mo can answer questions, give you coaching, and react to what you tell it. The conversation transcript appears above the end-call button so you can scroll through what was said.

If you're not on Pro, you'll see an upgrade prompt instead of the chat button. If you've used your weekly Coach Mo allowance, you'll see a reset reminder.

For more on how Mo works, see [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo) and [What makes Coach Mo different](/social-coach/what-makes-mo-different).

## Streak detail

Tap the streak card on Home for the full breakdown:

- **Hero number** — your current streak in days with a glowing flame icon
- **Last 30 days** — a 6×5 grid showing which days qualified, which didn't, and which used a shield. Today is outlined.
- **Next milestones** — up to 3 upcoming milestones with progress bars
- **Recent badges** — your most recently earned badges (up to 6)

## Events

Tap the sparkles icon on Home to see all active, upcoming, and completed events. Tap an event for its full details: tagline, rewards, deadline countdown ("3d 4h left"), and community progress if there is one.

## Friends leaderboard

The top 3 are shown as a podium with gold, silver, and bronze accents. Ranks 4 and below scroll below the podium with your row highlighted so you can spot yourself quickly.

## Complications

MoveTogether ships an Apple Watch complication that puts your Move ring progress on your watch face.

**What it shows:** a circular gauge with your current Move ring as a percentage. Updates every 15 minutes in the background.

**Adding it:**

1. Long-press your Apple Watch face.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Swipe to the complications screen.
4. Tap a complication slot, scroll to **MoveTogether**, tap to select.
5. Press the Digital Crown to save.

Designed for the **accessoryCircular** slot on modern Watch faces (Modular, Infograph, and similar).

## Requirements

- Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later
- MoveTogether iPhone app installed and signed in
- Apple Health permissions granted

## Background behavior

The Watch app refreshes data periodically when your wrist is up and your Apple Watch has connectivity (Wi-Fi or cellular). Coach Mo voice needs a live connection — it doesn't work in airplane mode or with both Bluetooth + Wi-Fi off.

<Note>
  If the complication shows "—" or data feels stale, open the MoveTogether Watch app once to trigger a refresh, or check Apple Health permissions in **iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MoveTogether**.
</Note>
