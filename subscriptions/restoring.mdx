---
title: "Restoring purchases"
description: "Get your subscription back on a new device or fresh install."
---

If you've reinstalled the app or signed in on a new device and don't see your subscription, you can restore it.

## How to restore

1. Make sure you're signed into the same **Apple ID** that originally purchased the subscription.
2. Open the MoveTogether app.
3. Go to **Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases**.
4. Within a few seconds your subscription should reappear.

## Make sure you're signed in to MoveTogether the same way

Restoring a subscription matches it to the **Apple ID** that bought it — but features unlock based on the **MoveTogether account** you're signed into. If you're signed in to a different MoveTogether account than the one that originally subscribed, restore will succeed but your account on this device still won't see the features.

To fix this: sign out of MoveTogether on the new device, sign in with the **same Sign in with Apple identity** you used originally, then restore again.
