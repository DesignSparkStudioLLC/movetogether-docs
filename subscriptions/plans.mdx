MoveTogether has two plans: **free** and **Pro**.

## Free

The Free plan gives you the core MoveTogether experience at no cost.

What's included:

- Up to 2 active competitions at a time
- Up to 3 communities
- Activity tracking with the three-ring system (Move, Exercise, Steps)
- Supported activity sources and competition leaderboards
- Add friends and compare on leaderboards
- Preset competition taunts
- Seasonal-event badges
- Template-based daily and weekly challenges
- 5 Coach Mo messages total, for the lifetime of the account
- Contextual, non-personalized ads

What's not included:

- Unlimited competitions
- Unlimited communities
- The friend activity feed, reactions, and comments
- Streak, achievement, and competition badges
- Direct messages and group chat
- Custom taunts
- Full analytics, including sleep and weight insights
- Coach Files, coaching styles, and per-activity insights
- Past You Ghost
- An ad-free experience

## Pro

The Pro plan unlocks the complete social, coaching, and analytics experience.

Everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited competitions
- Unlimited communities
- Friend activity feed, reactions, and comments
- Streak, achievement, and competition badges
- Direct messages and group chat
- Custom competition taunts
- Unlimited Coach Mo
- Coach Files, all coaching styles, and per-activity insights
- Personalized AI daily challenges
- Weekly Mo Report and Roast Cards
- Full analytics, including sleep and weight insights
- Past You Ghost
- No ads

Pricing: monthly and annual billing are available. Check the app for current pricing in **Settings → Subscription**.

Just want to try it? Start on the free plan. You can upgrade later.

Want the full social competition experience with unlimited AI coaching, more badges, messaging, and deeper analytics? Go Pro.
