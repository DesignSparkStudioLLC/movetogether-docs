MoveTogether has two plans: **free** and **Pro**.

## Free

The free plan gives you the core MoveTogether experience at no cost. A good way to try the app and get competing with friends.

What's included:

- Up to 2 active competitions at a time
- Activity tracking with the three-ring system (Move, Exercise, Steps)
- Any supported fitness tracker
- Competition leaderboards
- Add friends and see the daily Friends leaderboard
- 5 lifetime Coach Mo messages to try AI coaching
- Daily challenges from the general pool
- Communities (joinable interest-group chats)

What's not included:

- Unlimited competitions
- Friend activity feed and reactions
- Achievements
- Competition group chat
- Direct messaging
- Full Coach Mo access
- Personalized daily challenges
- Coaching persona selection
- Weekly Mo Report and Roast Cards
- Advanced analytics

## Pro

The Pro plan unlocks everything: the full social experience, AI coaching, advanced features.

Everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited competitions
- Friend activity feed and reactions
- Achievements with four medal tiers
- Competition group chat
- Direct messaging
- Unlimited Coach Mo access
- All 5 coaching personas
- Weekly Mo Report and Roast Cards
- Personalized AI daily challenges
- Advanced analytics and trends
- Priority support with faster response times

Pricing: monthly and annual billing are available. Check the app for current pricing in **Settings → Subscription**.

Just want to try it? Start on the free plan. You can upgrade later.

Want the full social competition experience with AI coaching, achievements, messaging, and weekly reports? Go Pro.
