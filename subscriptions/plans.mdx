MoveTogether offers three subscription tiers. You can start on Starter and upgrade any time.

## Starter

The free tier. Good for trying things out.

- 1 active competition at a time
- All three rings, full activity tracking
- Weekly Mo Report
- Coach Mo: limited preview only

## Mover

The most popular plan. Designed for active competitors.

- Unlimited concurrent competitions
- All competition types (weekend, weekly, monthly, custom)
- Full analytics on your activity history
- Group chat in competitions
- Coach Mo: 200 messages per month, voice on iPhone and Apple Watch

## Crusher

The all-access tier. For the most committed users.

- Everything in Mover
- Priority Coach Mo (faster responses, longer context)
- Early access to new features
- Advanced analytics and exports

## Where to see current pricing

Open **Settings → Subscription** in the app — pricing is shown in your local currency. Pricing varies by region and is set by the App Store.
