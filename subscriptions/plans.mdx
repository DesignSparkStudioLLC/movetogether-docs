## Overview

MoveTogether offers two plans designed to meet you wherever you are in your fitness journey: a **free** plan and a **Pro** plan.

## Free

The free plan gives you the core MoveTogether experience at no cost. It's a great way to try the app and get competing with friends.

**What's included:**
- Join or create up to 2 active competitions at a time
- Basic activity tracking with the three-ring system (Move, Exercise, Steps)
- Connect any supported fitness tracker
- View competition leaderboards
- A small weekly Coach Mo preview allowance to try out AI coaching
- Daily challenges from the general pool

**What's not included:**
- Unlimited competitions
- Friends list and social features (activity feed, friend requests)
- Achievements system
- Competition group chat
- Direct messaging
- Full Coach Mo access
- Personalized daily challenges
- Coaching persona selection
- Weekly Mo Report and Roast Cards
- Advanced analytics

## Pro

The Pro plan unlocks everything MoveTogether has to offer — the full social experience, AI coaching, and advanced features.

**What's included (everything in Free, plus):**
- Unlimited competitions
- Friends and social features (add friends, activity feed, reactions)
- Achievements system with four medal tiers
- Competition group chat
- Direct messaging
- Full Coach Mo access with a much larger weekly allowance
- All 5 coaching personas
- Weekly Mo Report and Roast Cards
- Personalized AI daily challenges
- Advanced analytics and trends
- Priority support with faster response times

**Pricing:** Monthly and annual billing options are available. Check the app for current pricing in **Settings → Subscription**.

## Which plan is right for you?

**Just want to try it out?** Start with the free plan — you can always upgrade later.

**Want the full social competition experience** with AI coaching, achievements, messaging, and weekly reports? **Go Pro.**
