MoveTogether has two plans: **free** and **Pro**.

Free covers tracking, joining competitions, friends, challenges, and basic coaching access. Pro expands hosting, social features, analytics, and coaching. Monthly and annual billing are available; check **Settings → Subscription** for current pricing.

## Competition hosting and chat

The Free hosting limit applies to competitions you host, not competitions you join. Draft and upcoming eligible competitions can use a host slot; ending or removing an eligible hosted competition releases that slot. Hosting Personal Goal and Stick To It competitions requires Pro, while anyone invited can join them.

Chat access depends on the channel. Competition chat requires the qualifying live competition membership. Community and league chat also depend on membership and the channel's rules. Pro includes direct messages and removes the Free-plan restrictions on social features; it does not replace a channel's membership requirements.

Daily challenge targets can use recent activity when enough history is available on either plan; a fallback is used for newer accounts. Weekly challenges show their own targets and reset schedule. See [daily challenges](/engagement/daily-challenges) and [weekly challenges](/engagement/weekly-challenges). Both plans can earn the monthly challenge collectible and appear on its leaderboard subject to its rules and privacy settings. Both plans can also [export an activity report](/engagement/export-activity-report) as a CSV.

## Which plan?

Just want to try it? Start on the free plan. You can upgrade later.

Want the full social competition experience with unlimited AI coaching, more badges, messaging, and deeper analytics? Go Pro.
