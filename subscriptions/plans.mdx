MoveTogether has two paid plans:

- **Membership** unlocks the social side: unlimited hosting (including Personal Goal, Stick To It, and Group Goal competitions), direct messages, group chat, unlimited communities, the full friends feed, and custom taunts. It's $$2.99 a month, or $$19.99 a year with a 3-day free trial (US prices; the store shows your local price). The monthly plan has no trial. Membership is sold in the app only.
- **Pro** includes everything in Membership, plus Coach Mo with no message count, deeper analytics, premium badges, Group Challenge hosting, and no ads unless you choose to watch one. In the app it's $$12.99 a month or $$99.99 a year (US prices; the store shows your local price). On the [web portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/) it's $$9.99 a month or $$79.99 a year, and the web annual plan starts with a 1-week free trial.

**New accounts:** accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later choose Membership or Pro during onboarding, on iPhone and Android. Annual Membership starts with a 3-day free trial and renews unless you cancel before the trial ends.

**Existing accounts:** accounts created before 2.1.1 stay on the **Free** plan at no cost, and can upgrade at any time.

## Hosting

The Free hosting limit counts competitions you host, not ones you join. Draft and upcoming competitions use a slot; ending or deleting one frees it. Limited-time events don't count. On iPhone, when you reach the limit you can tap **Watch an ad for 1 competition** to host one more.

Anyone can join any competition on any plan. Hosting Personal Goal, Stick To It, and Group Goal competitions takes Membership or Pro. Hosting a Group Challenge (a big event for up to 100 people, split into divisions) takes Pro. See [personal and shared goals](/competitions/personal-and-shared-goals) for how the goal modes work.

## Chat

Competition chat is open to everyone in the competition, on any plan. Direct messages and group chat take Membership or Pro. Each chat channel has its own membership rules; see [chat access by channel](/social-coach/direct-messaging#chat-access-by-channel).

## Coach Mo

Free and Membership accounts get 5 Coach Mo messages for the lifetime of the account. They don't reset. On iPhone, Free accounts that have used them can tap **Watch an ad for 1 message** to get one more, up to a daily limit. Membership accounts don't get this option.

Pro has no message count. Very heavy use can pause Coach Mo until the weekly reset. In Coach Mo settings, the **Usage** card shows **Weekly limits** and the day your limit resets, at 8 PM ET.

If you start Pro with a free trial, Coach Mo is a preview during the trial, and its messages don't refill. Coach Mo Files and the Weekly Mo Report written by Mo start once your paid Pro begins.

**Use Apple Intelligence** is a Pro setting on iPhone. It shows up in Coach Mo settings only on iOS 27 or later, on a device that supports Apple Intelligence. It runs normal replies privately on Apple Intelligence; Roast mode, game plans, and rich cards still use the standard engine.

See [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo) and [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings).

## Ads

The Free plan shows ads on iPhone. Membership shows none. Pro shows no ads unless you choose to watch one. The Android app doesn't show ads.

## Movecoins

Movecoins is on iPhone in the US. Every plan can earn Movecoins. Pro earns double Movecoins for activity. Membership and Pro can cash out for gift cards; Free plans and free trials can't. See [Movecoins](/movecoins/overview).

## Which plan?

Want to compete and chat with friends without limits? Membership. Want Coach Mo, deeper analytics, premium badges, and Group Challenges too? Pro.
