---
title: "Managing your subscription"
description: "How to check your MoveTogether subscription, switch between monthly and annual billing, cancel through the App Store, and resubscribe to Pro."
---

## Checking your current plan

Go to **Settings → Subscription**. Your current plan, billing period, and next renewal date are shown.

## Changing your billing period

To switch between monthly and annual billing, go to **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription**. You'll be sent to the App Store's subscription management screen where you can pick a new billing period. The change takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

## Canceling your subscription

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription**.
2. You'll be sent to the App Store's subscription management screen.
3. Tap **Cancel Subscription** and confirm.

<Note>
  Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. After canceling, you'll keep Pro features until the end of your current billing cycle. When your paid access ends, your account reverts to the free plan. You don't lose any data.
</Note>

## Resubscribing after cancellation

Go to **Settings → Subscription**, pick the Pro plan, and confirm through the App Store. Your Pro features come back immediately and your existing data (achievements, friends, history) is still there.
