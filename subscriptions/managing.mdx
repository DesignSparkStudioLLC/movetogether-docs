---
title: "Managing your subscription"
description: "How to change, cancel, or check the status of your subscription."
---

## Checking your current plan

Go to **Settings → Subscription**. Your current plan, billing period, and next renewal date are displayed.

## Changing your billing period

To switch between monthly and annual billing, go to **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription**. You'll be taken to the App Store's subscription management screen where you can select a new billing period. The change takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

## Canceling your subscription

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription**.
2. You'll be taken to the App Store's subscription management screen.
3. Tap **Cancel Subscription** and confirm your choice.

<Note>
  Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. After canceling, you'll keep access to Pro features until the end of your current billing cycle. When your paid access ends, your account reverts to the free plan. You won't lose any data.
</Note>

## Resubscribing after cancellation

Go to **Settings → Subscription**, select the Pro plan, and confirm through the App Store. Your Pro features reactivate immediately and your existing data (achievements, friends, history) is still there.
