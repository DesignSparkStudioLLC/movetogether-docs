---
title: "Managing your subscription"
description: "Switch plans, pause, or cancel."
---

All subscription changes happen through Apple's subscription manager — that's an App Store requirement.

## Open the subscription manager

**In the MoveTogether app:** Settings → Subscription → Manage.

**Directly on your device:** Settings app → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → MoveTogether.

## Switch plans

In the subscription manager, tap MoveTogether and pick a different plan. The change takes effect immediately if you upgrade, or at the end of your current period if you downgrade.

## Cancel

Tap MoveTogether in the subscription manager, then **Cancel Subscription**. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

## Pause

Apple doesn't currently allow pausing subscriptions for individual apps. Your only options are cancel (and resubscribe later) or downgrade.

## Resubscribe after canceling

Just tap **Subscribe** in the MoveTogether app — your account and history are kept on file, so resubscribing picks up right where you left off.

<Note>
  Lost access after switching devices or restoring from backup? See [Restoring purchases](/subscriptions/restoring).
</Note>
