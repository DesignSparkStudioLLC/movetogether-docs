---
title: "Managing your subscription"
description: "How to check, manage, cancel, restore, or redeem a MoveTogether Pro subscription through its purchase channel."
---

## Checking your current plan

Go to **Settings → Subscription**. Your current plan and available subscription actions are shown there.

## Manage or cancel your subscription

Open **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription**. MoveTogether opens the management surface for the channel that handles your subscription.

### Apple App Store

Manage your billing period or cancel from the App Store subscription-management screen.

### Google Play

Subscriptions purchased during the Android public beta are managed through Google Play's subscription-management surface.

### Web

Manage a web subscription in the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/).

After you cancel, Pro remains available through the end of the current paid period. Your account then returns to the free plan, and your data remains in your account.

## Resubscribe after cancellation

Open **Settings → Subscription** and choose the available Pro option. Confirm again through the App Store, Google Play, or web checkout, depending on the purchase channel you choose.

## Restore purchases

If you switched devices or reinstalled the app and do not see your subscription:

1. Sign in to the same MoveTogether account that originally held the entitlement.
2. Confirm that the Apple, Google, or web purchase account is the one used for the original purchase.
3. Open **Settings → Subscription** and tap **Restore Purchases**.
4. Reopen **Settings → Subscription** and check your plan status.

If Pro does not return, see [subscription issues](/troubleshooting/subscription-issues). Keep receipts in a private support channel; do not share them in public feedback.

## Redeem an offer code

Open **Settings → Subscription** and tap **Redeem Offer Code**.

- **iOS:** iOS opens its redemption sheet.
- **Android public beta:** Google Play opens its offer-code redemption page.
