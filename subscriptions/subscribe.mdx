## Subscribing for the first time

1. Open MoveTogether and go to **Settings → Subscription**, or tap an upgrade prompt for a Pro feature.
2. Review the Pro plan, current eligible price, and available billing options shown to you.
3. Choose a billing option and confirm through the purchase channel you are using.

### iOS

Confirm your purchase through the App Store. You may need to authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password.

### Android public beta

Confirm your purchase through Google Play. The app shows the current eligible Google Play price before you purchase.

### Web

Complete checkout through the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/). Your entitlement syncs to MoveTogether after checkout is complete.

## Introductory pricing and trials

If introductory, promotional, or trial pricing is available, it is shown before purchase in the relevant purchase flow. Eligibility, renewal terms, and the current price are displayed by the App Store, Google Play, or web checkout before you confirm.
