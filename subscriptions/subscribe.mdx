description: "Step-by-step instructions for subscribing to the Pro plan."
---

## Subscribing for the first time

1. Open MoveTogether and go to **Settings → Subscription** (or tap the upgrade prompt that appears when you try to use a Pro feature).
2. You'll see the Pro plan with pricing and features listed.
3. Pick your billing period (monthly or annual).
4. Confirm your purchase through the App Store. You may need to authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password.
5. Your plan is active immediately and Pro features unlock right away.

## Web purchase

You can also subscribe through our website at [movetogetherfitness.com](https://movetogetherfitness.com). An alternative purchase path that may have different pricing. Your subscription syncs to the app automatically regardless of where you purchased it.

## Introductory pricing

If you see introductory or promotional pricing, it's shown on the subscription screen before purchase. Introductory prices apply to new subscribers only and renew at the standard rate after the promotional period ends.

## Free trial

MoveTogether may offer free trials of the Pro plan from time to time. If a trial is available, you'll see it on the subscription screen. Your trial converts to a paid subscription at the end of the trial period unless you cancel beforehand.
