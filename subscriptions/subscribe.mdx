## Subscribe or upgrade

1. Open **Settings** (tap your photo at the top of Home, then the gear) and tap your plan card at the top. Or tap an upgrade prompt on any locked feature.
2. Review the plan, your price, and the billing options shown.
3. Choose one and confirm through your store.

- **iPhone:** confirm through the App Store with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple Account password.
- **Android:** confirm through Google Play. The app shows your Google Play price first.
- **Web:** Pro (not Membership) is also sold through the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/) for \$9.99 a month or \$79.99 a year, and the web annual plan starts with a 1-week free trial. Your plan syncs to the app after checkout.

In the app, Membership is \$2.99 a month or \$19.99 a year, and Pro is \$12.99 a month or \$99.99 a year (US prices; the store shows your local price). See [plans](/subscriptions/plans) for what each one includes.

## The Membership trial

Accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later choose Membership or Pro during onboarding. Annual Membership starts with a 3-day free trial, then renews at the annual price unless you cancel before the trial ends, through the store you used. Monthly Membership has no trial. See [managing your subscription](/subscriptions/managing).

## Other offers

If an introductory, promotional, or trial price is available, the App Store, Google Play, or web checkout shows it, with renewal terms, before you confirm.
