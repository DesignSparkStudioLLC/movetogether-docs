## How billing works

MoveTogether subscriptions purchased through the app are processed entirely through the **Apple App Store**. Your payment method on file with Apple is charged, and subscriptions auto-renew unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Apple handles all payment processing — MoveTogether does not see or store your payment information.

## Where to find your receipts

Apple sends email receipts for all App Store purchases. You can also view your purchase history by opening the **Settings** app on your iPhone, tapping your name at the top (Apple ID), tapping **Subscriptions** to see active subscriptions and billing dates, and tapping **Purchase History** to see past charges.

## Refund policy

Subscription payments are **non-refundable** as stated in our Terms and Conditions. If you no longer want to be charged, cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future renewals. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

## Common questions

**Will I be charged immediately when I subscribe?**
Yes. Your first payment is charged when you confirm your subscription in the App Store.

**What happens if my payment fails?**
Apple will attempt to charge your payment method again. If the charge continues to fail, your subscription may be paused or canceled by Apple.

**Can I share my subscription with family?**
MoveTogether does not currently support Family Sharing. Each person needs their own subscription.

**Do I lose my data if I downgrade?**
No. Your competition history, achievements, friends list, and all other data are preserved. You'll just lose access to Pro features until you resubscribe.
