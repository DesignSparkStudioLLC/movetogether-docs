---
title: "What is MoveTogether?"
description: "Head-to-head fitness competitions that work with the tracker you already wear."
---

MoveTogether turns the activity you're already tracking into competitions with friends, family, and coworkers — no matter which fitness tracker each person uses.

## How it works

1. **You wear what you already wear.** Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, Whoop, Oura, Polar, Suunto, or Coros — all eight are supported.
2. **MoveTogether normalizes your activity into three rings:** Move (calories), Exercise (minutes), and Steps. Every supported tracker can fill all three.
3. **You join a competition.** Weekend, weekly, monthly, or a custom date range. Compete with anyone, regardless of device.
4. **Coach Mo, your AI coach, keeps you on track** — with check-ins, a weekly recap, and voice chat on your Apple Watch.

## Who it's for

MoveTogether is built for groups that want a friendly, ongoing push: workout buddies, family group chats, work wellness teams, and small online communities. There's no equipment to buy, no separate app to learn — your existing watch or ring does the work.
