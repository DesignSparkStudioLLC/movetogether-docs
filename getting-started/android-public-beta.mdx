title: "Join the Android public beta"
description: "How to join the MoveTogether Android public beta through Google Play, install updates, share product feedback, and get support for sensitive issues."
---

MoveTogether is available on Android as a **public beta**. You can compete with friends across iPhone and Android now, while your feedback helps us improve the Android experience.

## Join and install

1. On the Google account you use with Google Play, open the [MoveTogether beta opt-in](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/studio.designspark.movetogether).
2. Follow Google's enrollment steps.
3. Open the [MoveTogether Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=studio.designspark.movetogether).
4. Install or update MoveTogether.

<Note>
  Google controls beta eligibility, enrollment, regional availability, and the install button. MoveTogether's website cannot confirm enrollment on Google's behalf.
</Note>

## Sign in and compete

Sign in with Apple or Google. If you already have a MoveTogether account, use the same sign-in method so your profile, friends, competitions, and Pro entitlement stay with that account.

## Get beta updates

Google Play delivers Android beta updates. Open the Play listing to check for an available update.

## Share beta feedback

Use [Share beta feedback](https://movetogetherfitness.com/roadmap) for bugs, rough edges, and feature requests that can improve the Android experience.

## Need help with your account?

For account access, billing, privacy, deletion, security, or another sensitive issue, use [MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact) or the in-app support flow instead of the public feedback board.
