MoveTogether is a social fitness app for friendly activity challenges. It is available on iOS and as an Android public beta, with supported data sources varying by platform.

## What you can do

Connect a supported data source, track your activity, and join or host competitions with friends. Current competition formats include Ring Close Count, Percentage of Goals, Total Activity, Steps, Calories, Exercise Minutes, Workout Count, Personal Goal, Stick To It, and WHOOP Strain. The exact formula and requirements for each are in the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference).

You can also follow friends, view activity and league leaderboards, build streaks, earn badges, and use Coach Mo for coaching and product help. Some features depend on your plan, data source, or device.

## Start here

1. Create a free account.
2. Connect a supported tracker or health source.
3. Set daily Move, Exercise, and Steps goals.
4. Join a friend’s competition or create one from **Compete**.

If you are new to group challenges, [your first competition](/getting-started/first-competition) walks through both paths. To create with guided questions, use [Build with AI](/getting-started/build-with-mo).
