MoveTogether is a social fitness competition app that turns your daily activity into friendly challenges with friends. It is available on iOS and as an Android public beta, with supported data sources varying by platform.

The idea is simple: staying active is easier (and more fun) when you're doing it with people you care about. MoveTogether gives you a reason to take the stairs, go for that extra walk, and stay consistent — because nobody wants to be last place in their friend group.

## What you can do

Compete with friends using supported data sources. Apple Health is the on-device source for iOS. Current connected providers include Google Health/Fitbit, Garmin, WHOOP, Strava, Polar, Oura, and Ultrahuman. Suunto is coming soon.

Create and join competitions using seven scoring methods: ring closures, goal percentages, total activity, step counts, calorie burn, exercise minutes, or workout counts. Pick a duration, add teams if you want, and invite anyone.

Track progress in real time. Leaderboards update throughout the day as everyone's activity syncs. You'll see who's ahead, who's catching up, and who needs a nudge.

Build streaks and earn achievements. Hit personal milestones, build daily streaks with Shield protection, and collect medals across four tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) that show off your fitness journey.

Stay in the loop with the activity feed. See when friends close their rings, finish workouts, hit streak milestones, earn achievements, or win competitions. React and cheer each other on. Pro plan.

Send direct messages. Chat one-on-one with friends, share images and links, and react to messages. Pro plan.

Get coaching from Coach Mo. Coach Mo is your AI fitness companion with five coaching personas. You'll get motivation, competition strategy, workout suggestions, weekly reports, and shareable roast cards. Pro plan.

## Who it's for

MoveTogether is for anyone who wants a little extra motivation to stay active. You don't need to be a gym veteran or a marathon runner. The app meets you where you are.

It's especially good for friend groups in different cities, coworkers who want to move more during the workday, families looking for a fun way to stay active together, and couples who'd rather compete than argue about who exercises more.

## What you'll need

- An iPhone running iOS 17 or later, or an Android device eligible for the public beta
- A supported data source for your platform
- A free MoveTogether account

Download the app, connect your tracker, invite your friends.
