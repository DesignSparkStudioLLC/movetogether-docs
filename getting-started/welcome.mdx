MoveTogether is a social fitness competition app that turns your daily activity into friendly challenges with friends. Whether your crew uses Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, WHOOP, Oura, Strava, Polar, Suunto, or Ultrahuman, everyone competes together on the same leaderboard.

The idea is simple: staying active is easier (and more fun) when you're doing it with people you care about. MoveTogether gives you a reason to take the stairs, go for that extra walk, and stay consistent — because nobody wants to be last place in their friend group.

## What can you do with MoveTogether?

**Compete with friends across any fitness tracker.** Your Apple Watch. Your friend's Fitbit. Your coworker's WHOOP. MoveTogether supports nine major fitness platforms and brings them all together so device differences never get in the way of a good competition.

**Create and join fitness competitions.** Set up competitions using five different scoring methods — ring closures, goal percentages, raw activity numbers, step counts, or workout-based scoring. Choose your duration, add teams if you want, and invite anyone.

**Track your progress in real time.** Watch leaderboards update throughout the day as everyone's activity syncs automatically. See who's ahead, who's catching up, and who needs a nudge.

**Build streaks and earn achievements.** Hit personal milestones, build daily activity streaks with shield protection, and collect achievement medals across four tiers — Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum — that show off your fitness journey.

**Stay connected through your activity feed.** See when friends close their rings, complete workouts, hit streak milestones, earn achievements, or win competitions. React and cheer each other on. Available on the Pro plan.

**Send direct messages to friends.** Chat one-on-one with friends, share images and links, and react to messages. Available on the Pro plan.

**Get personalized AI coaching from Coach Mo.** Coach Mo is your AI fitness companion with five unique coaching personas to choose from. Get personalized motivation, competition strategy, workout suggestions, weekly reports, and shareable roast cards. Available on the Pro plan.

## Who is MoveTogether for?

MoveTogether is for anyone who wants a little extra motivation to stay active. You don't need to be a gym veteran or a marathon runner. Whether you're just starting your fitness journey or you've been at it for years, the app meets you where you are.

It's especially great for friend groups who live in different cities, coworkers who want to move more during the workday, families looking for a fun way to stay active together, and couples who'd rather compete than argue about who exercises more.

## What you'll need

- An iPhone running iOS 17 or later
- A supported fitness tracker (Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, WHOOP, Oura Ring, Strava, Polar, Suunto, or Ultrahuman)
- A free MoveTogether account

That's it — download the app, connect your tracker, and invite your friends.
