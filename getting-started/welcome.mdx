MoveTogether is a social fitness app for friendly activity competitions. It's on iPhone through the App Store and on Android through Google Play. It works with any watch, ring, or app that syncs to Apple Health or Health Connect, or with just your phone.

## What you can do

Track your Move, Exercise, and Steps rings, then join or host competitions with friends.

There are eight scoring modes on iPhone: Ring Close Count, Percentage of Goals, Total Activity, Workout Count, Personal Goal, Stick To It, Distance, and Head to Head (steps, calories, or exercise minutes). Android has the same modes except **Distance**, so seven in all. See the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference).

You can also follow friends, climb weekly Move Leagues, build streaks, earn badges, and ask Coach Mo for coaching.

## Find your way around

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    The tab bar has **Home**, **Social**, and **Compete**. The red **\+** button sits at the end of the bar.

    **Social** has two parts: **Feed** (friends and their activity) and **Chats** (messages and communities). Swipe or tap to switch.

    The **\+** button opens: **Help**, **Invite friends**, **New message**, **Join with code**, **New competition**, **Data Sources**, **Sync activity**, and **Ask Coach Mo**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    The tab bar has **Home**, **Social**, **Compete**, and **Chat**, then a **\+** button.

    The **\+** button opens: **Help**, **Invite friends**, **Join with code**, **New competition**, **Data Sources**, **Sync activity**, and **Ask Coach Mo**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

- **Profile:** on iPhone, tap your photo at the top right of Home. On Android, open it from Home.
- **Settings:** open Profile, then open Settings. Paths in this Help Center such as **Settings → Data Sources** start there.
- **Help:** **\+ → Help** opens the support chat on both apps.
