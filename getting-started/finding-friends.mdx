Friends live in the **Social** tab. On iPhone, open **Social → Feed**.

## How to add friends

**Search by username.** Open **Social** (on iPhone, the **Feed** side) and use the search field (**Search by username…**). Tap **Add** to send a request.

**Share your invite link.** Tap the **+** button and choose **Invite friends**, then send the link in any messaging app. See [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals).

**Find friends from contacts (iPhone).** In **Social → Feed**, tap **Find friends from contacts**. MoveTogether checks your contacts privately to show who is already on the app, and you can invite the rest. Android doesn't have this yet.

**Suggestions.** Under **People you may know**, MoveTogether may suggest people based on mutual friends and contacts.

## Friend requests

You get a notification when someone sends you a request. Open **Social** and tap **Friend requests** to see what's pending, then tap **Accept** or decline. **Requests sent** shows requests you're waiting on.

## Who can send you friend requests?

Go to **Settings → Privacy Settings**, find **Who can send friend requests** under **Social**, and pick **Everyone**, **Friends of friends**, or **No one**.

## Removing a friend

Open the person's profile from **Social**, open its options menu, tap **Remove friend**, and confirm. Removing a friend doesn't remove either of you from competitions you share.
