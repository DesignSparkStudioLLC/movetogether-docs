## How to add friends

**Search by username.** If you know your friend's MoveTogether username, tap the **Friends** tab, then tap the search icon. Type their username and send a friend request.

**Share your invite link.** Every MoveTogether user has a unique invite link. Go to your **Profile** tab, tap **Share Profile** or **Invite Friends**, and send your link through text, email, social media, or any messaging app.

**Invite from contacts.** In the Friends tab, tap **Invite Friends**. MoveTogether can check your phone contacts to see if anyone you know already has an account. For contacts who aren't on the app yet, you can send them an invite.

**Friend suggestions.** MoveTogether may suggest people you might know based on mutual friends and contacts.

## Friend requests

When someone sends you a friend request, you'll receive a notification. Go to the Friends tab and tap **Friend Requests** to see pending requests. Tap **Accept** to add them as a friend, or **Decline** to dismiss the request.

## Who can send you friend requests?

You control who can send you friend requests through your privacy settings. Go to **Settings → Privacy**, and under **Social Controls**, find **Who can send friend requests**. Choose from **Everyone**, **Friends of Friends**, or **No One**.

## Removing a friend

To remove someone from your friends list, go to the Friends tab, find the person you want to remove, tap their profile, then tap **Remove Friend** and confirm your choice. Removing a friend won't remove them from any active competitions you're both in, but they'll no longer appear in your activity feed or be suggested for future competitions.
