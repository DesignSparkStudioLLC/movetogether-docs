## How to sign up

1. Download MoveTogether from the App Store.
2. Open the app and tap **Get Started**.
3. Choose how you'd like to create your account:
   - **Sign in with Apple** — Uses your Apple ID. The fastest option, and Apple lets you hide your email for privacy.
   - **Sign in with Google** — Uses your Google account for quick setup.
4. Review and accept the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines. You can tap each one to read the full policy before agreeing.
5. Choose a display name and username. Your display name is what friends see on leaderboards. Your username is how people find and add you.
6. Add a profile photo (optional). You can upload a photo from your camera roll or take a new one. You can always change this later in your profile settings.

## Choosing a good username

Your username is unique to you and is how friends will find you in the app. Keep it short and recognizable so friends can easily search for you. Usernames can include letters, numbers, and underscores. You can change your username later in your profile settings, but your friends will need to search for the new one.

## What happens next

After creating your account, the app will guide you through a short onboarding flow — including connecting your fitness tracker, setting your fitness goals, and customizing your experience. This is the most important setup step, as it's how MoveTogether reads your activity data so you can compete.

If you skip any steps during onboarding, you can always complete them later in Settings.
