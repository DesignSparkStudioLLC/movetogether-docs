Creating an account takes under a minute.

## Sign in with Apple (recommended)

1. Download **MoveTogether** from the App Store.
2. Tap **Sign in with Apple** on the welcome screen.
3. Choose whether to share or hide your email address.
4. You'll land on the onboarding flow — pick your goals, connect your tracker, and you're in.

Sign in with Apple is the fastest path and doesn't require remembering a password.

## Sign up with email

If you'd rather not use Sign in with Apple, choose **Sign up with email** on the welcome screen and follow the prompts.

## After signing up

You'll be guided through:
- A short goal-setting flow (what brought you to MoveTogether)
- Connecting your fitness tracker
- Inviting friends or joining your first competition

<Note>
  Need to delete your account later? See [Delete your account](/account/delete-account) — it's a one-tap action in Settings.
</Note>
