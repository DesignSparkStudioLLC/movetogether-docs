## How to sign up

1. Get MoveTogether:
   - **iPhone:** download it from the App Store.
   - **Android:** install it from Google Play. See [MoveTogether on Android](/getting-started/android).
2. Open the app. The first screen shows the sign-in buttons. Choose one:
   - **Sign in with Apple** (on Android, **Continue with Apple**) uses your Apple ID. Apple can hide your email for privacy.
   - **Sign in with Google** (on Android, **Continue with Google**) uses your Google account.
3. Review the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines, then tap **Accept and continue**.
4. Set up your profile: your first and last name, a username, and your birthday. Your name is what friends see on leaderboards. Your username is how people find and add you.
6. Add a profile photo if you like. You can change it later from Profile.

## Choosing a good username

Your username is how friends find you, so keep it short and recognizable. Usernames can include letters, numbers, and underscores. Change it later from **Settings → Personal Details → Username**; changes are limited to once every 30 days, and your old handle is held for 30 days.

## What happens next

Onboarding asks a few quick questions, connects your activity through Apple Health or Health Connect, shows you the league you've been placed in, and offers to invite a friend and turn on notifications. [First-run onboarding](/getting-started/first-run-onboarding) has the details.

Accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later then choose a plan: Membership (\$2.99 a month, or \$19.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, US prices) or Pro. See [plans](/subscriptions/plans).
