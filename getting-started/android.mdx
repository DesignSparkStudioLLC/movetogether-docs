MoveTogether is on Android through Google Play. iPhone and Android friends compete in the same competitions and on the same leaderboards.

## Install

1. Open the [MoveTogether Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=studio.designspark.movetogether).
2. Tap **Install**.

<Note>
  Google controls regional availability, device compatibility, and the install button. MoveTogether's website cannot confirm availability on Google's behalf.
</Note>

## Your data comes from Health Connect

On Android, MoveTogether reads your activity from Health Connect. Your wearable shares into Health Connect from its own app. See [Connect your tracker to Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker).

MoveTogether reads six Health Connect types and no more: **steps, active calories, exercise sessions, sleep, weight, and height**. Sleep, weight, and height are read for Pro members; without Pro, MoveTogether reads the three activity types. Heart rate is not read.

It never writes to Health Connect. Background refresh and history are separate optional permissions. With history access MoveTogether can load up to 90 days of past activity; without it, reads are limited to 30 days. You can revoke any of it in Health Connect at any time.

## Sign in and compete

Sign in with Apple or Google. If you already have a MoveTogether account, use the same sign-in method so your profile, friends, competitions, and plan stay with that account.

## Get updates

Google Play delivers MoveTogether updates. Open the Play listing to check for an available update.

## Share feedback

Use [Share feedback](https://movetogetherfitness.com/roadmap) for bugs and feature requests.

## Need help with your account?

For account access, billing, privacy, deletion, security, or another sensitive issue, use [MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact) or the in-app support flow instead of the public feedback board.
