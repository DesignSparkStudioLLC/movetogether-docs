title: "Language and region"
description: "Change the app language through iPhone or Android settings and distinguish language from units and time-zone behavior."
---

MoveTogether supports English, Spanish, French, and German. The Help Center is currently in English.

## iPhone

Open **MoveTogether → Settings → Language**. This opens the system settings for the app; MoveTogether does not provide a separate in-app translation picker. Use the language option offered by iOS, then return to MoveTogether.

If iOS does not offer a per-app language choice, review your device's preferred languages in its language settings. The controls available depend on your iOS configuration.

## Android public beta

Open MoveTogether's **Settings → Language → App language**. On supported Android versions, this opens the app-language system screen. On older versions it opens the app's system settings, and language can follow the device setting instead.

## Website and units

The marketing website has its own language switcher. Changing the website language does not change your phone's app language.

For kilometres versus miles or kilograms versus pounds, use [units and appearance settings](/account/appearance-and-display). Language, units, and the time windows used by a feature are separate choices. For example, [Move Leagues](/competitions/leagues) reset at one UTC instant worldwide even when two members read the app in different languages.
