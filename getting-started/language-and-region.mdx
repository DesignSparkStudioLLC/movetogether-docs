---
title: "Language and region"
description: "What languages MoveTogether supports and how language selection works today."
---

The MoveTogether app and marketing website support English, Spanish, French, and German. The Help Center is currently available in English.

The app uses your current iPhone and app language behavior; there is no separate language picker in MoveTogether. To change the language used by the app, use the language controls provided by iOS for your device or app. The website offers English, Spanish, French, and German versions from its language switcher.
