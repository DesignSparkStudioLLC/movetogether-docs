title: "First-run onboarding"
description: "What MoveTogether asks for when you create an account, including data-source setup, coaching choices, and optional permissions."
---

Onboarding sets up your identity, activity source, and first MoveTogether experience. The exact path varies with your choices and whether you opened an invite link. If you leave partway through, the app can resume the unfinished flow when you return.

## Identity

Add the name and unique username people will use to recognize you. A profile photo is optional and can be changed later. Usernames are checked for availability as you enter them.

## Data-source setup

After identity, choose how you track activity. On iOS, the Apple Health path includes an equipment step so MoveTogether can tailor coaching and challenge suggestions. During the Android public beta, Health Connect opens Android's permission flow. Connected wearable providers use their own secure authorization flow on either platform. See [Connect a data source](/getting-started/connect-tracker) for the current service list.

Next, **Get Started** introduces your first experience, followed by **League intro** and **Seasonal events**. The app can help you create or join a first competition, but you can also explore and use the Compete tab later.

## Invitations and notifications

You may see **Invite** to share your referral link, then **Notifications**. An invite-link variant can join the competition from the link instead of following the standard first-competition path.

## Coach Mo setup

The later flow introduces **Meet Mo**, lets you choose a **Persona**, and shows a **First Insight**. After that value step, you may see the standard, dismissible Pro offer; price and trial eligibility come from your current offering. You can then choose a **Morning Brief** time and respond to the **Location** request when it is shown.

Permissions are optional unless a feature requires them. Later, manage sources in **Settings → Data Sources**, Coach Mo choices inside Coach Mo, privacy in **Settings → Privacy Settings**, and notifications in **Settings → Notifications**.

## What you can finish later

- Add or change your photo from **Profile**.
- Add or reconnect a tracker from **Settings → Data Sources**.
- Create or join a competition from **Compete**.
- Change notification categories from **Settings → Notifications** and system permission from your phone's settings.
- Change your Coach Mo persona or Morning Brief schedule inside Coach Mo.
