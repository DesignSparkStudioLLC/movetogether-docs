Onboarding sets up your profile, connects your activity, and shows you the league you're already in. iPhone and Android follow nearly the same order. If you leave partway through, the app can resume where you stopped when you return.

## The steps, in order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your profile">
    Add your first and last name, a unique username, and your birthday, and pick what Coach Mo should focus on. A profile photo is optional and can be changed later. Usernames are checked for availability as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A few quick questions">
    One-tap screens about what usually stops you and how MoveTogether helps. There's nothing to set up here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your device">
    Tell MoveTogether what you track with.

    - **iPhone:** **What do you use?** Pick your wearable, or **Just my iPhone**.
    - **Android:** **How do you track your activity?** Pick **My phone or Wear OS watch**, or **Garmin, WHOOP, Oura or another account** and then your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your activity">
    Allow MoveTogether to read Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). If you use a wearable, turn on sharing in its own app first; the screen shows how. See [connect your tracker](/getting-started/connect-tracker).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your league">
    You're placed in a weekly league with people at your level. On iPhone, a **Time to Move** screen comes first, where you can tap **See my league**, or **Join with invite code** if a friend sent you a code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your first move from Coach Mo">
    Coach Mo suggests a first small goal for today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    On iPhone, new accounts choose Membership or Pro here. On Android this comes after the last step. See [Membership](#membership) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a friend">
    Share your invite link, or skip for now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notifications">
    Turn notifications on, or skip them for now. You can change this later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you opened a friend's invite link, onboarding can join you to their competition instead.

## Membership

Accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later must choose Membership or Pro to use the app. On iPhone this screen comes right after your first move from Coach Mo; on Android it comes when you finish onboarding. It can't be skipped: **Sign Out** is the only other way out.

Membership is \$2.99 a month, or \$19.99 a year (US prices). Annual starts with a 3-day free trial and renews unless you cancel; monthly has no trial. The app shows your price before you confirm. See [plans](/subscriptions/plans).

Accounts created before 2.1.1 don't see this screen and keep the Free plan.

## What you can finish later

- Change your photo from **Profile** (on iPhone, tap your photo at the top right of Home).
- On iPhone, choose or change the tracker that counts in **Settings → Data Sources**. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source).
- Create or join a competition from **Compete**, or with the **\+** button.
- Change notification categories in **Settings → Notifications**, and system permission in your phone's settings.
- Change privacy in **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
