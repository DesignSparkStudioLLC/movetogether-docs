---
title: "Building a competition with Mo"
description: "How to use Build with AI to assemble a competition draft, revise its details, switch to manual setup, and create it after review."
---

**Build with AI** is a guided competition builder in the create flow. It asks questions and assembles a local draft; it does not create a competition until you review it and tap **Create competition**.

## Build a competition

1. Open **Compete** and tap **+**.
2. Tap **Build with AI**.
3. Answer the guided questions about your group, date window, scoring, and invitations. You can type an answer or choose a suggested option.
4. Review the live draft as it fills in.
5. Choose the review option when the builder has enough details.
6. Check the final competition screen, make any needed edits, then tap **Create competition**.

The normal create flow applies the final checks, including host limits and Fair Play requirements.

## Change your mind

Tap **Set it up myself** to switch to the manual wizard with the answers collected so far. You can also revise details in the normal review flow before creating.

Leaving the builder before creating does not send invitations or create a server competition. It is a guided setup tool, separate from ordinary Coach Mo chat; asking Coach Mo in chat does not authorize autonomous competition creation.

For the manual path and join options, see [your first competition](/getting-started/first-competition).
