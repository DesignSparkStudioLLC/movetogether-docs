MoveTogether for Android is **coming soon**. There is no public Android download or beta enrollment available right now.

## What to expect

We are preparing the Android experience so more friends can compete together across phones and supported fitness trackers.

We will publish installation, setup, data-source, and support guidance here when Android is ready for public use. We are not announcing a launch date yet.

In the meantime, MoveTogether remains available on iOS through the App Store.
