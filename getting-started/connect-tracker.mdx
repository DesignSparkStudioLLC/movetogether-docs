---
title: "Connect a data source"
description: "Connect Apple Health on iOS, Health Connect on Android public beta, or a supported wearable service, and review sync status in Data Sources."
---

MoveTogether can use Apple Health, Health Connect, or a connected wearable service for activity data. Available sources vary by platform. Go to **Settings → Data Sources**, then select the source you want to connect.

## Available services

- Apple Health
- Health Connect
- Google Health / Fitbit
- Garmin
- WHOOP
- Strava
- Polar
- Oura
- Ultrahuman

Suunto appears in the picker as **Coming soon** and cannot be connected yet. Apple Watch data reaches MoveTogether through Apple Health; the native MoveTogether Apple Watch app is a separate companion experience.

## Connect and manage

1. In **Settings → Data Sources**, tap **Connect a service**.
2. Select the service and complete its authorization flow.
3. Return to **Data Sources** to check its status. A paused service can be reconnected; an active service can be disconnected when that option is available.

### Apple Health

Apple Health uses the iOS permission sheet rather than a provider login. Allow the categories you want MoveTogether to read, including the activity metrics used by your competitions. To review the choice later, open **iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MoveTogether**.

Your Apple Watch first syncs its data to Apple Health, then MoveTogether reads the authorized categories. If today's activity is missing, confirm the Watch has synced to the Health app before troubleshooting MoveTogether.

### Health Connect on Android public beta

Select **Health Connect** from **Settings → Data Sources**, then complete Android's permission flow. Return to Data Sources to review the connection and the permissions available to your account and device.

### Wearable services

Google Health / Fitbit, Garmin, WHOOP, Strava, Polar, Oura, and Ultrahuman use the provider's authorization flow. MoveTogether does not receive or store the password you enter on the provider's sign-in page. The first sync can take time after you return to the app.

Open the provider's companion app and let the device finish syncing there before checking MoveTogether. If a connection is paused or expired, use the reconnect action in **Data Sources**.

The screen also shows which sources contribute to health metrics. Do not assume that connecting more than one service will deduplicate the same activity; review your sources and use the connection that best fits your setup.

For help when data is late or missing, see [Troubleshoot data sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).
