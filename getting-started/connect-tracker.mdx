MoveTogether reads your daily activity from one of eight supported sources. You only need to connect one.

## Supported trackers

- **Apple Watch** (via Apple Health)
- **Fitbit**
- **Garmin**
- **Whoop**
- **Oura**
- **Polar**
- **Suunto**
- **Coros**

## How to connect

1. Open the MoveTogether app.
2. Go to **Settings → Connections** (or follow the onboarding prompt if it's your first sign-in).
3. Tap your tracker brand.
4. You'll be sent to your tracker's official login page to authorize MoveTogether.
5. Once you approve, your last 90 days of activity will start syncing into MoveTogether.

## How long does the first sync take?

Most trackers backfill within a few minutes. Garmin and Whoop can take up to 15 minutes for the initial 90-day pull.

## Can I connect more than one tracker?

You can, but only one tracker is used as your **active source** for competitions at a time. Switch your active source from **Settings → Connections** at any time.
