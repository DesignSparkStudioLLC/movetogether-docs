title: "Connect a data source"
description: "Connect Apple Health, Android Health Connect, or a wearable service, verify the first sync, and manage multiple activity sources."
---

MoveTogether reads activity from an on-device health service or a connected wearable provider. You do not need an Apple Watch to use a supported connected provider.

## Choose the right connection

| Source | Where it connects | What to check first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Health | iPhone permission sheet | Recent activity is visible in the Health app. Apple Watch activity reaches MoveTogether through this path. |
| Health Connect | Android permission flow | Your tracker app has written the permitted data to Health Connect. |
| Google Health / Fitbit, Garmin, WHOOP, Strava, Polar, Oura, Ultrahuman | Provider authorization | Sign in to the provider account that holds your activity. |

Suunto is shown as **Coming soon** and cannot be connected yet. The native [Apple Watch app](/account/apple-watch-app) is a companion experience; installing it and granting Apple Health access are separate steps.

Not every provider supplies every metric. A connected status does not guarantee steps, sleep stages, weight, or other data that your device does not record or share.

## Connect a wearable service

1. Sync your device with its own companion app.
2. Open **MoveTogether → Settings → Data Sources**.
3. Tap **Connect a service**, then select your provider.
4. Complete the provider's sign-in and authorization screens.
5. Return to **Data Sources** and check the connection status.
6. Refresh your activity after the provider has finished its first sync.

The provider handles its password screen; MoveTogether does not receive that password. If a provider displays an access requirement or upgrade screen, review it before continuing. Connection does not make unavailable source metrics appear.

## Apple Health on iPhone

Select Apple Health in Data Sources and use **Connect** or **Review Access**, depending on its current state. Allow the categories you want MoveTogether to read.

If you use an Apple Watch, check today's values in the iPhone Health app first. If the Watch has not synced to Health, refreshing MoveTogether cannot recover data that has not arrived there yet.

Review permissions later in the Health app's access settings for MoveTogether. Permission labels and the path through iPhone Settings can vary by iOS version. For an individual metric, use **Health → Browse → the metric → Data Sources & Access** to inspect its contributors.

## Health Connect on Android

Open **Settings → Data Sources → Health Connect** and complete Android's permission flow. Core activity uses authorized steps, active calories, and exercise sessions. Optional sleep, weight, and height access supports additional features. Background reading and history access are separate permissions.

MoveTogether reads from Health Connect; it does not write changes back. See [Android public beta](/getting-started/android-public-beta) for the permission and history scope.

## More than one source

You can add another service. On iPhone, **Primary source** appears when there are at least two eligible providers. Use it to choose a preference or leave **Automatic** selected. See [choose your primary source](/getting-started/primary-activity-source) before interpreting differences between device totals.

## Reconnect or disconnect

A paused service can offer **Reconnect**. Complete authorization again, then check for new activity. If you see **Disconnect**, it stops that connection's future syncing; some providers instead direct you to manage access in their own app.

Disconnecting is not account-data deletion. Existing MoveTogether records remain subject to your [data and privacy controls](/account/data-and-privacy).

If a Fitbit connection shows **Update connection**, follow that flow to move the connection to Google Health. For missing readings, start with [manage and troubleshoot data sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).
