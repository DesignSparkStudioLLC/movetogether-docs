title: "Your first competition"
description: "How to join a competition or host your first one manually or with Build with AI, including scoring, teams, invites, and host limits."
---

Open **Compete** to join an existing competition or tap **+** to host one.

## Join a competition

1. Open a friend’s invite link, enter a code from **Compete → Join**, or open an in-app invitation.
2. Review the dates, scoring method, and participants.
3. Tap **Join Competition**.

You can join on every plan. If you cannot join, the competition may be full, ended, restricted to invited people, or using an invalid link or code. The Free host limit does not prevent joining.

## Host manually

1. In **Compete**, tap **+** and choose the manual setup.
2. Name the competition, select dates, and choose whether it repeats.
3. Choose scoring. **Step Count** is a clear first choice; see the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference) before choosing a more specialized mode.
4. Choose privacy and, if useful, team mode or a group goal.
5. Select friends now or send invitations after creation.
6. Review the summary and tap **Create competition**.

The new competition appears as upcoming until its start date. A Free account can host up to two eligible active, upcoming, or draft competitions; joining other people’s competitions remains unlimited.

## Build with AI

From the create screen, tap **Build with AI**. Answer the guided questions about your group, dates, scoring, and invitees. You can revise the suggested details, switch to the manual wizard, then review and tap **Create competition** yourself.

The builder helps construct a draft. It does not let ordinary Coach Mo chat create or submit competitions autonomously. For a detailed walkthrough, read [building a competition with Mo](/getting-started/build-with-mo).

## Fair Play notice

New accounts can see a Fair Play notice instead of the create button. Follow the notice’s requirements, usually connecting a tracker and allowing activity to sync. You can still join a competition someone else hosts.
