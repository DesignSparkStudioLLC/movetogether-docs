## Joining a competition

**If a friend shared a link:** Tap the link to open MoveTogether straight to the competition page. Tap **Join Competition**.

**If you have a competition code:** Open the app, go to the **Compete** tab, and tap **Join**. Enter the code and tap **Join**.

**If a friend invited you in-app:** You get a notification. Tap it to see the competition details, then tap **Join Competition**.

**Browse public competitions:** Open the **Compete** tab to find public competitions and seasonal events.

## Creating a competition

Ready to challenge your friends? Here's how to set one up:

**Step 1: Competition info.** Go to the Compete tab and tap **+**. Give it a fun name, set start and end dates, and choose whether it repeats. Toggle **Private** on if you only want invited friends to join.

**Step 2: Scoring method and team mode.** Pick how participants earn points: Ring Close Count (points for closing activity rings), Percentage of Goals (points based on goal completion), Total Activity (points for every calorie, minute, and step), Step Count (points per step), Calorie Burn (points for active calories), Exercise Minutes (points for minutes of exercise), or Workout Count (a point per dedicated workout). Optionally turn on Team Mode and pick 2, 3, or 4 teams.

**Step 3: Invite friends.** Select friends from your list, or skip and invite them after the competition is created.

**Step 4: Review.** Check your competition details and confirm to launch.

## How scoring works

Once a competition starts, everyone's activity syncs automatically from their tracker. The leaderboard updates throughout the day. You don't need to manually log anything. Your score is based on the scoring method picked when the competition was created.

## Tips for your first competition

- Start with 7 days to get a feel for the pacing.
- Pick **Step Count** scoring if this is everyone's first time. It's the most straightforward.
- Invite 3 to 5 friends. Enough people to feel competitive, but small enough that everyone stays engaged.
- Check the leaderboard daily to stay motivated.

## "Why can't I create a competition yet?"

If you're brand new to MoveTogether, you may see a Fair Play notice instead of the Create button. This is a one-time gate that prevents accounts with no activity history from immediately creating competitions — it keeps things fair for groups who've been at it for a while.

The notice will tell you what you still need to unlock creating. Usually it means: connect a tracker, let activity sync for a day or two, and the gate lifts on its own. You can still **join** competitions other people create in the meantime.
