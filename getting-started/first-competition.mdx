On iPhone, tap the red **\+** button: **Join with code** joins a competition, and **New competition** hosts one. You can also do both from **Compete**. On Android, open **Compete** and tap **Create** to host one.

## Join a competition

1. Open a friend's invite link, use **Join with code**, or open an in-app invitation.
2. Review the dates, scoring method, and participants.
3. Tap **Join Competition**.

You can join on every plan. If you can't join, the competition may be full, ended, limited to invited people, or the link or code may be out of date.

## Host manually

These steps follow the iPhone wizard. Android asks for the same things on one screen.

1. Tap **\+**, then **New competition**.
2. Name the competition.
3. Choose a scoring mode. iPhone has eight: Ring Close Count, Head to Head, Workout Count, Percentage of Goals, Total Activity, Personal Goal, Stick To It, and Distance. Android has the first seven (no Distance). **Head to Head** on steps is an easy first choice; see the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference) for the others.
4. Pick dates. On iPhone you can also choose whether it repeats.
5. Choose public or private and, if you like, team mode or a shared goal.
6. Pick friends now or send invitations after creating it.
7. Review the summary and tap **Create competition**.

The competition shows as upcoming until its start date. Membership and Pro can host unlimited competitions. Accounts on the older Free plan can host up to two active, upcoming, or draft competitions at a time; joining is unlimited on every plan.

Some extras need a plan to host. Anyone can join them:

- **Personal Goal**, **Stick To It**, and a **shared goal** need Membership or Pro.
- A **Group challenge** (up to 100 people, iPhone only) needs Pro. See [Group Challenges](/competitions/group-challenges).

## Build with AI

On the create screen, tap **Build with AI** and answer a few questions about your group, dates, scoring, and invitees. You can edit the draft or switch to manual setup, then review and tap **Create competition** yourself. See [building a competition with Mo](/getting-started/build-with-mo).

## Fair Play agreement

The first time you create a competition, a **Fair play** sheet appears before it is created. It lists a few rules: log activity honestly, respect other competitors, report suspected cheating instead of accusing publicly, and have fun. Tick **I agree to play fair and follow the community guidelines.** and tap **Accept and continue**.

You only agree once. After that, creating a competition goes straight through. If you tap **Cancel**, nothing is created, and you can try again whenever you're ready. Joining a competition doesn't show this sheet.
