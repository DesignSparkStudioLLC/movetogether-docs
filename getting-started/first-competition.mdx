## Joining a competition

**If a friend shared a link:** Tap the link to open MoveTogether directly to the competition page. Tap **Join Competition** to enter.

**If you have a competition code:** Open the app, go to the **Compete** tab, and tap the **Join** button. Enter the competition code and tap **Join**.

**If a friend invited you in-app:** You'll receive a notification. Tap it to view the competition details, then tap **Join Competition**.

**Discover public competitions:** Browse the **Discover** section in the Compete tab to find public competitions and seasonal events you can join.

## Creating a competition

Ready to challenge your friends? Here's how to set one up:

**Step 1: Competition info.** Go to the Compete tab and tap the **+** button. Give your competition a fun name, set your start date and end date, and choose whether it repeats. Toggle **Private** on if you want only invited friends to join.

**Step 2: Scoring method & team mode.** Choose how participants earn points: Ring Close Count (points for closing activity rings), Percentage of Goals (points based on goal completion percentage), Raw Numbers (points for every calorie, minute, and step), Step Count (points for every step taken), or Workout Based (points only during specific workout types). Optionally toggle on Team Mode and choose 2, 3, or 4 teams.

**Step 3: Invite friends.** Select friends from your friends list to compete, or skip and invite friends after creating the competition.

**Step 4: Review.** Review all your competition details and confirm to launch your competition.

## How scoring works

Once a competition starts, everyone's activity syncs automatically from their connected fitness tracker. The leaderboard updates throughout the day — you don't need to manually log anything. Your score is based on the scoring method selected when the competition was created.

## Tips for your first competition

- Start with a 7-day competition to get a feel for the pacing.
- Choose **Step Count** scoring if this is everyone's first time — it's the most straightforward.
- Invite 3–5 friends for the best experience: enough people to feel competitive, but small enough that everyone stays engaged.
- Check the leaderboard daily to stay motivated.
