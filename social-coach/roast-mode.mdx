title: "Roast mode"
description: "What roast mode is, how to turn it on, what changes when it's on, and how it's different from the Drill Sergeant persona."
---

Roast mode makes Coach Mo meaner. Mo uses your real numbers as ammunition for sarcastic, no-holds-barred commentary about your week. When you want a wake-up call instead of a pep talk, this is the switch.

## Turning it on

Two paths:

**The toggle.** Open **Coach Mo settings** → flip **Roast mode** on. It stays on across conversations until you flip it off.

**Quick suggestion.** On a fresh Coach Mo conversation, the empty state includes a **"Roast me"** suggestion chip with a flame icon. Tap it and the chat input fills with "Roast me on this week's effort. Don't pull punches." You can send as-is or edit before sending. This doesn't flip the global toggle — it's a one-shot.

## What changes

Same Mo, sharper edges:

- Tone gets cutting instead of supportive
- Mo references your actual numbers (steps, calories, missed days) as setups
- Your chosen persona's voice is preserved, just turned mean — a Hype Man in roast mode is still over-the-top, just over-the-top in your face

## A taste of the difference

Same situation: you walked 847 steps yesterday.

**Default Mo (normal):**
> Hey, light day yesterday. Want to talk about what got in the way, or are we just resetting for today?

**Default Mo (roast):**
> Not you sitting at 847 steps like that's a flex. My grandma's Fitbit is judging you right now.

**Sports Analyst (normal):**
> 847 steps puts you well below your weekly average of about 7,200. Want to dig into what shifted?

**Sports Analyst (roast):**
> Let me put this in perspective. 847 steps puts you in the bottom 3% of all MoveTogether users this week. Historically bad.

You get the idea.

## Roast mode vs. the Drill Sergeant persona

These are two separate things you can mix freely:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---------|------------------|
| **Persona** | Mo's voice (Default / Drill Sergeant / Hype Man / Big Sibling / Sports Analyst) |
| **Mode** | Mo's edge (Normal / Roast) |

**Drill Sergeant in Normal mode**: tough but encouraging — pushes you with discipline.

**Default Mo in Roast mode**: friendly voice, sarcastic edge.

**Drill Sergeant in Roast mode**: the most intense combination available. Don't pick this when you're already having a bad day.

## Who can use it

Roast mode is available on **both Free and Pro**. Free members can use it within their 5 lifetime Coach Mo messages; Pro includes unlimited Coach Mo access.

## Turning it off

Open **Coach Mo settings** and flip **Roast mode** off. The next response will be back in your persona's normal tone.

<Note>
  Roast mode is meant to be funny and motivating in the right mindset, not actually harsh. If anything Mo says crosses a line for you, tell Mo directly — it can soften up in the same conversation. For account or content concerns, [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
</Note>
