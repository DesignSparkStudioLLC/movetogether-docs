Roast mode changes Coach Mo from supportive to sarcastic. It can use your activity context for the joke, but it is meant to address effort rather than your identity or body.

## Turning it on

On iPhone, there are a few ways:

**The flame button.** In Coach Mo, tap the flame button at the left of the message bar. Tap it again to turn roast mode off.

**The settings switch.** Open **Coach Mo settings** and turn on **Roast mode**. It stays on across conversations until you turn it off.

**Your first visit.** The first time you open Coach Mo, it asks **How should Mo talk to you?** Choose **Roast me** to start in roast mode, or **Supportive** for normal coaching. You can change this later.

**Quick suggestion.** On a fresh Coach Mo conversation, the empty state includes a **Roast me** suggestion with a flame icon. Tap it and the message field fills with "Roast me on this week's effort. Don't pull punches." You can send it as is or edit it first. It's a one-time prompt and doesn't turn on roast mode.

## Choose an intensity

**Roast mode**, your **coaching style**, and **roast intensity** are separate controls. Intensity sets how much Mo swears:

- **Clean:** Roasts stay PG-13. No swearing, still savage.
- **Classic:** Swears like a friend who actually swears.
- **Unhinged:** No filter. Mo goes for the jugular.

If you've never picked a level, Mo uses **Classic**. The first time you turn on the **Roast mode** switch in Coach Mo settings without having picked one, Mo asks **How hard should Mo roast?** so you choose.

You can change intensity in two places, and they always match:

- **Coach Mo settings → Roast intensity**
- **Settings → Notifications → How much Mo swears**, on every plan, because Mo's notifications can swear too

Intensity applies to roast language in chat, Coach Mo notifications, and voice. Changing it does not turn roast mode on; the roast switch still controls whether Mo uses the roast tone in chat.

Accounts known to be under 18 only see **Clean**, with an **Available at 18+** note.

## If Mo goes too far

Tell Mo directly. If you say something like "that's harsh" or "stop swearing", Mo switches your intensity to **Clean**, tells you it did, and stops swearing in chat and in its notifications from then on. You can switch back any time in **Coach Mo settings → Roast intensity**.

## Roasts by voice

On Apple Watch voice (paid Pro), Mo follows your intensity setting but roasts only when you ask, for example "roast me". See [Talk to Coach Mo by voice](/social-coach/voice).

## What changes

Same Mo, sharper edges:

- Tone gets cutting instead of supportive
- Mo references your actual numbers (steps, calories, missed days) as setups
- Your style's voice stays: a Hype Man in roast mode is still over the top, just in your face

## A taste of the difference

These are the sample lines the style picker shows, not promised responses. Same situation: you walked 847 steps.

**Classic Mo (roast):**

> not you sitting at 847 steps like that's a flex. my grandma's fitbit is judging you rn

**Sports Analyst (roast):**

> Let me put this in perspective. 847 steps puts you in the bottom 3% of all MoveTogether users this week. Historically bad.

You get the idea.

## Roast mode vs. the Drill Sergeant style

These are two separate things you can mix freely:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---------|------------------|
| **Personality** | Mo's voice (Classic Mo / Drill Sergeant / Hype Man / Big Sibling / Sports Analyst) |
| **Roast mode** | Mo's edge (normal or roast) |
| **Roast intensity** | How much Mo swears (Clean / Classic / Unhinged) |

**Drill Sergeant in normal mode**: tough but encouraging, pushing you with discipline.

**Classic Mo in roast mode**: friendly voice, sarcastic edge.

**Drill Sergeant in roast mode**: the most intense combination. Choose **Clean** intensity if you want the directness without profanity.

## Who can use it

Roast mode is on **every plan**. On the Free plan and Membership you get 5 Coach Mo messages total for the lifetime of the account. Pro has no message count, but very heavy use can pause Mo until the weekly reset. Styles other than Classic Mo need Pro.

## Turning it off

Tap the flame button again, or open **Coach Mo settings** and turn **Roast mode** off. The next response will be back in your style's normal tone.

<Note>
  Roast mode is meant to be funny and motivating in the right mindset, not actually harsh. If anything Mo says crosses a line, tell Mo directly and, if you aren't on Clean already, it switches you there. For account or content concerns, use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
</Note>
