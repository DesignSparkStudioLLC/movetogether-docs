Coach Mo is MoveTogether's AI coach. It can discuss your activity, goals, routines, and competitions, but it is not medical advice.

<Note>
  On the Free plan and Membership, you get 5 Coach Mo messages total, for the lifetime of the account. Pro has no message count, but very heavy use can pause Mo until the weekly reset. See [Coach Mo access](#coach-mo-access).
</Note>

## Start a conversation

On iPhone, tap the red **+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**, then type a question. Pro members can also use [Coach Mo voice](/social-coach/voice) on Apple Watch. Your conversations are saved so you can return to them later, and suggested follow-up questions can help you continue without starting over.

## Choose a coaching style

Coach Mo has five coaching styles:

| Style | Tagline in the app |
| --- | --- |
| Classic Mo (the default) | Gen Z energy, zero filter |
| Drill Sergeant | No excuses, soldier |
| Hype Man | Your biggest fan |
| Big Sibling | Caring, honest, always real |
| Sports Analyst | The numbers don't lie |

The first time you open Coach Mo directly, the **Pick a voice** screen lets you choose any style. After that, you change it in Coach Mo settings under **Personality**, and switching to any style other than Classic Mo needs Pro.

Your style changes how Mo communicates, not the fitness data or app controls available to it.

## Product help, coaching, and support

Coach Mo handles different questions in different ways:

- **Product help** answers questions about how MoveTogether works, using the Help Center. These answers don't use one of your 5 lifetime messages. A question that needs your own data, or reports something broken, is treated as coaching instead.
- **Personal coaching** uses your fitness context. On the Free plan and Membership this uses your 5 lifetime messages. Pro has no message count.
- **Account and app problems** go to the support chat, not to Mo. If you tell Mo something is broken (syncing, your account, billing, or a bug), Mo can show a **Get help with this** card. Tap **Continue to support** to open the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support), or **Not now** to keep chatting. When the support chat opens, it receives the message you sent Mo and your sign-in status, so you don't have to repeat yourself.

You can also reach the support chat any time: on iPhone, tap the red **\+** button and choose **Help**, or go to **Settings → Resources → Contact Support**. On Android, go to **Settings → Contact Support**.

## What Coach Mo can help with

- General fitness motivation, goal setting, and routine ideas.
- Context about your recent activity, streaks, and competitions.
- Weekly reports and in-app insights when available.
- Remembering details you choose to keep in Files.
- Proposing temporary plans for travel, illness, busy periods, rest days, or another schedule you describe. You review and confirm a plan before it is applied.

When personalization is enabled, Mo can use context such as activity history, workouts, sleep and recovery signals, goals, streaks, achievements, competitions, weekly progress, and rivalries. The activity trace on a response can show what Mo checked before answering; see [Reading Coach Mo's thinking](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace).

Coach Mo cannot autonomously create competitions, send messages or invitations, edit raw fitness data, or change your goals. Those actions stay with you. The separate guided competition builder can collect your choices and prepare a draft, but you review and explicitly create the competition; see [Build a competition with Mo](/getting-started/build-with-mo).

## Activity trace

Coach Mo shows its work through an **activity trace**, a list of what data it looked up (your competition stats, activity history, achievements) before forming its response. This helps you understand how Coach Mo arrives at its recommendations.

## Conversations

Every conversation with Coach Mo is saved. Browse your history in the **conversations drawer**, pick up where you left off, or start a new topic anytime. See [Managing your Coach Mo conversations](/social-coach/managing-conversations).

## Memory and Files

Coach Mo has a long-term memory system called **Files**. It remembers important details about your fitness journey, goals, and preferences. Opening Files to view, edit, and organize these memories needs Pro. See [Managing Coach Mo's memory](/social-coach/managing-coach-mo-memory).

## Coach Mo access

| Plan | Coach Mo |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 5 messages total, for the lifetime of the account. After they're used, you may see **Watch an ad for 1 message**. |
| Membership | 5 messages total, for the lifetime of the account. No ad option. |
| Pro free trial | A limited preview. The app shows how many trial messages you have left, and they don't refill. |
| Pro | No message count. Very heavy use can pause Mo until the weekly reset. |

The 5 lifetime messages don't reset or refill. On the Free plan, ad credits depend on an ad being offered, are limited to two per UTC day, and never reset the lifetime allowance.

On Pro, the usage card in [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings#usage) shows **Weekly limits** with a percentage used and when it resets (**Limit resets every** a set day **at 8 PM ET**).

If your last Pro payment didn't go through, Coach Mo pauses until you update your payment method. The rest of Pro keeps working in the meantime.

Apple Watch voice requires a paid Pro subscription. See [Talk to Coach Mo by voice](/social-coach/voice) for its separate access behavior.

## Contextual insights

Coach Mo can surface **contextual insights** as inline cards on relevant screens. When you're viewing a competition, your activity, or your achievements, Coach Mo may offer a quick insight without you opening a conversation.

## Important disclaimer

Coach Mo cannot diagnose, prescribe treatment, or replace a qualified health professional. It gives general fitness information and guidance only. Always consult a doctor before starting a new fitness program, especially if you have health concerns. Don't share sensitive personal information such as passwords or payment details in Coach Mo chat. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) for how this feature processes information.
