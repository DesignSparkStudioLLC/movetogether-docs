## Start a conversation

Open Coach Mo from the coach control in the app, then type a question or use voice where it is available. Your conversations are saved so you can return to them later, and suggested follow-up questions can help you continue without starting over.

## Choose a coaching style

Pro members can choose among five personas in Coach Mo settings:

| Persona | Coaching style |
| --- | --- |
| Default | Balanced, friendly, and supportive |
| Drill Sergeant | Direct, tough-love accountability |
| Hype Man | High-energy encouragement and celebration |
| Big Sibling | Warm, caring, and gently accountable |
| Sports Analyst | Data-focused and strategic |

Your persona changes how Mo communicates, not the fitness data or app controls available to it.

## Free and Pro access

Free members can send 5 Coach Mo messages for the lifetime of the account. That allowance does not reset. Pro includes unlimited Coach Mo access; service use is managed under a weekly cost budget, so the app may show usage information without advertising a message limit.

Apple Watch voice access also requires Pro. See [the Apple Watch app](/account/apple-watch-app) for the Watch experience.

## What Coach Mo can help with

- General fitness motivation, goal setting, and routine ideas.
- Context about your recent activity, streaks, and competitions.
- Weekly reports and in-app insights when available.
- Remembering details you choose to keep in Files.
- Proposing temporary plans for travel, illness, busy periods, rest days, or another schedule you describe. You review and confirm a plan before it is applied.

When personalization is enabled, Mo can use context such as activity history, workouts, sleep and recovery signals, goals, streaks, achievements, competitions, weekly progress, and rivalries. The activity trace beneath a response can show what Mo checked before answering; see [Reading Coach Mo's thinking](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace).

Coach Mo cannot create competitions, send messages or invitations, edit raw fitness data, or change your goals on its own. Those actions stay with you.

Coach Mo cannot diagnose, prescribe treatment, or replace a qualified health professional. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) for how this feature processes information.
