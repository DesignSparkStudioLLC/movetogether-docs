<Note>
  Direct messaging needs Membership or Pro, for you and for the friend you're messaging.
</Note>

## Starting a direct message

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Any of these opens a conversation:

    - Tap the red **\+** button in the tab bar, then **New message**, and pick a friend.
    - Open **Social**, switch to **Chats**, and tap the pencil (**New message**) next to **Your Chats**.
    - Open a friend's profile and tap the message button at the top.

    Your direct messages appear in **Social → Chats** under **Your Chats**, mixed in with your group chats and sorted by the most recent message.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Open the **Chat** tab and tap the **New message** button at the top, then pick a friend.

    To go back to an existing conversation, tap **Direct Messages** at the top of the **Chat** tab.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

In the friend picker, a friend without Membership or Pro shows **Can't receive messages yet** and can't be messaged until they have one.

Type your message and tap send. You can send text, images, and links.

## Reactions

Tap and hold any message to open the reaction picker, then pick an emoji. Your reaction shows under the message. Tap the same emoji again to remove it. If several people react with the same emoji, you'll see a count next to it.

## Sending images and GIFs

Use the attachment button next to the message field to add a photo. On iPhone you can also take a photo with the camera or search for a GIF. Attached media previews before you send.

## When new messages arrive

Conversations update while you're in them. To control direct-message notifications, see [Notification settings](/account/notifications).

## Content moderation

Images shared in direct messages are checked for inappropriate content. All messages are subject to our [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation).

## Chat access by channel

Direct messages need Membership or Pro. Other chats have their own rules:

| Channel | Who can open and participate |
| --- | --- |
| Direct messages | Membership and Pro members messaging a friend who also has Membership or Pro |
| Competition chat | Participants in an eligible active or upcoming ordinary competition, on every plan; seasonal and evergreen events do not grant this chat access |
| Communities | Joined members; on the older Free plan, up to three communities and 10 messages a week |
| League chat | Members of an eligible current league group when the group meets the in-app eligibility requirements |
| MoveTogether Updates | Everyone can read official announcements; replies are unavailable |

Competition chat supports text messages and reactions. If a competition chat won't open, confirm that you're still a participant and that the competition is active or upcoming. See [Use Chat, Updates, and mentions](/social-coach/chat-announcements-mentions) for where each chat lives.
