---

<Note>
  Direct messaging is available on the Pro plan.
</Note>

## Sending a direct message

To start a conversation with a friend, go to their profile and tap the message icon, or access your DMs from the **Friends** tab. Type your message and tap send. You can send text messages, images, and links.

## Reactions

React to any message by tapping and holding it, then choosing a reaction. This is a quick way to acknowledge a message without typing a full reply.

## Hiding conversations

If you want to clean up your message list, you can **hide** a conversation. This removes it from your visible list but doesn't delete it. If the person sends you a new message, the conversation will reappear.

## Content moderation

Images shared in DMs are automatically scanned for inappropriate content to keep the platform safe. All messages are subject to our [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation).

## Competition group chat

In addition to DMs, every competition has its own **group chat** where participants can talk trash, encourage each other, and share the competition experience. Group chat is available on the Pro plan and supports text messages and reactions.
