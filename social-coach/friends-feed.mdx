<Note>
  Adding friends is on every plan. Membership or Pro unlocks the full activity feed and comments. On the older Free plan you see a short preview of the feed and can still react to it.
</Note>

## The Social tab

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    The **Social** tab has two pages, **Feed** and **Chats**. Switch between them with the control at the top of the screen, or swipe left and right.

    - **Feed** holds your friend requests, the **Moving now** rail, **Friend activity**, contacts, and **People you may know**. Search for people by username at the top.
    - **Chats** holds your group chats and direct messages. See [Use Chat, Updates, and mentions](/social-coach/chat-announcements-mentions).

    The app remembers which page you used last.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    The **Social** tab holds your friend requests, the **Moving now** rail, **Friend activity**, and **People you may know**. Search for people by username at the top.

    Messages are in the separate **Chat** tab. See [Use Chat, Updates, and mentions](/social-coach/chat-announcements-mentions).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Today's Leaderboard** is on the **Compete** tab, not on Social.

## Friend requests

Requests sent to you appear under **Friend requests** at the top of the feed. Tap **Accept** to add the person, or decline the request. Requests you've sent appear under **Requests sent** until the other person answers, and you can cancel them there. See [finding friends](/getting-started/finding-friends) to add more people.

## Moving now

The **Moving now** rail shows today's ring progress for your friends and, if you're in a league, for people in your league group. A friend's circle shows when they were last active, and a league member's shows their rank. Tap **All friends** to see your full friends list, and tap anyone to open their profile.

## Friend activity

**Friend activity** highlights notable moments from your friends, such as personal records, streaks, and competition wins. Everyday ring progress shows in **Moving now** instead, so on a quiet day this section may say **Nothing big yet today**. When there are more than five items, tap **See all activity** to see the rest.

If the feed doesn't load, pull down to try again. If an update you expect hasn't appeared, refresh after your friend's activity has synced.

## Reacting to activities

Cheer an activity with a reaction: **Like**, **Fire**, **Strong**, or **Clap**. Each kind toggles on its own, so you can add more than one kind to the same activity. Tap a reaction you've already added to remove it.

Comments need Membership or Pro. On the older Free plan you can react to the activities in the preview, but opening comments shows the upgrade screen.

## Who sees your activities?

Only your friends see your activities in their feed. To stop sharing them, go to **Settings → Privacy Settings** and turn off **Show my activity in friends' feeds** in the **Activity** section. See [Privacy Settings](/account/privacy-settings).
