## The Friends tab

The **Friends** tab is your social hub inside MoveTogether. It brings together your friends list, friend suggestions, direct message notifications, contact integration, and a live activity feed — all in one place. Tap the Friends icon in the bottom navigation bar to access it.

## Your friends

At the top of the Friends tab, you'll see your friends displayed as profile circles. A green dot means they're currently active in the app, and a gray dot means they're offline. Tap any friend to view their profile, or tap the **Add** button to invite new friends.

## Today's leaderboard

Below your friends list is **Today's Leaderboard** — a daily ranking of you and all your friends based on today's activity. You can see everyone's current score, how many rings they've closed, and where you rank. Your row is highlighted so you can quickly spot your position.

## Activity feed

The activity feed shows real-time updates from your friends. You'll see competition wins, streak milestones, ring closures, achievement unlocks, and completed workouts. Every update is about real progress someone in your circle has made.

## Reacting to activities

Cheer your friends on by tapping **React** on any activity in the feed. Choose from Fire, Clap, Heart, Muscle, or Party reactions. Each person can add one reaction per activity. Tap your reaction again to remove it.

## Who sees your activities?

Only your friends can see your activities in their feed. You control this in **Settings → Privacy → Activity Sharing**. Toggle off **Show my activity in friends' feeds** to hide your activities.
