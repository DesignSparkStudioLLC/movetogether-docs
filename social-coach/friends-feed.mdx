</Note>

## The Friends tab

The **Friends** tab is your social hub inside MoveTogether. It brings together your friends list, friend suggestions, contact integration, and activity. The **Moving now** rail at the top shows friends’ everyday ring progress. The **Friend activity** feed is for highlights such as personal records, streaks, and competition wins. Free members can see a two-item preview; Pro can browse the full feed and use **See all activity** when more highlights are available. Direct messages live in the separate **Chat** tab.

## Your friends

At the top of the Friends tab, you'll see your friends as profile circles. A green dot means they're currently active in the app, a gray dot means they're offline. Tap any friend to view their profile, or tap **Add** to invite new friends.

## Today's leaderboard

Below your friends list is **Today's Leaderboard**. A daily ranking of you and all your friends based on today's activity. You'll see everyone's current score, how many rings they've closed, and where you rank. Your row is highlighted so you can spot your position fast.

## Activity feed

The activity feed highlights notable progress from your friends, such as competition wins, personal records, and streak milestones. Everyday ring progress appears in **Moving now**. Refresh after a friend syncs if an expected update has not appeared.

## Reacting to activities

Cheer a visible activity with its reaction buttons: **Like**, **Fire**, **Strong**, or **Clap**. Each kind toggles independently, so you can add more than one kind to the same activity; tap a selected reaction again to remove that kind.

Comments and comment reactions are Pro features. Reacting to an activity is available on Free when that activity is visible in the preview.

## Who sees your activities?

Only your friends can see your activities in their feed. In **Settings → Privacy Settings**, find the **Activity** section and turn off **Show my activity in friends' feeds** to hide your activities.
