</Note>

## The Friends tab

The **Friends** tab brings together your friends list, friend suggestions, contact integration, today's leaderboard, and a live activity feed. Direct messages and their notifications live in **Chat**.

## Your friends

At the top of the Friends tab, you'll see your friends as profile circles. A green dot means they're currently active in the app, a gray dot means they're offline. Tap any friend to view their profile, or tap **Add** to invite new friends.

## Today's leaderboard

Below your friends list is **Today's Leaderboard**. A daily ranking of you and all your friends based on today's activity. You'll see everyone's current score, how many rings they've closed, and where you rank. Your row is highlighted so you can spot your position fast.

## Activity feed

The activity feed shows real-time updates from your friends. Competition wins, streak milestones, ring closures, achievement unlocks, completed workouts. Every update is about real progress someone in your circle made.

## Reacting to activities

Cheer your friends on by tapping **React** on any activity in the feed. Pick from Fire, Clap, Heart, Muscle, or Party. Each person can add one reaction per activity. Tap your reaction again to remove it.

## Who sees your activities?

Only your friends can see your activities in their feed. Control this in **Settings → Privacy Settings**. Turn off **Show my activity in friends' feeds** to hide your activities.
