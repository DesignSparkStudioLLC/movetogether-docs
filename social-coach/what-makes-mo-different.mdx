Coach Mo is built for fitness coaching with your own data, not a general chatbot told to act like a coach.

## Built around your data

When personalization is on and data is available, Mo can use your activity, competition history, and goals. Ask "how did I do last week?" and Mo looks at your actual numbers. Ask about pacing a weekend competition and Mo can factor in your competitors' averages.

A generic chatbot says "try to walk more." Mo can say "you hit your Move ring on weekdays but drop off on Saturdays. Want a Saturday challenge?"

## Memory that lasts

Mo's long-term memory, **Files**, keeps your goals, habits, wins, and what has and hasn't worked, so you don't have to reintroduce yourself. With Pro you can view, edit, or delete Files any time. See [managing Coach Mo's memory](/social-coach/managing-coach-mo-memory).

## Privacy

Before Coach text prompts reach Anthropic, direct names are replaced with placeholders, and your MoveTogether user ID is left out. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) and the [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy).

On iPhone, Pro members on iOS 27 or later with a device that supports Apple Intelligence can turn on **Use Apple Intelligence** in [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings#use-apple-intelligence). Normal replies then run on Apple Intelligence. Roast mode, game plans, and rich cards still use the standard engine.

## Mo shows its work

The **activity trace** at the top of a reply shows lookups such as competition stats, activity history, and achievements. It doesn't expose all internal reasoning. See [reading Coach Mo's thinking](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace).

## Five coaching styles, one memory

Mo comes in five styles: **Classic Mo** (the default), **Drill Sergeant**, **Hype Man**, **Big Sibling**, and **Sports Analyst**. Same memory of you, different voice. The first time you open Coach Mo you can pick any style. After that, switching to a style other than Classic Mo needs Pro. See [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo#choose-a-coaching-style).

## More than chat

- A **Weekly Mo Report** (Pro) every Monday recapping your week
- **Share cards** from that report to share your week with friends
- **Contextual insights** as cards on screens you're already viewing
- **Follow-up suggestions** for your next question

## The honest take

Mo is an AI assistant, not a doctor or certified trainer, and it sometimes gets things wrong, especially on fitness science far from your data. When it does, tell Mo and it can correct itself in the same conversation.
