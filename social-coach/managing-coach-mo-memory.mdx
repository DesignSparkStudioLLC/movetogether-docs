title: "Managing Coach Mo's memory (Files)"
description: "How to open Files on Pro, find a document, and add, edit, or delete the memories Coach Mo keeps about you."
---

Coach Mo remembers things about you between conversations using a system called **Files**. Files holds your goals, habits, preferences, wins, and other context Mo has learned about you over time.

You can view, edit, add to, and delete what's in Files. Mo's memory of you is yours to manage.

<Note>
  Opening Files needs Pro. A Pro free trial doesn't include it. On the Free plan, Membership, or a trial, tapping **Files** shows an upgrade screen instead.
</Note>

## Opening Files

On iPhone, tap the red **+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**, swipe right from the left edge (or tap the menu icon) to open the conversations drawer, then tap **Files** near the top. Files opens as a large sheet over your conversation.

## How Files is organized

Files has two folders. Tap a folder to expand it and see its documents. Tap a document to see what's saved in it.

**Core**

- **User:** basic facts about you

**Coaching Memory**

- **Goals:** what you're trying to achieve
- **Habits:** things you do regularly (or want to)
- **Barriers:** what gets in the way of your goals
- **Preferences:** what you like, when you work out, what you don't want recommended
- **Wins:** PRs, streaks, and milestones Mo notices
- **Patterns Mo Noticed:** patterns Mo finds in your recent activity
- **Roast Material:** tease-worthy details Mo saves as you chat, for roasts

Folders start collapsed.

## Adding a memory

Inside a document:

1. Tap the **+** button in the top-right.
2. Type the memory (up to 150 characters).
3. Tap **Save**.

**Preferences** has sections (Exercise, Nutrition, Recovery, and Other), so you'll see a section picker there.

You can't add to **Patterns Mo Noticed** yourself. Mo fills it in, and you can correct or remove what's there.

Mo also adds to Files automatically as it learns things during conversations. You don't have to enter everything by hand.

## Editing or deleting a memory

Tap any item to open **Edit bullet**. From there:

- Change the text and tap **Save**
- Tap **Delete bullet**, then confirm **Delete** to remove it

You can also swipe left on an item and tap **Delete**.

Deleted memories disappear from **Files** right away, so Mo will not use that saved memory as long-term context in future conversations. This does not delete the conversation transcript where the information was discussed; remove that separately in [Managing your Coach Mo conversations](/social-coach/managing-conversations).

## Searching Files

Tap **Search files** at the top of Files. Search matches document names (such as "Goals" or "Wins"), not the text of each item inside them.

## Sorting

The **Sort** menu orders the documents inside each folder by **Modified**, **Date created**, or **Name**, in either direction. Most recently modified first is the default, and your choice is saved between app launches.

## Wiping all of Files

To start clean, go to **Coach Mo settings → Delete all files**. This wipes every fact, goal, habit, and preference Mo has saved in Files. It does not delete chat transcripts, and it cannot be undone.

</Note>
