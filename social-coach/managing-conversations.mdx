---
title: "Managing your Coach Mo conversations"
description: "How to find, start, rename, delete, and search past Coach Mo conversations on iPhone, and how chat transcripts differ from Mo's long-term memory stored in Files."
---

Every conversation with Coach Mo is saved automatically. You can browse them, rename them, delete them, or search them.

## Opening the conversations drawer

On iPhone, tap the red **+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**, then swipe right from the left edge or tap the menu icon at the top left to open the conversations drawer. You'll see your past conversations listed with their title and how long ago they happened.

## Starting a new conversation

Tap the compose icon (**New conversation**) next to the search field at the top of the drawer. Your current conversation is saved automatically before the new one starts.

## Renaming a conversation

Coach Mo gives every chat a default title based on what you talked about. To change it:

1. Open the conversations drawer.
2. Touch and hold the conversation.
3. Tap **Rename**.
4. Enter a new title and tap **Save**.

A good rename helps when you want to find a specific past conversation later (for example, "Marathon training plan" instead of the auto-title).

## Deleting a conversation

1. Open the conversations drawer.
2. Touch and hold the conversation.
3. Tap **Delete**, then confirm **Delete**.

<Note>
  Deleting a conversation removes it permanently. Your Coach Mo memory (Files) isn't affected. Anything Mo learned about you from that conversation stays in Files unless you remove it from there too.
</Note>

## Searching

Tap **Search** at the top of the drawer. Search matches each conversation's title and its most recent message. It doesn't search every message in older parts of a conversation, so if a search comes up empty, try words from the title.

## What about Coach Mo's memory?

Conversations and **Files** (Coach Mo's long-term memory) are separate. Conversations are the transcripts of what you said back and forth. Files is what Mo remembers about you between conversations.

Deleting a conversation does **not** delete the memories Mo formed during it. Manage those in **Files** from the conversations drawer (Pro), or wipe them all from **Coach Mo settings**. Conversely, deleting a File does not delete its conversation transcript.
