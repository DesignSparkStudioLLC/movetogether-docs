<Note>
  Communities are on every plan. Membership and Pro have no practical join or weekly-message cap. On the older Free plan you can join up to three communities and send up to 10 community messages a week.
</Note>

Communities are joinable group chats built around a shared goal or training style, like Runners, Early Birds, or a strength crew. They're an easy way to meet people who train like you without sending a friend request.

## Browsing and joining

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open **Social** and switch to **Chats**.
    2. Scroll to **Explore Communities** and swipe through the cards.
    3. Tap **Join** on a card to join right away, or tap the card to preview the community first and then tap **Join Now**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open the **Chat** tab.
    2. Scroll to **Explore Communities** and swipe through the cards.
    3. Tap the **\+** on a card to join right away, or tap the card to preview the community first and then tap **Join Now**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Previewing a community doesn't join it. Once you join, the community moves into **Your Chats**. On the Free plan, leave a community before joining a fourth.

## Posting and etiquette

Once you've joined, type in the message bar to post. Communities support text messages and reactions. Keep it on topic for the group, hype people up, and talk a little trash when a competition calls for it. The usual rule applies: be the person you'd want in the group.

On the Free plan, the chat warns you when you have 3 or fewer of your 10 weekly messages left, and tells you when you've used them all.

## Leaving a community

1. Open the community from **Your Chats**.
2. Open **Community Info**: on iPhone, tap the picture at the top right; on Android, tap the community's name at the top.
3. Tap **Leave Community**, then confirm.

You'll stop seeing its messages. You can rejoin anytime from **Explore Communities**.

## Moderation and reporting

Communities are public spaces, so the same automatic moderation that protects direct messages and competition chat runs here too. You can report a message or a member, and everything posted is subject to our [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation).
