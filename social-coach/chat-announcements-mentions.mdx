---
title: "Use Chat, Updates, and mentions"
description: "Find direct messages, group chats, official updates, and @mentions in the Chat tab."
---

The **Chat** tab brings together competition and community conversations. Its toolbar opens **Direct Messages** and the notifications view for @mentions.

## Find the right conversation

- Open **MoveTogether Updates** for official, read-only announcements.
- Tap a competition or joined community card to open its group chat.
- Use **Direct Messages** to start or continue a one-to-one conversation.
- Open the notifications view to review messages that @mention you.

Chat-tab badges can reflect unread updates and mentions. Notification delivery also depends on your device permission and **Settings → Notifications** preferences.
