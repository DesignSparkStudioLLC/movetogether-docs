Your chats live in a different place on each app.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Open **Social** and switch to **Chats** (tap it at the top, or swipe left from **Feed**). From top to bottom you'll see:

    1. The **MoveTogether Updates** card, for official announcements.
    2. **Your Chats**: your group chats (communities and competition chats) and your direct messages in one list, newest first. Above the list, pick **All** to see every chat or **Mentions** to see only messages where someone @mentioned you. The pencil next to **Your Chats** starts a new direct message.
    3. **Explore Communities**, for communities you haven't joined yet.

    There are no buttons in the top bar on this page. To start a direct message from anywhere, tap the red **\+** button in the tab bar, then **New message**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Open the **Chat** tab. Along the top are three buttons: **Direct Messages**, **Notifications**, and **New message**. Below them you'll see:

    1. The **MoveTogether Updates** card, for official announcements.
    2. **Your Chats**: your joined communities and competition chats.
    3. **Explore Communities**, for communities you haven't joined yet.

    Tap **Direct Messages** for your one-to-one conversations, and **Notifications** for your @mentions.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Find the right conversation

- **A group chat:** select it under **Your Chats**.
- **A community you haven't joined:** scroll to **Explore Communities** and tap its card. The preview is read-only until you join. See [Communities](/social-coach/communities).
- **MoveTogether Updates:** tap the card at the top. Updates are official, read-only announcements and don't accept replies.
- **A direct message:** on iPhone, it's in **Your Chats**. On Android, tap **Direct Messages**. Starting a new direct message needs Membership or Pro.
- **Your @mentions:** on iPhone, tap **Mentions** above **Your Chats**. On Android, tap **Notifications**. Mentions come from your direct messages, communities, and competition chats, and tapping one opens the conversation it came from.

## Unread counts

On iPhone, the **Mentions** filter shows a count of unread mentions, and each chat in **Your Chats** shows its own unread badge. The **Social** tab icon shows your number of pending friend requests, or a dot when you have unread chats and no requests.

On Android, the **Direct Messages** and **Notifications** buttons can show unread counts, and the **MoveTogether Updates** card shows how many posts you haven't read.

Whether you get a notification for a new message also depends on your phone's notification permission and your choices in [Notification settings](/account/notifications).

For who can use each kind of chat, see [Direct messaging](/social-coach/direct-messaging#chat-access-by-channel).
