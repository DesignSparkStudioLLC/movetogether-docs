title: "Reading Coach Mo's thinking"
description: "What the 'Prepared in' header on Mo's replies means, what each trace step icon represents, and how to read what Mo looked up."
---

When Coach Mo answers a question, you can see what it looked up to form that response. This is called the **activity trace**, and it's there so you can check Mo's reasoning instead of trusting a black box.

## Where the trace appears

At the top of each of Mo's replies, above the text, you'll see a collapsible header like:

> **Prepared in 4 seconds · 3 steps**

Tap it to expand or collapse the trace and see the individual steps Mo took.

## What it looks like while Mo works

While Mo is writing a reply, the header is open and reads **Preparing your response...** until the first step arrives, then **N steps** as steps come in. The "Preparing your response" text gently pulses so you know Mo is still working, not stuck.

Once the reply is done, the header settles to **Prepared in X seconds · N steps**. If **Keep thinking expanded** is off, the trace then collapses.

## Step types

Each step has a small icon that tells you what kind of step it was:

| Icon | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| 🧠 Brain, or another icon for the kind of reasoning (a chart, arrows, a clipboard, a checkmark, or a clock) | Reasoning: Mo thinking through how to answer |
| 🔍 Magnifying glass | Memory searched: Mo looked through Files for context |
| 📄 Document | Document read: Mo opened a specific memory document |
| ➕ Plus circle | Bullet added: Mo wrote something new to Files |
| ✏️ Pencil circle | Bullet updated: Mo edited an existing memory |
| 📦 Archivebox | Bullet archived: Mo removed an outdated memory |
| 📊 Bar chart | Activity read: Mo looked at your real activity numbers |

Tap a step to see the content Mo retrieved or wrote.

## Why this exists

If Mo says "you've been hitting your Move ring on weekdays but dropping off Saturdays," you can open the trace, see that Mo actually read your recent activity, and check the claim against reality.

It also helps when Mo gets something wrong: the trace usually shows where it went sideways, such as reading the wrong document.

## Keeping the trace expanded

**Keep thinking expanded** is on by default, so traces stay open. To have them collapse instead, open **Coach Mo settings** and turn **Keep thinking expanded** off. You can still tap any header to open it.

## What the trace doesn't show

The trace shows the steps Mo took, not all of its internal reasoning. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) for the current provider disclosure and available controls.
