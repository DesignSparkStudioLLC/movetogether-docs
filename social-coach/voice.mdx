Coach Mo has two different voice-related controls. On Apple Watch, paid Pro members can have a live spoken conversation. In the iPhone Coach screen, the microphone is **Dictate**: it turns speech into editable text for a normal Coach message. The iPhone control does not start a live voice call or read replies aloud.

## Dictate a Coach Mo message on iPhone

1. On iPhone, tap the red **+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**.
2. Tap the **Dictate** microphone in the message field.
3. Allow microphone and speech-recognition access if iOS asks.
4. Speak, then review and edit the transcribed text.
5. Tap **Send**. Coach Mo replies in the conversation as text.

Use this when you want to speak your question but still review it before sending.

## Start a live voice conversation on Apple Watch

1. Open the MoveTogether app on your Apple Watch.
2. Tap the microphone button (**Talk to Coach Mo**) at the top of the Watch home screen.
3. Tap **Chat with Coach Mo** and allow microphone access when watchOS asks.
4. Speak after Mo's greeting. Mo's latest response appears on screen while the call is open.
5. Tap the red phone button to end the conversation. You can then choose **Talk again** or **Close**.

Coach Mo voice requires a paid Pro subscription. A Pro free trial can't use voice, and on other plans the Watch shows **Pro feature**. If you've used Coach Mo's weekly allowance, the Watch shows **Coach Mo is resting** and "It resets in a few days." instead of starting a call.

## Choose how Mo sounds

Voice uses your selected coaching style. The first time you open Coach Mo directly on iPhone, the app can show **Pick a voice**; choosing an option saves that style. To change it later, open Coach Mo settings and choose **Personality** (styles other than Classic Mo need Pro). Your style applies to Coach responses generally, including Watch voice; the Watch does not have a separate voice picker.

Voice follows your **Roast intensity** setting (Clean, Classic, or Unhinged) for how much Mo swears, but it doesn't follow the roast mode switch. Mo roasts you by voice only when you ask, for example "roast me", and goes back to its normal tone on your next turn. See [Roast mode](/social-coach/roast-mode) for the intensity levels and age restrictions.

## If voice does not start

- Confirm that the Watch is signed in to the same MoveTogether account and that the account has paid Pro.
- Allow microphone access for MoveTogether on the Watch, then start a new call.
- Check that the Watch has a network connection and try again. If you see **Something went wrong**, tap **Try again**.
- If the Watch says **Coach Mo is resting**, wait a few days for the weekly reset rather than repeatedly reconnecting.
- If it remains unavailable, use typed Coach Mo on the iPhone or the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).

Coach Mo voice gives general fitness guidance. It is not medical advice or an emergency service.
