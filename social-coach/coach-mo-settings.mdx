On iPhone, tap the red **\+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**. Open the conversations drawer (tap the menu icon at the top left, or swipe right from the left edge), then tap **Settings** at the bottom of the drawer.

## Usage

The **Usage** card shows how much Coach Mo you've used, as a bar and a percentage (for example, "47% used"). Its label depends on your plan:

| Label | Plan | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lifetime usage** | Free and Membership | Your 5 lifetime messages. They don't reset or refill. |
| **Trial preview** | Pro free trial | A limited preview whose messages don't refill. |
| **Weekly limits** | Pro | No message count, but very heavy use can pause Mo until the reset. The card shows when that is, for example **Limit resets every** Sunday **at 8 PM ET**. |

On the Free plan, after your 5 messages are used you may be offered **Watch an ad for 1 message** (up to two a day). Membership doesn't include that option. Product how-to questions can be answered through Mo's separate help path without using a message; see [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo#product-help-coaching-and-support).

For the full plan comparison, see [Coach Mo access](/social-coach/coach-mo#coach-mo-access).

## Personality

Tap **Personality** to open the style picker. Pick from:

- **Classic Mo** (the default): Gen Z energy, zero filter
- **Drill Sergeant:** No excuses, soldier
- **Hype Man:** Your biggest fan
- **Big Sibling:** Caring, honest, always real
- **Sports Analyst:** The numbers don't lie

Switching to any style other than Classic Mo needs Pro; without Pro you'll see an upgrade screen. Your style affects every Coach Mo response, notification, and Weekly Report.

## Morning Brief

Tap **Morning Brief** to turn the daily brief on or off and choose its greeting time. The row shows your saved time, or **Off**. Morning Brief is a Pro feature. See [Use Morning Brief](/engagement/morning-brief).

## Plans & rest days

Tap **Plans & rest days** to review temporary plans Mo has proposed or you've confirmed. See [Manage Coach plans and rest days](/social-coach/coach-plans-rest-days).

## Roast mode

A switch: **on** makes Mo meaner, using your real numbers as ammunition. **Off** returns to normal coaching. You can also flip roast mode from the flame button in the Coach message bar.

The first time you turn on **Roast mode** here without having picked an intensity, Mo asks **How hard should Mo roast?** so you choose a level. This doesn't appear for accounts known to be under 18.

Roast mode works on top of any coaching style. For more on what roast mode does, see [Roast mode](/social-coach/roast-mode).

## Roast intensity

Below the roast switch, **Roast intensity** lists **Clean**, **Classic**, and **Unhinged**. Tap one to choose it. It controls how much Mo swears in roasts, Coach pushes, and voice. If you've never picked a level, Mo uses **Classic**. The roast switch still decides whether roast tone is on.

The same choice appears in **Settings → Notifications** as **How much Mo swears**, on every plan. Changing it in either place changes both.

For accounts known to be under 18, only **Clean** is available and the screen says **Available at 18+**.

## Keep thinking expanded

A switch that controls the **activity trace** at the top of Mo's replies. It's on by default.

- **On:** the trace stays open so you see Mo's steps right away
- **Off:** the trace collapses after a reply finishes; tap to expand

See [Reading Coach Mo's thinking](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace) for what the trace shows.

## Use Apple Intelligence

This switch appears only for Pro members on iOS 27 or later, on a device that can use Apple Intelligence. It's off by default. When it's on, normal replies run on Apple Intelligence. Roast mode, game plans, and rich cards still use the standard engine.

## Re-sync chat history

Reloads your conversation history from the server. Use it if conversations look out of date or didn't carry over to a new device.

## Delete all chats or files

Two destructive buttons sit at the bottom of settings.

**Delete all chats** wipes every conversation with Mo. Mo's memory of you (Files) is unaffected.

**Delete all files** wipes everything in Files: facts, goals, habits, preferences, and wins. Mo starts fresh. Conversations are unaffected.

Both ask for confirmation first and can't be undone. Use them when you want a clean slate.

<Note>
  Deleting your conversations or Files only affects Coach Mo. Your activity data, competitions, friends, achievements, and account are all unaffected. To delete your whole account, see [Your data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy).
</Note>
